### AGORA-143 — The NovelAI seam: `agents/fallback.py`, the backup's checks, its usage line and settings

**Description:**
This is the backup model's client and the checks on its answer, without any routing yet. Files: `agents/fallback.py`
(new), `agents/usage.py`, `agents/roster.py`, `agents/settings.py`, `agents/agent.py` (the token taken out of the
environment at start), `agents/kira.toml`, `server/kira.env.example`, `tests/test_fallback.py` (new),
`tests/test_usage.py`, `tests/test_roster.py`, `tests/test_compose.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`FallbackClient(token, model, sink)`** sends `POST https://text.novelai.net/oa/v1/chat/completions` over `aiohttp`.
  - **Headers:** `Authorization: Bearer`, and a `User-Agent` of its own (`matrix-agora/<version>`). Cloudflare refuses
    Python's default with 403 «error code: 1010»; this was verified on 2026-10-28.
  - **Body:**
    - `{"model": "glm-4-6", "messages": [system, user], "max_tokens", "temperature": 0.7, "top_p": 0.9, "stream": true}`;
    - the system message is the instruction, and the user message is the `Name: text` transcript;
    - `reply(transcript, instruction, max_tokens, kind)` gives `(text, finish)` or None.
  - **The stream:** the `data:` deltas are put together until `[DONE]`. A `<think>…</think>` block is cut. A
    `finish_reason` of `length` maps to `max_tokens`.
  - **Failures:** a 60 s total timeout, any error, a non-200 status or an empty answer gives None. The log names the
    type or the status only, never the token or a text. `__repr__` never shows the token.
- **The backup's checks** (`backup_check(text) -> reason | None`, pure) run on the backup's answer before anything
  else reads it. The answer is dropped when:
  - it has ы, э, ъ or ё (`russian`);
  - Latin letters outnumber Cyrillic ones in its words, after `split_tags` takes out the audio tags (`latin`);
  - it has a letter of another script: Han, Arabic, Hangul, Hebrew or kana (`script`);
  - one symbol repeats more than 10 times in a row (`run`);
  - she calls herself a model, an AI or a bot (`self`). This is v5.13's `refusal_marker`, plus «я бот» opening a
    sentence that is no question.
- **Usage:**
  - `fallback_usage_line(ts, agent, kind, model, ok)` writes `engine: "novelai"` and `billing: "subscription"`, so it
    is never priced. The stream reports no counts, so the tokens are null.
  - The usage line keeps one shape, with `blocked` null.
- **The capability `fallback`:**
  - persona only, and it needs `private` and `recall`;
  - on in `agents/kira.toml`;
  - a member without it is unchanged.
- **Settings** (`cap:fallback`, group «Резерв»):
  - `FALLBACK_MODEL` (text, empty means off);
  - `FALLBACK_WINDOW_S` (300), `FALLBACK_DAY_MAX` (10), `FALLBACK_CONTEXT` (32000), `FALLBACK_HISTORY_N` (60).
- **The secret:**
  - `NOVELAI_API_TOKEN` is taken out of her environment at start, as `GUARDIAN_API_KEY` is;
  - `server/kira.env.example` names it and `FALLBACK_MODEL`;
  - no other service reads `kira.env`.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md:**
  - §Token accounting: the engine `novelai`;
  - §Configuration: the settings and the capability;
  - §Security: what reaches NovelAI, and the token;
  - §Contracts: `kira.env`.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
A tested client that turns her prompt into GLM-4.6's answer or None, and checks that drop a broken or out-of-character
answer.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The request:
  - carries the URL, the Bearer, its own `User-Agent`, the model, the messages, `max_tokens`, the sampling and
    `stream`;
  - streamed deltas are put together, and a `<think>` block is cut;
  - a hanging stream ends at the timeout;
  - an error or a non-200 gives None, with no token or text in a log.
- [ ] The checks drop each failure kind. They keep an answer with audio tags whose words are Cyrillic, and her
  in-character «Ти думаєш, я бот? 😄».
- [ ] The backup's usage line is `novelai` and `subscription`, with no cost in the report or the panel.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_fallback.py`, with an in-process fake server or a patched session and no network;
  - the existing pins extended: usage, roster, settings, `kira.env.example`.
- [ ] **Contract:** `kira.env.example`'s keys (`tests/test_compose.py`) and the usage line's engine
  (`tests/test_usage.py`), with ARCHITECTURE.md updated.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests · compose

### AGORA-144 — The mark `waiting`: Gemini's readers, the facts, her history's closed lines

**Description:**

backup reads everything. Files: `agents/archive.py`, `agents/facts.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `panel/memory.py`

`specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The archive.**
  - **The marks:** `MARKS` gains `waiting`, and `GEMINI_CLOSED` becomes `("censored", "waiting")`.
  - **v5.13 review #3:** at open, every `censored` line, which rests on Gemini's verdict alone, becomes `waiting`.
    v5.15's judge rates it. This is idempotent.
  - **`Archive.mark(keys, mark)`** sets a mark, and `waiting` and `censored` drop the vector. `censor` stays, as
    `mark(keys, "censored")`.
  - **Readers:** `search_words`, `lines`, `around` and `latest` take `gemini: bool = True`. False, for the backup,
    returns every mark. `vectors` and `unembedded` stay Gemini-only.
  - **`for_model(model)`** (pure) gives the marks a model may read: a Gemini model no closed one, any other every one.
- **The facts.**
  - `Fact` gains `mark` (`gemini` by default, from a `mark:` header line). `add_fact(…, mark=…)` writes it, and only
    when it is not `gemini`, so older files are unchanged.
  - `facts_section(…, gemini=True)` leaves a closed fact out.
  - `sync_facts` carries each fact's mark into the archive.
  - The facts' extraction (Gemini) is never shown a closed fact.
- **Her history:**
  - `private_history` stays `(speaker, text)`;
  - `self._closed` holds the `(speaker, text)` pairs closed to Gemini;
  - `gemini_history()` filters them out, conservatively: a same text open elsewhere is left out too.
  - At start, the history is read with `gemini=False`, and `_closed` is filled from the marks.
  - v5.13's `censor_unanswered` becomes `mark_unanswered(stage, lines, mark)`:
    - the lines stay in her history, so the backup reads them;
    - they join `_closed`, and leave the session;
    - they are marked in the archive.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md:**
  - §Memory: `waiting`, the readers' `gemini` and the closed lines;
  - §Contracts: the archive's marks and the fact file's `mark:` header.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**

wait too.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Each reader honours `gemini`, while `vectors` and `unembedded` never return a closed line.

### AGORA-146 — Her private answer through the route: the backup's prompt and budget, the window

**Description:**
`private_reply` (the kinds `private` and `private_nudge`) answers by the route when `FALLBACK_MODEL` is set and she
has `fallback`. Agora, the background calls and every other agent are unchanged. Files: `agents/agent.py`,
`agents/logic.py` (the budget, pure), `tests/test_private_route.py` (new), `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Gemini's state:**
  - the prompt is built from `gemini_history()`, the recall with `gemini=True` and `facts_section(gemini=True)`;
  - on a block or a refusal (`LAST_BLOCK`), the backup answers with its own prompt;
  - `after(block)` opens the window;
  - the snapshot's owner lines and her answer are marked `waiting` (`mark_unanswered`).
- **The window:**
  - only the backup answers;
  - each owner's line and her answer are marked `waiting`;
  - her answer joins her history and `_closed`, and stays out of the session.
- **The window's end:**
  - Gemini, with the closed lines left out, and the backup start together (`asyncio.gather`);
  - Gemini's answer wins, and `after(gemini)` closes the window;
  - if Gemini blocks or refuses, the backup's ready answer goes out, its lines are marked `waiting`, and a new window
    opens;
  - a line she writes first goes the same way;
  - the log names the route and how many closed lines Gemini left out, never a text.
- **The pins:**
  - pinned `backup` is the window, with no end;
  - pinned `gemini` is Gemini's state, where a block still goes to the backup but no window opens.
- **The backup's prompt:**
  - `FALLBACK_HISTORY_N` lines of every mark;
  - the facts with `gemini=False`;
  - **the recall:** words only (`search_words(gemini=False)`), with no meaning search. The query is the owner's line,
    and the backup's talk never goes to Gemini's embeddings.
  - the rest as in her private prompt.
- **The budget** (`fit_budget`, pure):
  - the request is counted at 2.0 characters a token (Opus measured 36,864);
  - over `FALLBACK_CONTEXT`, it leaves out, in this order: the month digests, the week digests, «Agora today», and
    the oldest history lines (never fewer than 10);
  - the canon, the life section, the facts, the recall, «Сьогодні» and the mood are never cut;
  - `memories_section(now, layers=…)` builds the memories without a given layer.
- **The backup's answer:**
  - `backup_check` comes first;
  - then the usual post-processing: the «запам'ятай» marker (a fact kept from it waits, `mark="waiting"`), the trim,
    `PASS`, the audio tags, the voice;
  - a dropped answer is silence, logged by reason.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md** §The message flow: the private answer's route.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-143, AGORA-144, AGORA-145

**Expected result:**
A block or a refusal on her private answer never leaves the owner without an answer, and nothing said in the backup's
talk reaches Gemini.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Gemini's state:** nothing extra is called, and the lines stay `gemini`.
- [ ] **A block:** the backup answers, the window opens, and the lines are `waiting`.
- [ ] **The window:** the backup alone. Nothing of it is in a Gemini call: not the answer's history, the recall, the
  facts, the summary, the reflection or an embedding.
- [ ] **The window's end:** Gemini wins, or the backup's ready answer and a new window.
- [ ] **The rest:** the pins, a line she writes first, and a dropped backup answer is silence.
- [ ] **The budget:** the order of the cuts, never fewer than 10 history lines, and the kept sections never cut.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_private_route.py`, with fake Gemini and fake backup clients and an injected clock:
  - the order of the calls;
  - the marks;
  - Agora and the other agents unchanged;
  - no text in a log.
