# v7.6 — Issues

Issues for phase **v7.6 — Her memory as Lumi's: a short live window, short summaries, recall by her own tool**
(version **v7 — Claude for everyone**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v7.6 and the
contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase
(AGORA-259 → **AGORA-260…264**).

**What the phase delivers:** a second shape for a companion's memory in her private prompt, `MEMORY_MODE = lumi`,
switched per companion in the panel beside today's `layered`. In `lumi` mode:
- **the live window:** her history word for word is a short window that moves in batches;
- **the running digest:** the older talk of her day is folded into one digest, kept in the tail;
- **the gists:** the talk summaries are one-line gists by the day and the week;
- **her own tool:** she looks up the rest herself through `recall` and a look by date.

It builds on v7.5's prompt split and Gemini cache. The panel for Claude stays in v7.7.

## v7.6 — Her memory as Lumi's

### AGORA-260 — The switch, the live window in batches and her day's running digest

**Description:**
Adds the `lumi` mode's base: the live switch, a short history window, and a running digest of her day's older talk,
kept in the tail. It also closes v7.5 code review #9, the start-only cache.
Files: `agents/settings.py`, `agents/agent.py` (`_gemini_lines`, `prompt_layers`), a new pure module
`agents/lumi.py`, `agents/logic.py` (the digest's place in the tail), `agents/gemini_cache.py`, `agents/usage.py`
(`PART_NAMES`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The switch:** `MEMORY_MODE` (`layered` | `lumi`, default `layered`). It is a companion's own setting (`cap:private`)
  and a live switch (`LIVE`): every answer reads it, so a change in the panel works at once with no restart.
- **The live window (pure, `lumi_window_start(n_lines, n, batch)`):**
  - her Gemini history is the last `LIVE_WINDOW_N` (40) to `LIVE_WINDOW_N + LIVE_WINDOW_BATCH` (60) open lines;
  - its start moves by a whole batch (20 lines) at a time, so between two moves a new line only adds to its end;
  - his lines since her last one are always in it, as today;
  - in `lumi` mode it replaces `PRIVATE_HISTORY_HOURS` / `_STEP_H`; in `layered` mode nothing changes.
- **Her day's running digest (`state/<name>.digest.json`, written atomically):**
  - it holds her local day, the time of the last line folded, and the digest text;
  - the open lines of her day that are older than the window and not yet folded are folded into it a batch at a time
    by the digest model (`TALK_DIGEST_MODEL`, default the background `GEMINI_MODEL`; Flash-Lite with thinking off is
    set in the panel), usage kind `digest`;
  - the folding runs in the background, like the talk summaries (the idle watcher), so no answer waits for it; at most
    a few batches per tick;
  - it is checked as a talk summary is: the `talk_check` probe, and written again, gentler, when it blocks her prompt
    (`TALK_GENTLE`);
  - never a `blocked` line, never a softened span's lines;
  - at her midnight, by her own clock (v5.27), it starts empty, and the finished day's digest is kept for AGORA-261.
- **In the tail:** a `digest` layer «Раніше сьогодні ви говорили: …», right after «Сьогодні». It is never in the
  start, so it never breaks the cache. In `lumi` mode only, and only in her private prompt to Gemini.
- **The cache over the window (v7.5 code review #9):**
  - the planner's use branch takes a start-only cache (`lines == 0`) only while `start_only_until` holds;
  - after that, the next answer makes a full cache (`grew`);
  - a test pins it, replacing `tests/test_gemini_cache.py`'s current assertion.
- **The settings:** `LIVE_WINDOW_N`, `LIVE_WINDOW_BATCH`, `TALK_DIGEST_MODEL` and `DIGEST_WORDS` (120), each a
  companion's own setting.
- **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE §Memory (the `lumi` mode, the window, the digest), §Prompt assembly (the `digest` layer in the
  tail), §State files (`<name>.digest.json`), the settings table.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
With `MEMORY_MODE=lumi` her Gemini history is 40–60 lines that move in batches, and the rest of her day comes to her
as one running digest in the tail; with `layered` nothing changes.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `lumi_window_start` is pure. The window is N to N+batch lines, its start moves only by whole batches, and his
  unanswered lines are always in it.
- [ ] The digest folds today's lines older than the window in batches, never a `blocked` or softened line, is checked
  like a talk summary, and starts empty at her midnight. The finished day is kept.
- [ ] In `lumi` mode the `digest` layer is in the tail after «Сьогодні», and the start is unchanged by it. In
  `layered` mode the prompt is byte-for-byte today's.
- [ ] `MEMORY_MODE` is live: the next answer after a change uses the new mode.
- [ ] A start-only cache holds only for `START_ONLY_S`; then a full cache is made (v7.5 #9).
- [ ] **Tests:** the window's steps and floor; the digest folded in batches over made-up lines, a `blocked` line left
  out, a block written gentler, the reset at her midnight (a southern zone too); the layer's place in the tail; the
  switch read per answer; the planner's #9 (Gemini mocked, clock injected).
- [ ] **Contract:** the prompt order (the `digest` layer, `tests/test_prompt_slow.py`), the state file
  `<name>.digest.json`, the settings (`tests/test_settings.py`); ARCHITECTURE updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-261 — Short summaries: one-line gists by the day and the week, her day memories as one line

**Description:**
In `lumi` mode her talk summaries reach the prompt as one line a day (the last 7 days) and one line a week (the weeks
before), and her day memories as one line each. The full summaries stay in the archive.
Files: `agents/talk.py`, `agents/agent.py` (the talk tick, `talk_now`, `memories_section`), `agents/archive.py` (the
new levels), `agents/chronicle.py` or `agents/memory.py` (the memories' gists).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The day's gist (`talk` row, level `gist_day`, written once and never rewritten):**
  - from the finished day's running digest (AGORA-260) by one call of the digest model: one line of at most
    `GIST_WORDS` (30) words, written as a human's own memory (`HUMAN_MEMORY`);
  - for a day with no digest (before the switch, or a day the digest missed), from that day's `day` / `hour` rows;
  - checked like a talk summary (the `talk_check` probe, `TALK_GENTLE`).
- **The week's gist (level `gist_week`):** once a week (Monday to Sunday, local) is over, one line from its day gists
  (or its `day` rows), the same way.
- **Selection (pure, `gists_due` / `gist_section`):**
  - the gists due, oldest first;
  - the section shows a line for each of the last `GIST_DAYS` (7) days before today, then a line for each of the
    `GIST_WEEKS` (4) weeks before those, labelled as she remembers them (`_label`);
  - no hourly text;
  - in `lumi` mode this replaces `talk_section`, and `TALK_PROMPT_WORDS` does not apply.
- **The talk tick:**
  - in `lumi` mode it writes the gists due;
  - `hour` / `day` rows keep being written in both modes, so `layered` mode works the moment it is switched back and
    the archive stays whole.
- **Her day memories as one line:**
  - in `lumi` mode `memories_section` shows, for each layer it would show, a one-line gist of that memory;
  - the gist is written once by the digest model and kept beside the memory file (`<key>.gist.md`); it is never
    rewritten, and the memory itself is untouched;
  - a memory without a gist yet shows its first sentence until the gist is written.
- **The invariants:**
  - nothing of her future;
  - a gist never calls her a model or a bot (`BANNED_RE`, written again once, then empty);
  - never a `blocked` line's text.
- **The settings:** `GIST_DAYS`, `GIST_WEEKS` and `GIST_WORDS`, each a companion's own setting (`cap:talk`).
- **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE §Memory (the gists, their levels and files), §State files (`talk` levels, `<key>.gist.md`),
  the settings table.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-260

**Expected result:**
In `lumi` mode her talk before today is a few lines — one a day for a week, one a week before that — and her day
memories one line each, while the archive keeps every full summary.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `gist_day` is written once from the day's digest, or from its `day`/`hour` rows when there is none, and
  `gist_week` once a week is over. Neither is ever rewritten.
- [ ] `gist_section` shows the last 7 days by the day and then 4 weeks by the week, with no hourly text, and returns
  None when there is nothing.
- [ ] A day memory shows its gist in `lumi` mode, or its first sentence until the gist exists. In `layered` mode it is
  as today.
- [ ] The gists are checked like the talk summaries and never hold a closed line's text.
- [ ] **Tests:** the gists due and the section over made-up rows and an injected clock (a DST day, a week's end, the
  bands); written from the digest and from the rows; a block written gentler; the memory gists; `layered` unchanged
  (Gemini mocked).
- [ ] **Contract:** the `talk` levels and the gist files (ARCHITECTURE §State files), the settings
  (`tests/test_settings.py`); ARCHITECTURE updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-262 — Recall by her own tool on Gemini: `recall` and a look by date, in the cache

**Description:**
In `lumi` mode her answers on Gemini can call two tools: `recall(query)`, which searches her archive by meaning and
words, and a look by date, which gives her talk of a given day. The tool declarations live in the explicit cache with
the start. The automatic recall block shrinks.
Files: `agents/llm.py` (`GeminiClient.reply`, the cache's create config), `agents/gemini_cache.py` (the fingerprint),
a new `agents/memory_tools.py` (the declarations, the pure result rendering), `agents/agent.py` (the tools' handlers
over `recall_section` and the archive), `agents/usage.py` (`tool_calls`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The declarations:**
  - `recall(query: string)`: what she remembers about something, which runs her archive by meaning and words
    (`recall_section`) and returns up to `RECALL_TOOL_N` (5) lines with their neighbours;
  - `day(date: string, YYYY-MM-DD)`: what they talked about that day, which returns that day's gist and `day`/`hour`
    summaries, or for today the digest;
  - the names, descriptions and results are human words in Ukrainian («згадати», «що було того дня») and never say
    model, bot, tool or AI.
- **The loop in `GeminiClient.reply`, for answer kinds when `self.tools` is set:**
  - a `function_call` in the answer is run by the agent's handler;
  - the model's content is sent back as returned, so thinking's signatures are kept, followed by the
    `function_response`;
  - at most `RECALL_TOOL_ROUNDS` (3) rounds;
  - a further call is answered with «більше не згадується», and the round after it is the last;
  - a handler that fails returns an empty result, never an exception, and never silences the answer.
- **The cache:**
  - with `cached_content` the request may carry no tools, so the declarations go into
    `CreateCachedContentConfig(tools=…)`;
  - the fingerprint includes the tool set, so a `lumi` cache and a `layered` cache never match;
  - a plain call (no cache) sends the tools in its own config.
- **The results:**
  - never a `blocked` line or fact, never a softened span's line (`_soft_hidden`), never her future (no dated life
    events past today);
  - stripped of kept notes (`strip_notes`);
  - rendered as the recall block is, labelled as she remembers it.
- **The automatic recall:** in `lumi` mode `recall_section` gives `LUMI_RECALL_N` (4) lines, instead of `RECALL_N`.
- **On the other answer models** (v7.4's switch: Sol, Sonnet, Opus, Codex) the tools are not offered, and the memory
  stays inline: the recall block at `RECALL_N`, the gists and the digest as in `lumi` mode.
- **The usage line:**
  - an answer's line carries `tool_calls` (how many);
  - each round is its own call line, with kind `private` and the same `latency_ms` rules, so the cost and the time
    count every round.
- **The settings:** `RECALL_TOOL_N`, `RECALL_TOOL_ROUNDS` and `LUMI_RECALL_N`, each a companion's own setting.
- **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE §Memory (her tool), §Prompt assembly (the cache holds the tools), §State files (the usage line's
  `tool_calls`).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-260

**Expected result:**
In `lumi` mode on Gemini she looks up what is not in her prompt herself, at most three times an answer, every round on
the cached start, and nothing closed or future ever comes back to her.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A function call round runs the handler and sends back the model's content and the response. The answer's text
  comes after it.
- [ ] The rounds are capped. A failing handler gives an empty result, and the answer is still given.
- [ ] With the cache, the tools are in the cache's config and not in the request. The fingerprint differs by the tool
  set.
- [ ] The results never hold a `blocked` line, a closed fact, a softened line or her future, and the declarations never
  name a model or a tool.
- [ ] Off Gemini no tools are offered, and the recall block stays at `RECALL_N`.
- [ ] **Tests:** the SDK faked with a `function_call` part (one round, three rounds and the cap, a failing handler);
  the cache's create config with the tools, and the plain config; the handlers over a made-up archive (blocked and
  softened lines left out, a future date empty); the words of the declarations; `tool_calls` on the usage line.
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage line's `tool_calls` (`tests/test_usage.py`), the settings (`tests/test_settings.py`);
  ARCHITECTURE updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

The panel shows the switch and her new memory.
Files: `agents/usage.py` (the line's `memory_mode`, the analytics), `agents/agent.py` (the sinks), `panel/memory.py`,

  - nothing `blocked` is shown, as before.
- **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE §The panel and §Token accounting.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-261, AGORA-262

**Expected result:**
The owner sees in Analytics what each mode costs and how fast it answers for each companion, and switches the mode in
her settings.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Every answer line of a companion carries `memory_mode`. Old lines read as `layered`.
- [ ] «By memory» groups by agent and mode with the measures above. The background calls of `lumi` (`digest`, the

- [ ] The settings tab offers `MEMORY_MODE`. The Talk tab shows the digest and the gists, never a `blocked` text.
- [ ] **Tests:** the analytics over made-up lines in both modes (including old lines without the field); the panel's
