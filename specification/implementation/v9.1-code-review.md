### 1. 🟠 A recalled fact is dated by the archive's last sync — `agents/archive.py` `render_recalled`, `sync_facts`

**Where the date comes from:**
- `render_recalled(…, stamped=True)` labels a fact row «(те, що ти знаєш про Ich, з DD.MM)» from the row's `ts_ms`.
- `Archive.sync_facts(seen, ts_ms)` replaces every fact row each time the fact set changes, and at every start (the
  agent's `_facts_seen` begins empty).
- It writes them all with `self.clock()`.

**Failure scenario:**
1. Ich told Кіра about his sister on 2026-10-18.
2. The agent restarts on 2026-11-18 (any deploy).
3. Her recall of that fact now says «з 18.11».

Every recalled fact claims the day of the last deploy or fact edit. That is exactly the wrong-time memory v9.1 exists
to remove. Her private prompt's facts block itself is right, because it reads `Fact.added`.

**Proposed fix:**
- `render_recalled` takes the facts' days: `fact_days`, a map from the archive key `fact:<id>` to `Fact.added` as a
  date.
- The agent passes them from `load_facts`, and a fact row shows «з DD.MM» only from that map.
- With no known day, the row shows none.
- The archive's rows and their `ts_ms` are unchanged (no contract change).

### 2. 🟠 The window's time lines break Gemini's history cache in `layered` mode — `agents/agent.py` `gemini_history_shown`

**How the cache works** (v7.5):
- In `layered` mode the cache holds the start and her history but the newest two lines.
- It is used only while the history still begins with its lines (`CachePlanner.plan`, its fingerprint over the cached
  lines).

**What breaks it:** with `stamps` the window's first entry is a time line whose text moves with the clock: «— 40 хвилин
тому (13:40): —» is a new text every minute, and «— 6 годин тому (…): —» every hour.

**Failure scenario:**
1. Кіра's `MEMORY_MODE` is switched back to `layered` (a live panel switch).
2. Every answer finds the history changed.
3. The cache is made again (`gemini cache: made (history)`), its storage is paid each time, and it is never read.

Both companions are in `lumi` today, where the cache holds the start alone, so nothing is lost yet.

**Proposed fix:**
- In `layered` mode, the window's time lines take the day-level label with its hour («— сьогодні (18.11, 08:15): —»),
  which is stable all day.
- `lumi` keeps the hour-level age the owner asked for.
- `history_times` gains `fine` for this.

### 3. 🟡 A malformed thought time raises in prompt building — `agents/thoughts.py` `thought_rows`

**The defect:** with `now` given, `now.replace(hour=int(t.time[:2]), minute=int(t.time[3:5]))` raises `ValueError` for
a time like «25:10».
- `_BLOCK_RE` accepts any `\d{2}:\d{2}`.
- The day file is hers and is written by code, so it is unlikely — but it is state, and a raise there leaves her
  whole answer silent.

**Proposed fix:** an out-of-range time falls back to the part of the day, as before.

  `history_ts`, the ranking and the turns read nothing new.

  the log says `answer: dates=N (place)`.
- **The digest file is never rewritten.** Its marks get their age at render time only (`aged_marks`).
- **`TIME_LINE_RE`** keeps a stamped time line out of her reply.

## Fixes applied

1. **#1 — a recalled fact's day** (a0e6c50; ea69161 makes the test's date tz-aware for lint).
   - `render_recalled(…, fact_days)` takes the facts' days by archive key (`fact:<id>` → `Fact.added`), and the
     agent passes them (`_fact_days`, from `load_facts`).
   - A fact row shows «з DD.MM» only from that map, never from the row's `ts_ms`, which is the last sync's time.
   - **Test:** `test_a_recalled_fact_is_dated_by_the_day_she_learned_it_never_by_the_archives_sync` — a fact synced
     on 18.11 but learned on 18.10 reads «з 18.10», and with no known day there is no date.
2. **#2 — the window's time lines in `layered`** (e74b021).
   - `history_times(…, fine)`: `gemini_history_shown` passes `fine` only in `lumi`.
   - In `layered` the label is «— сьогодні (18.11, 08:15): —», the same all day.
   - **Test:** `test_in_layered_mode_her_windows_time_lines_stay_the_same_all_day_for_the_cache` — the labels are
     equal at 09:00 and 23:59, `lumi` stays hour-level, and the agent's `layered` window is unchanged an hour on.
3. **#3 — an out-of-range thought time** (02f1b73).
   - `thought_rows` falls back to the part of the day when the hour or the minute is out of range.
   - **Test:** `test_an_out_of_range_thought_time_never_breaks_her_prompt`.
