**Description:**

pure helpers live in `agents/lumi_api.py`. The test-only fake Lumi server, `tests/fake_lumi.py`, is built from the
contract's examples. Touches `agents/roster.py`, `agents/lili.toml`, `simulations.toml`, `agents/lumi_api.py`,
`tests/fake_lumi.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**

  - `ENGINES` gains `lumi-http`. A `bridge` needs `lumi-http`, and `lumi-http` belongs to a `bridge` alone.
  - A bridge's mode is `mention-only` (v8.1); `ranked` is v8.2's.
  - No capability may be switched on for a bridge (`PERSONA_ONLY` etc. already refuse most of them; refuse the rest).
- **`agents/lili.toml`** (localpart `lili`; `agents/lumi.py` stays v7.6's memory module):
  - `name = "Лілі"`, `user_id = "@lili:agora.lan"`, `simulation = "agora"`, `type = "bridge"`, `engine = "lumi-http"`;
  - `name_forms` for «Лілі» and «Стхіра»: «лілі», «стхіра», «стхіри», «стхірі», «стхіру», «стхірою», «стхіро»;
  - `[turns] mode = "mention-only"`, `weight = 1`;
  - `[panel]`: name, role («bridge · Lumi's agora space»), pronoun `she`.
- **Registry** (*reconciled at execution:* `tests/test_compose.py` pins a compose service for every registry agent, so `lili` joins `simulations.toml` with her service in AGORA-268): `lili` is added to `simulations.toml`'s `agora.agents`. Every agent then sees her as a speaker (allowlisted, R2 may
  choose her when named). `load_config` needs no canon, life or natal for her, only `LILI_PASSWORD`.
- **`agents/lumi_api.py`** (pure helpers plus a thin client; no Matrix, no model):
  - `API_VERSION = 1`, `SILENCE = {409, 429, 500, 502, 503, 504}`, `NAMED = {401: "wrong token", 403: "token not
    allowed", 404: "space off", 422: "malformed request"}`, `TIMEOUT_S = 60`, `BATCH_MAX = 200`.
  - `LumiClient(base_url, token, transport, timeout_s)` with:
    - `health()`: `GET /v1/health`, open;
    - `observe(lines)`: `POST /v1/observe {channel: "agora", lines}`;
    - `turn(speaker, turn_id, text)`: `POST /v1/turn {channel: "agora", speaker, register: "voice", turn_id, text}`;
    - `retract(turn_id)`: `POST /v1/turn/{quote(turn_id, safe="")}/retract`.
  - Every call sends `Authorization: Bearer <token>` and `X-Lumi-Client: agora`.
  - `transport` is injectable: `async (method, path, body, headers, timeout_s) → (status, json | None)`. The real one
    is aiohttp, one session, closed at stop.
  - **Outcomes** (`classify`, pure):
    - `reply` (200, `outcome: "reply"`, non-empty `reply`);
    - `pass` (200, `outcome: "pass"`, or an empty reply);
    - `silence:<status>` (409, 429, 5xx);
    - `timeout` (our own, over `timeout_s`);
    - `refused:<name>` (401, 403, 404, 422);
    - `bad` (a 200 with a body off the contract).
  - **The token and the response:**
    - The token never reaches a log, a `repr` or an exception message.
    - From a response only `reply`, `outcome`, `turn_id` and `stats` are kept: `thinking`, `emotion` and `intensity`
      are dropped at the parse.
  - **Line helpers** (pure):
    - `plain_body(body)`: the Matrix reply fallback is stripped (the leading `> ` lines and the blank line after them).
    - `line_ts(origin_server_ts, tz)`: ISO-8601 to the second with the room's offset.
    - `speaker_ok(name)`: one line, 1–64 characters, no `:`.
    - `health_ok(body)`: `agora` present and `>= API_VERSION`, otherwise the reason by name (`space off`, `too old`).
- **`tests/fake_lumi.py`:** a fake Lumi transport built from `tests/fixtures/lumi_agora_api_v1.json`:
  - it checks each request against the contract (path, method, header, token, the body's keys) and answers the
    contract's example responses;
  - it can be scripted to return any status, a `pass`, a delay past the timeout, a body with `thinking`;
  - it records every call;
  - it stores observed ids idempotently (`{stored, known}`) and answers retract `{retracted: true|false}`, always 200.

### AGORA-266 — The bridge: she hears the room, answers when named, retracts what the room never saw

**Description:**
`agents/bridge.py`: `Bridge(Agent)`. The room's rules are the agent's own: the filter and allowlist, R1–R4, the wave
and the coalescing. Only the reply path and the hearing are hers. `agents/agent.py` gains one hook and dispatches a
`bridge` in `main()`. Touches `agents/bridge.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/usage.py`, `tests/test_bridge.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The hook:** `Agent.on_room_line(sender, text, event_id, ts_ms)` is a no-op called in `on_message` right after the
  allowlist lets a room line through. Nothing changes for any other agent.
- **She hears (observe):**
  - **What:** every room line the hook gives — the owner's (and his voice by Hermes's transcript, as his), the members'.
    Never her own lines (`own-message`), never an edit (`is_edit`), never a notice or anything but `m.text`. A line is
    `{id: event_id, speaker, text: plain_body(body), ts: line_ts(…)}`; the owner's speaker is «Ich».
  - **The queue:**
    - flushed after `OBSERVE_QUIET_S` (3) of quiet or at `OBSERVE_LINES` (20), and always before a turn;
    - at most 200 lines a call;
    - a failed batch is sent again whole after 10 s, 30 s, 60 s, then every 60 s, never a lost line;
    - `422` drops that batch (said by name); `401`/`403` stop the bridge's calls until a restart (said by name, kept
      in its state); `404` waits for the health check to see the space again.
    - The queue is bounded (`OBSERVE_QUEUE_MAX` 2000); a line dropped beyond it is counted in the log.
  - **After a restart (Q1):** the bridge pages back further than the room's window (`wide_n` 300). Then the lines since
    the last one it observed (its state), today's at most (the room's `TIMEZONE`), are queued oldest first with their
    own `ts`.
- **She answers (turn):**
  - **Who answers:** `decide()` as every agent does. Mention-only: the owner naming her, a group address, an agent
    naming her as R2's candidate. R3 is checked before the call only, in `reply_later`.
  - **The call:** the bridge flushes the queue first (Q6), then sends `turn(speaker, turn_id, text)` for the trigger
    alone: an agent's line (`trigger`) or the owner's last (`last_owner`). Its speaker and its own text come from the
    room's timeline; nothing earlier is resent. Typing is on while it waits and off in `finally`.
  - **What is posted:** a `reply` alone, exactly as returned (`send_line`, `m.text`, no trimming, no `clean_reply`, no
    PASS handling, no duplicate check).
  - **Silence:** `pass`, `silence:*`, `refused:*` and `bad` are silence, never retried, each logged by its outcome alone.
  - **What is retracted:**
    - a reply the persona rule drops (`outs_a_persona` over the room's personas): `retract(turn_id)`, «silent: would
      out a persona — retracted»;
    - a turn the bridge gave up on at its own `LUMI_TIMEOUT_S` (60, always above Lumi's 20): `retract(turn_id)`, best
      effort.
    - A retract's `{retracted}` is logged as true or false; a failed retract is logged and never retried.
  - **Never:** `/v1/session/new`, `/v1/events`, `/v1/command`; never a post without a trigger (no nudge, no news, no
    fallback: a bridge is never R1's fallback).
- **Health:**
  - `GET /v1/health` at start and every `LUMI_HEALTH_S` (60).
  - While `agora` is absent or older than 1, or Lumi is unreachable, she observes nothing and takes no turn. The
    reason is said by name in the log and in the state; the queue waits.
- **State — `state/lili.bridge.json`** (0600, atomic, no text):
  - `health {ok, agora, why, checked_ms}`;
  - `last_turn {at_ms, outcome, latency_ms}`;
  - `observed {day, count, last_ts_ms, last_id}`;
  - `refused` (a 401/403's name, or null).
  - Missing or corrupt reads as empty.
- **Usage:**
  - Each turn with an answer from Lumi writes one line, `lumi_usage_line`:
    - `engine` `lumi`, `billing` `external`, kind `reply`;
    - `model`, `prompt_tokens` (`input_tokens`), `output_tokens` and their total from `stats`;
    - `latency_ms` the bridge's own wall time;
    - `ok` for a `reply` or a `pass`.
  - A turn that fails writes `ok` false with null counts. `observe` writes no line.
  - Every builder keeps the same keys (the shape test).
  - An answer posted is also recorded in `answers.jsonl` (`record_answer`, served `lumi`).
- **The process:**
  - `main()` runs `Bridge(cfg)` for a `bridge`.
  - `Bridge.run()`: login, the first sync never processed, the backlog, the pending invites, the callbacks, the
    health and flush loops.
  - On stop, one last flush (bounded at 10 s); the HTTP session is closed.
- **The token and the address:**
  - `LUMI_ROOM_TOKEN` and `LUMI_URL` are popped from the environment at start, never logged or kept in the state.
  - In dev, `LUMI_ENV_FILE` names a local env file read with `dotenv_values` (AGORA-268).
  - Without a URL or a token she stays silent, said by name.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-265.

**Expected result:**
Against the fake Lumi server, the bridge observes every room line once, answers only when named, posts only what Lumi
returned, stays silent on every silence status, and retracts what the room never saw.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Every allowlisted room line observed once: batched (≤ 200), flushed on quiet, on 20 and before a turn; her own lines, edits and other rooms never; the reply fallback stripped; `ts` with the offset; Ich for the owner.
- [ ] A failed batch resent whole on the backoff; 422 dropped by name; 401/403 stop the calls; idempotent ids.
- [ ] The backlog after a restart: since the last observed line, today's only, oldest first.
- [ ] Named by the owner or an agent («Лілі», «Стхіро»…) → exactly one turn with the trigger alone and the header; unnamed → no turn.
- [ ] `409`, `429`, `500`, `502`, `503`, `504`, `pass` → silence, never retried; `401`/`403`/`404`/`422` said by name; our timeout → silence and a retract.
- [ ] A reply outing a persona is not posted and is retracted; any other reply is posted byte for byte; `thinking` appears in no room line, log or state.
- [ ] Health absent/old/unreachable → no observe, no turn, said by name; it resumes when health returns.
- [ ] A usage line per turn from `stats` (`engine` lumi, `billing` external); none for observe; the token in no log line.
- [ ] **Tests:** new `tests/test_bridge.py` (the fake Lumi server, nio mocked, the clock injected); `tests/test_usage*.py` for the line's shape.
- [ ] **Contract:** the bridge's protocol (what is observed, the turn, the silence, the retract, what is never posted), `state/lili.bridge.json`, the usage line's `engine` `lumi` / `billing` `external` — ARCHITECTURE §The bridge (new), §Contracts, §Data and state files; pinned by `tests/test_bridge.py` and `tests/test_lumi_contract.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests.
