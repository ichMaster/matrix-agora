## Findings

### 1. 🟠 The `ask` move names Лілі — `agents/moves.py:75`
**Scenario:**
- `persona_move` draws the `ask` addressee from every speaker but the asker, and that now includes the mention-only
  bridge.
- With the real roster and the default weights, Ада's `ask` chose Лілі in 445 of 4,978 asks, about 2% of her replies.
- Naming her makes her R2's only candidate. Each such ask costs one of Lumi's 20 room turns an hour.
- When she is silent (a `pass`, a 429, Lumi away), nobody answers and the chain stops.

ROADMAP v8.1 depends on exactly this filter: «v4.6 review #4 (the `ask` addressee filter: a mention-only bridge is not
asked by the personas)». v4.6's review deferred it to the bridge phase, and the v8.1 issues missed it.
**Fix:** draw the addressee only among the speakers whose mode is not `mention-only`, and never a bridge. Pin it in
`tests/test_moves.py`.

### 2. 🟠 An edit and the shared timeline — `agents/bridge.py` `on_message`
**Scenario:**
- `Agent.on_message` has no edit filter in Agora, so every other agent adds the owner's edit to its timeline and decides
  on it. The bridge drops edits before `Agent.on_message`, so they never reach its timeline.
- Then the owner writes O1, Бруно writes «Лілі, а ти що думаєш?» (B1), and the owner edits O1.
- For Ада, R3 on B1 is false («moved on»); for the bridge it is true, so she still answers B1. The wave counts differ
  too.

Turn-taking must be derived from shared inputs. Q4's premise, «as matrix-agora's agents do», holds only for Hermes and
the private chat.
**Fix:** the bridge passes an edit into `Agent.on_message`, so its timeline and history match every agent's. It neither
hears an edit (Q4: no observe) nor answers one: `decide` gives a silent decision for an edit. The other agents keep
their behaviour.

### 3. 🟠 A deploy before G4 crash-loops `lili` — `server/docker-compose.yml`
**Scenario:**
- `server/deploy.sh` runs `docker compose up -d` for every service.
- Before G4, `server/.env` has no `LILI_PASSWORD`, so `load_config` raises `ConfigError` and the process exits 1.
  `restart: unless-stopped` restarts it forever.
- With the password set but no `@lili` account, the login fails the same way. With an older image, `agents/lili.toml` is
  missing.
- The deploy's own check watches the homeserver alone, so nothing notices.
**Fix:**
- The service goes behind a compose profile, `profiles: ["lili"]`. A plain `up -d` leaves it alone until
  `COMPOSE_PROFILES=lili` is set in `server/.env` at G4. The panel's start names the service, which enables the profile.
- `COMPOSE_PROFILES` joins `server/.env.example`.

### 4. 🟡 What the room never saw is not always retracted — `agents/bridge.py` `reply`
**Scenario:** a retract follows only the persona drop and the bridge's own timeout. These cases miss it:
- the homeserver refuses the send (`send_line` returns `None`), and the answer is even recorded;
- a 200 off the contract (`bad`);
- a reset after Lumi answered (`unreachable`).

Each breaks Q2: what Lumi remembers is what the room saw.
**Fix:** retract when the send gives no event id or raises, and on `bad` and `unreachable`. A retract is idempotent, and
`false` when nothing was stored.

### 5. 🟡 The queue's backoff and the pre-turn wait — `agents/bridge.py` `on_room_line`, `flush`, `reply`
**Scenario:**
- From 20 lines, every new line spawns a flush. Those wait on `_flush_lock` past the `_retry_at` check, so each one
  calls Lumi anyway: 9 observe calls in a frozen clock where the backoff allows 1.
- The pre-turn flush waits for the lock without a bound. With 30 s observe timeouts, a turn can wait minutes with typing
  on.
**Fix:**
- Check `_retry_at` and `_lumi_closed()` again inside the lock.
- Spawn a flush at 20 lines only when the lock is free.
- Bound the pre-turn flush at `PRE_TURN_FLUSH_S` (5 s); the turn then goes anyway (Q6).

### 6. 🟡 A refused token still sends the turn — `agents/bridge.py` `reply`
**Scenario:** the pre-turn flush gets a 401 and logs «no more calls until a restart». The `/v1/turn` call goes out
anyway.
**Fix:** check `_lumi_closed()` again after the flush.

### 7. 🟡 `plain_body` strips a typed quote — `agents/lumi_api.py` `plain_body`
**Scenario:**
- The owner types «> Бути чи не бути\n\nось питання, Лілі». Lumi hears «ось питання, Лілі», his quote gone.
- A line that is only a quote becomes empty. If Lumi answers 422, the whole batch is dropped.
**Fix:** strip only Element's reply fallback, whose first line is `> <@user:server> …`. Any other `>` line stays as
typed.

### 8. 🟡 No address or token: the card says nothing — `agents/bridge.py` `check_health`
**Scenario:**
- Without `LUMI_URL` or `LUMI_ROOM_TOKEN`, `check_health` returns before it writes the state, so the card reads «not
  checked yet» for good. ROADMAP says the reason is «said by name in its log and on its card».
- Lines are still queued up to the 2,000 cap, with nowhere to go.
**Fix:** write `health {ok: false, why: "no LUMI_URL"}` at start, and queue nothing while there is no client.

### 9. 🟡 Analytics' wording and colours — `panel/static/app.js`
**Scenario:** the cost-by-agent bar with no cost is labelled «subscription», so Лілі's `external` bar reads
«subscription». Lumi has colour slot 8, the fallback that `openai`, `anthropic` and `codex` also get.
**Home:** backlog. These are cosmetic and touch every unpriced bar's wording.

## What's solid

- **The token.** `LUMI_URL` and `LUMI_ROOM_TOKEN` leave the environment before `Agent.__init__`.
  - `LumiClient` and `TurnResult` have safe `repr`s, and transport errors become a type name.
  - No log line, state field, usage line or panel field holds a text, her reply or the token.
  - `lili.env` is read by `lili` alone, and `lili.dev.env` is never synced.
- **The contract.** `classify`, `kept` and `health_ok` follow it.
  - Silence is never retried, and the named errors are named.
  - The retract is URL-encoded and read as true, false or unknown.
  - A turn carries the trigger alone, and the reply is posted byte for byte. Typing is reset in `finally`.
- **The turns.** No attribute clashes with `Agent`. A mention-only member is never R1's fallback, never ranked, and R2's
  candidate only when named. Hermes's transcripts reach her as the owner's lines. Her own lines and voiced captions are
  never heard.
- **The backlog.** No gap and no overlap with the sync; oldest first, today's only, from `last_ts_ms`.
- **Usage and the panel.** `external` is unpriced and never makes a total incomplete. `bridge_view` cannot cause a 500.
  `forget` is refused. Her settings are the room's shared turns, so `reserved()` and the wave read the same values.
