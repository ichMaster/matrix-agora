## v8.2 — Лілі under the full rules

### AGORA-270 — Лілі ranked: the roster, her TOML, the fallback turn, a ranked room's waves

**Description:**
A bridge may be `ranked`. Лілі's TOML becomes `ranked`, weight 1, and she joins R1 (`OWNER_REPLIERS`), R2 and the
fallback like any ranked member. The bridge answers a fallback turn for the owner's line it stands in for. Touches
`agents/roster.py`, `agents/lili.toml`, `agents/bridge.py`, the turn tests, and ARCHITECTURE.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The roster:** `BRIDGE_MODES = ("mention-only", "ranked")`. `ambient` and `none` stay refused for a bridge.
- **`agents/lili.toml`:** `[turns] mode = "ranked"`, `weight = 1`.
- **The bridge's fallback turn:**
  - `Bridge.reply(fallback_for=…)` answers that owner line, not a newer `last_owner`.
  - As every reply, it is checked only before the call: `fallback_later` already checks `fallback_due` and R3. After
    the call only the persona rule drops a reply, which is retracted.
- **A pass:**
  - Lumi's `pass` is a PASS in the room: silence, never retried.
  - When R1 chose her and she passes, is away, busy or slow, the room's own fallback answers the owner after
    `FALLBACK_S`. The best-ranked member R1 did not choose is unchanged, and the bridge is never awaited.
- **The `ask` move:** it now may name her, as any ranked member. The filter keeps skipping `mention-only` members
  (code review v8.1 #1) and the herald.
- **The pinned turns:** R1/R2 draws, shares of first places, the simulated waves and the cat's nudge that the
  real roster pins are updated for an eleventh ranked member, each with a comment.
- **A simulated wave with Лілі ranked** runs `decide_reply` for every member over random owner and agent lines. It never
  exceeds `MAX_BOT_TURNS` beyond the answers to the owner. Her pass is modelled as silence, and the fallback answers.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §The bridge: ranked, the fallback turn and a pass;
  - §Turn-taking: the bridge as a ranked member;
  - §Contracts: the TOML schema's bridge modes.

**Dependencies:** None.

**Expected result:**
Лілі takes turns like Ада and Бруно; when she stays silent, the room never stalls.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A bridge loads `ranked` or `mention-only`, and nothing else. Лілі's TOML is `ranked`, weight 1.
- [ ] R1 can choose her for an unnamed owner line, and R2 for an agent's line. The fallback can be her, and her fallback turn answers the line it stands in for.
- [ ] Her `pass` on a chosen turn is silence, and the room's fallback then answers the owner.
- [ ] A simulated wave with her ranked never exceeds `MAX_BOT_TURNS` beyond the answers to the owner.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_roster.py`, `tests/test_lumi_api.py` (the bridge modes);
  - `tests/test_bridge.py` (ranked: an unnamed owner line chosen for her; the fallback turn);
  - `tests/test_turns.py` (her ranked wave; the pins updated);
  - `tests/test_moves.py`, `tests/test_nudge.py`.
- [ ] **Contract:** the TOML schema's bridge modes, and the turn-taking semantics with a ranked bridge. ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and §Turn-taking; pinned by `tests/test_turns.py` and `tests/test_lumi_api.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests.

### AGORA-271 — Her turns by outcome today — the bridge's state and her card

**Description:**
The owner judges «she answers about as often as the others» in the room. To make that visible:
- **The state:** the bridge counts today's turns by outcome in `state/lili.bridge.json`.
- **The card:** her card shows them.

Lumi's side suggested this too (its v3.3.2 review, a LOW deferred). Touches `agents/bridge.py`, `panel/memory.py` and
`panel/static/app.js`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The bridge's state:** `turns {day, reply, pass, silent}`, reset at the room's midnight (`TIMEZONE`).
  - `silent` counts every other outcome: busy, cap, timeout, unreachable, refused, off-contract, the persona drop.
  - No text is kept.
- **`bridge_view`** carries `turns_today` (`{reply, pass, silent}`, zeros on another day).
- **The card** reads «today: N replies · M passes · K silent», next to the lines she heard.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-270.

**Expected result:**
The owner sees how often she answers and how often she passes, without opening a log.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Each turn counts once, under its outcome. The counts reset at the room's midnight, and hold no text.
- [ ] `bridge_view` and the card show today's counts; another day's counts read zero.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_bridge.py` (the counts by outcome, the day roll), `tests/test_panel_bridge.py`, `tests/test_panel_static.py`.
- [ ] **Contract:** `state/lili.bridge.json` gains `turns`, and the panel's memory view `bridge.turns_today`. ARCHITECTURE §Data and state files, §The panel; pinned by the tests above.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests.
