## Findings

### 1. 🔴 A leading tag hides a name prefix from `clean_reply`
**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `_compose`; `agents/logic.py` `clean_reply`.

**Scenario:** `clean_reply` looks for `Name:` at the start of a line, and it runs before the split. The palette tells
her to put a tag before a sentence, so a line can start with a tag and then a name:
- `"Ну так.\n[sighs] Ада: а я думала інакше."` reaches the room and her voice with Ада's line in it, which breaks the
  rule "never another's words";
- `"[sighs] Ich: ти де?"` puts the owner's own line into the private chat;
- `"[laughs] Кіра: ну…"` goes out with her own name prefix.

**Fix:** `clean_reply` reads a line's prefix past any leading tags. Another speaker's line still ends the reply; her
own prefix is removed and the tags before it are kept for the voice.

### 2. 🟠 Tags and punctuation are sent
**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `_compose`, the check `if not words`.

**Scenario:** the palette's own example written as a whole reply, `[starts laughing] … [laughs] … [laughs harder] …`,
becomes the words `"………"`; `[laughs]!` becomes `"!"`. `voiceable()` accepts both, so the line is voiced, posted,
kept in her history, the session and the archive, and the others answer it. `strip_pass`'s `\w` check ran before the
split, so it does not catch this.

**Fix:** a reply whose words hold no letter or digit is silence: `if not re.search(r"\w", words)`.

### 3. 🟠 Variants of a tag reach her text
**Where:** `agents/emotion.py` `TAG_RE`.

**Scenario:** these stay in the message, the history and the archive, and a non-tag model reads them aloud:
- `[Laughs]`, `[LAUGHS]` (a capital at a sentence's start);
- `Так[laughs]` (the lookbehind fails right after a word);
- `[sighs.]`, `[laughs softly, then sighs]` (punctuation inside the bracket);
- `[зам’ялася]` (the typographic apostrophe).

**Fix:** a tag is a bracket that starts with a letter of any case and holds 2–40 characters other than a digit, a
bracket or a newline, and is not a link's text (`[тут](url)`). `[1]`, `arr[i]` and `[ОСТРІВ 3]` stay. The palette
already says nothing but these marks goes in square brackets. The cost: an index of two letters or more after a word,
such as `arr[idx]`, is now taken for a tag; she does not write code.

### 4. 🟡 A tag inside a fact
**Where:** `agents/facts.py` `split_marker`.

**Scenario:** `split_marker` runs before the split, and `.strip("…[]")` removes only the closing bracket.
`"ЗАПАМ'ЯТАЙ: він не любить зиму [sighs]"` is stored as `він не любить зиму [sighs`, which then appears in her
private prompt and in the panel's Facts tab.

**Fix:** the fact passes through `split_tags` before it is stripped.

### 5. 🟡 The tidy-up touches text without tags
**Where:** `agents/emotion.py` `split_tags`.

**Scenario:** the space before punctuation is removed everywhere, so `"Ну , добре :) так ?"` becomes
`"Ну, добре:) так?"` even though no tag was there.

**Fix:** tidy only where a tag was removed: a removed tag and the spaces around it become one space between words, and
nothing at a line's edge or before punctuation.

### 6. 🟡 A non-UTF-8 palette in the panel
**Where:** `panel/memory.py` `models_view`.

**Scenario:** `read_text(encoding="utf-8")` raises `UnicodeDecodeError`, which `except OSError` does not catch, so
the memory route returns a 500. It is unlikely, because her own config load would fail first.

**Fix:** catch `ValueError` as well; the Voice tab then shows no chips.

### 7. 🟡 A malformed voice setting at startup
**Where:** `agents/agent.py`, the TTS set-up (`float(os.environ.get("VOICE_STABILITY", …))`).

**Scenario:** `VOICE_STABILITY=0,3` in `kira.env` raises `ValueError`, and the container restarts in a loop.

**Why defer:** every other numeric env setting fails the same way at startup, and the panel validates its own
overrides. A change belongs to a pass over all of them.

### 8. 🟡 An out-of-range voice setting
**Where:** the same set-up.

**Scenario:** `VOICE_SPEED=1.5` set by hand is refused by ElevenLabs on every call. Every line goes out as text only and
is logged as "tts failed"; nothing is charged to the budget.

**Why defer:** only a hand edit of `kira.env` reaches it; the panel holds the values to their ranges.

### 9. 🟡 `eleven_v3` and stability
**Where:** `agents/settings.py`, the `ELEVENLABS_MODEL` choices.

**Scenario:** the panel offers `eleven_v3`. If v3 accepts only the stability presets 0.0, 0.5 and 1.0, her 0.3 would
fail every synthesis there.

**Why defer:** not verified; checking needs a paid live call. She runs `eleven_v4_turbo`, which accepts 0.3
(measured 2026-10-25).

### 10. 🟡 The palette's wording
**Where:** `agents/canon/kira.voice.md`.

**Note:** «ти можеш підказати йому, як їх сказати … і голос це зробить, а в тексті її ніхто не побачить» describes
her voice as something she gives instructions to. It names no model or bot and passes `BANNED_RE`, so the persona rule
holds; the wording is the owner's call, and the palette was verified on her real voice as written.

## What's solid

- **Every send path goes through `_compose`:** the Agora reply, fallbacks, resume, the private answer, writing first,
  and both doors. The fixed strings (`PRIVATE_CANT_LISTEN` and the like) hold no tags.
- **Only words reach any text:** the `m.text` body, the Telegram text, `private_history`, the session, the archive,
  `_last_sent`, `same_message`, `own_questions` and the facts extraction. The `m.audio` content has no caption.
- **A `Spoken` turned back into text gives the words** (`json.dumps`, sqlite binding, f-strings, `.strip()`); losing
  `.speech` only drops the emotion.
- **The palette reaches only reply prompts.** Summaries, memories and plans never see it.
- **A non-tag model never receives a tag** (`speech_for`), and with the voice off the fallback model gets words only.
- **The budget is consistent:** the cap check and `record_tts` both count the speech. Hermes keeps his own settings.
  Claude's request is byte-for-byte unchanged at the defaults.
- **`models_view` leaks nothing:** no voice id, no palette text, the path comes from the registry, bad JSON returns
  None, and the page escapes every value.
