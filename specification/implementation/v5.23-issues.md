Issues for phase **v5.23 — Кіра's memory by time: a day word for word, two days by the hour, four by the day, the
rest by recall** (version **v5 — Voice**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.23 and

24 hours, but never fewer than the last 20 lines. Before the window come the hours of the 2 days before it, then
4 days by the day, and nothing older: only her recall (`RECALL_N` 10, set in the panel on 2026-11-02) brings older
talk back.

and on v5.22's prompt parts, which show the result in Analytics → Prompts. The prompt ordered for a cache

### AGORA-188 — Gemini's window by time: the last 24 hours, never fewer than 20 lines

**Description:**
`Agent._gemini_lines` keeps the private lines open to Gemini from the last `PRIVATE_HISTORY_HOURS` (24) hours, and
never fewer than the last `PRIVATE_HISTORY_MIN` (20) lines (the owner, 2026-11-03: «Так, 20 рядків»). It touches
`agents/agent.py`, `agents/settings.py`, ARCHITECTURE.md and the tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The cut,** a pure function in `agents/logic.py` or `agents/private.py`, for example `window_by_time(times,
  now_ms, hours, floor, unanswered) -> int`. Over the open lines' times (oldest first) it returns the index of the
  window's first line:
  - each line from `now − hours` on;
  - at least the last `floor` lines;
  - never fewer than his lines since her last one (the existing guard);
  - `hours` 0 is no cut by time, as before.
- **`_gemini_lines`:**
  - `HISTORY_N` (300) stays the most it reads from the archive (`archive.latest`);
  - the closed lines are filtered out as now;
  - then comes the cut by time;
  - then the owner's `PRIVATE_HISTORY_N` cap, as now (0 = `HISTORY_N`; 1 = only his lines since her last one).
- **Unchanged:**
  - `gemini_history_shown`'s time lines (`history_times`);
  - `talk_now`'s edge (the window's first line), the backup's view (`private_history`, 300 lines of every mark), the
    recall's exclusion of the window's lines;
  - the log line «private chat: N lines of history from the archive», which now counts the lines after the cut.
- **Settings** in her agent settings (`agents/settings.py`, group «private», `cap:private`):
  - `PRIVATE_HISTORY_HOURS` (24, 0–168): the hours of the private chat Gemini sees word for word; 0 means by lines,
    as before;
  - `PRIVATE_HISTORY_MIN` (20, 0–300): the fewest last lines it sees, whatever their time.
  - Both are read at start, like `PRIVATE_HISTORY_N`, so a save restarts her.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Memory («Her private chat in layers»: the window by time and its floor);
  - §Configuration (the two rows, beside `PRIVATE_HISTORY_N`);
  - the private prompt's description.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Gemini's history window holds her last day of open talk word for word, at least the last 20 lines, and no more than
300.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A line 23 h 59 min old is in the window and one 24 h 01 min old is not, in `TIMEZONE`, across a DST switch.
- [ ] After a pause of 3 days the window still holds the last 20 open lines, and his unanswered lines are always in
  it.
- [ ] `PRIVATE_HISTORY_HOURS` 0 is the window by lines; `PRIVATE_HISTORY_N` still caps it.
- [ ] The time lines, the summaries' edge, the backup's view and the recall's exclusion behave as before.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - the pure cut on plain times: the edge, the floor, the unanswered guard, 0;
  - `_gemini_lines` over a made-up archive with an injected clock;
  - the backup's view unchanged;
  - the settings' names, defaults and bounds pinned (`tests/test_settings.py`).
- [ ] **Contract:** the private prompt's history window (ARCHITECTURE §Memory and §Configuration), pinned by the
  tests above.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-189 — The levels `hour` and `day`: 3 days by the hour, a day each to 7 days, nothing older in her prompt

**Description:**
`agents/talk.py` changes its levels from `hour` / `4h` / `week` to `hour` / `day`:
- hours for the last `TALK_HOUR_DAYS` (3) days;
- a summary a day for each day ended before the hours' band and within `TALK_DAY_DAYS` (7) days;
- nothing older is written or shown.

The four-hour blocks and the weeks already written stay in the archive. It touches `agents/talk.py`,
`agents/agent.py`, `agents/settings.py`, ARCHITECTURE.md and the tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`period_of`:** a `day` level from local midnight to local midnight, DST-safe as the week was; `iso_hour` is
  unchanged.
- **`periods_due(now_ms, tz, open_times, held, hour_days, day_days)`:**
  - `hour`: as now, starting within the last `hour_days` days;
  - `day`: ended before the hours' band, and ended within the last `day_days` days;
  - no `4h` and no `week` is ever due.
- **`FINER = {"day": "hour"}`:** a day is written from its hours when they cover it (`covered`), otherwise from its
  open lines. The first backfill's days 4–7 have no hours.
- **`SPAN["day"]`** («цього дня»); `TALK_WORDS_DAY` (150) caps a day's words.
- **`talk_section(rows, before_ms, now, tz, words_budget, day_days)`:**
  - only `hour` and `day` rows;
  - only those that began before the window's first line and no earlier than `day_days` days ago;
  - a day with text hides its hours;
  - oldest first, an empty row skipped, `TALK_PROMPT_WORDS` the cap;
  - `_label` gives a day `ago` alone (no part of the day), never a date or an hour.
- **The agent:**
  - `talk_day_days` and `talk_words["day"]` replace `talk_4h_days` and the `4h` and `week` words;
  - `_talk_tick` and `talk_now` pass them.
- **Settings** (`agents/settings.py`, «Пам'ять», `cap:talk`):
  - `TALK_HOUR_DAYS` now defaults to 3 (1–7);
  - new `TALK_DAY_DAYS` (7, 3–30) and `TALK_WORDS_DAY` (150, 20–400);
  - `TALK_4H_DAYS`, `TALK_WORDS_4H` and `TALK_WORDS_WEEK` go. A saved override of a removed name is dropped at the
    next save (v5.21 review #2) and ignored with a warning at start.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Memory («Her private chat in layers»: the levels, the bands, the writer's finer rows, what she sees);
  - §Contracts (the `talk` table's levels: `hour`, `day`; rows of `4h` and `week` kept, never shown);
  - §Configuration (the rows).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Her prompt's summaries are the hours of the 3 days before now that precede the window, then one summary per day back
to 7 days, and nothing older.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `periods_due` gives hours within 3 days and days ended within 7 (across midnight and a DST switch), and never
  a `4h` or a `week`.
- [ ] A day is written from its hours when they cover it, and from its lines when it has none. The hours stay.
- [ ] `talk_section` shows only `hour` and `day` rows, a day hides its hours, nothing older than 7 days, and an old
  `4h` or `week` row is never shown.
- [ ] A day is labelled by `ago` alone, with no date or hour.
- [ ] A removed setting saved in the panel is dropped at the next save and ignored at start.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_talk.py`: the bands, the labels, the section;
  - `tests/test_talk_writer.py`: a day from its hours, a day from its lines, the word cap, the backfill's order, no
    `4h`/`week` written;
  - `tests/test_settings.py`: the names, defaults and bounds.
  - Gemini mocked, the clock injected, made-up lines only.
- [ ] **Contract:** the `talk` table's levels and her private prompt's summaries section (ARCHITECTURE §Memory,
  §Contracts, §Configuration), pinned by the tests above.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
