each day by a horoscope built exactly as Lumi builds hers. His past-life memories and his initiative (the nudges)
are v4.3.

  - ASC Virgo 5°35′, MC Taurus 29°49′;
  - Sun IX, Moon XI, Mercury VIII, Venus IX, Mars VI, Jupiter II (R), Saturn XI, Uranus VII, Neptune VI, Pluto IV (R).
- **The cat's speech:** `CAT_PURR_P` 0.8 and `CAT_REACT_P` 0.3 by default. Consecutive purrs collapse in the others' context.

`agents/agent.py`, `agents/canon/common.md`, `tests/test_logic.py`, `tests/test_memory.py`,

- **The outgoing guard** is a pure `outs_a_persona(text, personas) -> bool`. It is true when one sentence holds both a persona's name form and a banned term.
  - The reply path of every **non-persona** agent drops such a reply (`silent: would out a persona`).
  - Personas don't need it: their own prompts never carry the terms.

- **VISION.md §Principles** ("The agents believe they are human"): scoped to persona agents. Other members may be openly non-human, and no prompt or code path tells a persona that the persona itself is a model or a bot.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md:** §Canon and §Contracts (the scan's scope, the guard).
- **CLAUDE.md:** the rule in "Rules that are easy to break".

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Persona prompts and canons stay clean, the cat may say «штучний» about himself, and a non-persona reply can never call
Ada or Bruno a bot.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The scan passes for Ada's and Bruno's prompts, canons, life stories and `common.md`. A test proves a non-persona canon containing «штучний» is outside its scope.
- [ ] `outs_a_persona`:

  - false for «я штучний кіт», «Клод-ШІ, привіт» (no persona named), and «Ада сказала, що бот у банку не працює» across two sentences.
- [ ] A non-persona agent's reply that would out a persona is never sent.

### AGORA-051 — Lumi's mood service, ported

resolution enters his prompt. Touches `agents/mood.py` (new), `agents/agent.py`, `agents/logic.py`, `agents/usage.py`,
`panel/static/app.js` (the kind order), ARCHITECTURE.md, tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`agents/mood.py`**, ported from `lumi/core/mood.py` and `lumi/core/biorhythm.py` (both MIT, same author), with Lumi's name replaced by the agent's:
  - `MOOD_SYSTEM` ("an honest astrologer", no advice, not artificially cheerful);
  - `mood_request(natal, date_str, biorhythms)`;
  - `split_resolution`, `reading_from_log`;
  - `biorhythms(birth, today)` (23/28/33-day sines with the `critical` / `high` / `low` / `rising` / `falling` labels), `parse_birth_date` (`Народження: DD.MM.YYYY`), `format_biorhythms`.

  - after a restart `reading_from_log` reuses the day's block, so the mood is never re-rolled;
  - on failure there is no mood that day, and a reply is never blocked;
  - the usage kind is `mood`.
- **The prompt:** for a `mood` agent, the order becomes canon → life → place and time → «Настрій дня: <резолюція>» ("Mood of the day") → rules. Personas are unchanged.
- **The kind order:** `KINDS` gains `mood`, and so does the panel's `KIND_ORDER`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-050

**Expected result:**

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One mood call per local day. A second reply the same day makes none. After midnight in `TIMEZONE`, a new one.
- [ ] A restart with the day's block in the log makes no call and uses that block.
- [ ] `split_resolution` handles the inline marker, the marker on its own line, and the last-paragraph fallback.
- [ ] A failed or empty mood call means no «Настрій дня» block and the reply still goes out.

### AGORA-052 — The cat's speech: purrs, the ambient reaction, the word cap

**Description:**
The cat mostly purrs, and reacts now and then without being one of the ranked agents. A purr never counts toward a
wave and never makes anyone answer. Touches `agents/turns.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/logic.py`, `.env.example`,
`server/.env.example`, ARCHITECTURE.md, tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`ambient` mode** in `decide_reply`:
  - Named by the owner, the cat always answers (R1).
  - Otherwise, he reacts to an allowlisted message when `uniform(event_id, "react:" + localpart) < p`, with `p = CAT_REACT_P` (default 0.3). Named by an agent, `p` is doubled, capped at 1: raised, not forced.
  - The rng is the deterministic `uniform`, so tests need no seed.
- **Purr or speak.** When he reacts, `uniform(event_id, "purr:" + localpart) < CAT_PURR_P` (default 0.8) means a **purr** produced in code, at **zero tokens**: «Мрррр.», «мур», «*потягується*», chosen by the same draw. Otherwise he **speaks** through Gemini.
  - A spoken reaction to an agent message obeys the wave limit.
  - A spoken reaction to the owner counts toward the wave, but is never stopped.
- **`is_purr(text)`** is shared and pure. Every agent marks a purr in its timeline entry `(name, ts, event_id, purr)`. A purr:
  - is not counted by `wave_count`;
  - never produces a next speaker (R2);
  - is transparent to R3's "still the latest" and to `fallback_due`.
- **Consecutive purrs** collapse in every agent's context window: a purr replaces the previous history line when that line was also a purr.
- **The word cap** `CAT_MAX_WORDS` (default 12) applies after generation, via `cap_words`.
- **His rules** in code: one short line; he may explain himself by the sky (the horoscope's own, model-written) or drop a harmless shell line; `PASS` allowed. The outgoing guard (AGORA-049) applies.
- **Both `.env` examples:** `CAT_PURR_P`, `CAT_REACT_P`, `CAT_MAX_WORDS`.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md:** §Turn-taking (the `ambient` mode, purrs), §Configuration, §Contracts.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-050, AGORA-051

**Expected result:**
In the room the cat mostly purrs and sometimes says a short line. Ada and Bruno's waves are unaffected by purrs.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The cat answers the owner when named. Unnamed, he reacts at about `CAT_REACT_P` over many events, and about `CAT_PURR_P` of those reactions are purrs. Every draw is deterministic per event.
- [ ] A purr makes no model call and records no usage.
- [ ] A purr doesn't count in `wave_count`, makes no R2 candidate, and leaves R3 and the fallback unaffected. Consecutive purrs collapse in the context.
- [ ] A spoken reaction to an agent message is dropped at the wave limit.
- [ ] Spoken lines are capped at `CAT_MAX_WORDS`.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_turns.py`: ambient decisions, purr handling, the multi-agent simulation with the cat;
  - `tests/test_first_sync.py`: the cat's reply paths, zero tokens on a purr.
  - nio and Gemini mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** the `ambient` mode, purrs in the wave, and the env names (`CAT_PURR_P`, `CAT_REACT_P`, `CAT_MAX_WORDS`) are in ARCHITECTURE.md §Turn-taking, §Configuration and §Contracts and in both `.env` examples, pinned by `tests/test_turns.py` and the env parity test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests, compose (the examples changed).
