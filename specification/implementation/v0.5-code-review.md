### #1 — unconsumed join response
**Failure scenario:** repeated joins/retries exhaust the aiohttp connector pool; later syncs stall with no
obvious cause.
**Anchor:** `agents/agent.py` `join_room`.
**Fix:** `await resp.read()` (or `resp.release()`) before returning; keep the status check.

### #2 — unbounded echo
**Failure scenario:** a pasted huge text in Agora is doubled by two bots, tripling room traffic and, from
v1.1, flooding the Gemini context.
**Anchor:** `agents/logic.py` `echo_reply`.
**Fix:** truncate the echoed text at 4 000 characters with `…`; pin with a test.
