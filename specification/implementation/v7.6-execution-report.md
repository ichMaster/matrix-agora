## Detailed Results

### AGORA-260: the switch, the window, the digest
- **The switch:** `MEMORY_MODE` (`layered` | `lumi`), a live switch per companion.
- **The window and the digest:** `agents/lumi.py` holds the live window after the fold point (`n` to `n + batch`
  lines) and the digest's file, its roll at her midnight and its layer in the tail. The fold runs off the reply path,
  one cheap call a batch of her day; an earlier day's lines are passed over with no call.
- **The cache:** in `lumi` it holds the start alone. v7.5 review #9 is closed.

### AGORA-261: the gists
- **Talk:** `gist_day` / `gist_week` rows give a line a day for 7 days and a line a week for the 4 weeks before.
- **Day memories:** one line each, from `<key>.gist.md`.
- **Reconciled:** a day's gist is written from that day's summaries, not from the running digest, which may miss lines
  still in her window at midnight.
