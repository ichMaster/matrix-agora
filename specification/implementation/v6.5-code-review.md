## Findings

### #1 🟠 Model calls in the idle loop without a timeout

**Where:** `agents/llm.py` `complete` (line 138), `probe` (224), `embed` (255), `search` (277).

**Failure scenario:** on 2026-11-10 at 08:58:52 Устина's session summary went to Pro (`think`) and never returned.
Her idle loop waited on it for three hours: she did not write first, her hourly events did not roll, no summary was
written. `20c3102` bounds `think`, but the same loop awaits `complete` (the summary's first call on Flash, the day
memories, plans, the today block, events, mood) and `embed` (`embed_tick`, every pass). A connection that hangs the
same way on any of these stops the loop again. `complete` also carries Flash answers (`reply` on a non-thinking
model), which v5.25 meant to cut at `REPLY_TIMEOUT_S`.

**Fix:** every call goes through `_within`: `complete` at `REPLY_TIMEOUT_S` for an answer (`refusal=True`, which
only `reply` passes) and at `THINK_TIMEOUT_S` otherwise; `probe`, `embed` and `search` at `THINK_TIMEOUT_S`. Each is
tried once more, then fails as a provider error does now (logged by its type, silence).

### #2 🟡 The backup's photo could reach Google

**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `_make_photo`.

**Failure scenario:** `_make_photo(shot, fal=True)` with `self.fal_photos is None` skips the fal branch and calls
`self.photos.make` (Google). The project rule is that the backup's talk never goes to Google. Today
`_offer_backup_photo` and `_photo_closed(door, fal=True)` both need the fal maker, so the call is not made.

**Fix:** `fal=True` without the fal maker returns `Made(None, why="off")`.

### #3 🟡 «фільтр вимкнено» before fal authorizes the account

**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `photos_answer`.

The request asks for the checker off. Whether fal honours it depends on the account (the owner's email to fal is
outstanding). The six flag probes of 2026-11-09 were all clean, so they show nothing either way. Nothing breaks: a
flagged photo is black, blocked and never sent. Only the line overstates. Revisit once fal answers.

### #4 🟡 Generated photos among her references

**Where:** `agents/photo.py` `load_faces`.

Her folder holds the owner's chosen faces and generated `gen-*` scenes. `pick_faces` keeps her main face and draws
three more from all of them. Whether generated scenes belong among the references is the owner's call.

## What's solid

- The key goes only to fal's queue and upload hosts, never to a file URL; the request, the URLs and the key never
  reach a log or `repr` (pinned by `test_falphoto`).
- A job is retried only before fal accepts it; a timeout after that cancels it, so fal never runs it twice.
- A flagged, black or empty photo is blocked and counted as paid, never sent; ffmpeg failing reads as not black and
  is logged.
- What the backup writes for Seedream is never logged or kept; the log names the route and the length only; tests
  use made-up lines.
- The backup's shot is only photo or selfie; a video line from it is dropped and logged.
- The route file is atomic and 0600; a corrupt or missing one reads as `google`.
- Кіра has no `photos_fal`, so `/photos` is closed for her and her backup never gets the photo skill.
- Every builder carries `images`; a row with an unpriced fal model has no cost («—»), never $0; the daily report's
  service gets `PRICE_IMAGES` through `env_file`.
