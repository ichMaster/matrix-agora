# v5.12 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.12 — Кіра reads the news and thinks: her thoughts on what she reads and on her talks**
(version **v5 — Voice: Hermes, the agents hear and speak**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in
[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.12 and the contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one
phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-131 → **AGORA-132…137**).

Кіра reads the news on a schedule and thinks about what she read and about her talks, with the owner and in Agora.
Her thoughts go into her archive and are recalled as «я думала…». They reach her prompts as «Що ти сьогодні
думала», her lines written first, a new Agora move and her day memory, and the panel lists them. It builds on v5.8's
archive and recall, v5.10's mood, v5.11's Zaxid.net feed and Claude's Guardian helpers. The other persons and
v5.13's backup model are later.

**Reconciled with the code (2026-10-28):**
- **`ago` already exists:** `agents.world.ago(then, now)` gives the bands the ROADMAP lists, since cbbf28b (v5.8).
  `archive.render_recalled` already uses it («минулого тижня, в Агорі — Ада: …»). This phase only renders a thought
  with it; no new `ago`.
- **Zaxid.net's feed is fetched already.** v5.11's `Agent.ensure_citynews` fetches it every three hours, merges it
  by link into `state/kira.citynews.json` and keeps the day's items. The reading takes its titles from there; no
  second feed fetch.
  - The full text is new: the article page's JSON-LD `articleBody`, fetched with `citynews.http_text(url)`.
  - The ROADMAP's «`aiohttp`» is stale: the feed uses stdlib `urllib` in a thread.
- **The Guardian helpers exist:**
  - `agents/guardian.py` has `search_url`, `read_url`, `results`, `article` and `http_fetch` (aiohttp, 10 s).
  - `search_url` always sends `q`. A section's newest items need no query, so an empty `q` is dropped.
- **The archive** (`agents/archive.py`):
  - `PLACES = ("agora", "private", "facts")`, and `append` refuses a place not listed.
  - `around()` brings a recalled line's neighbours from its own place; facts get none.
  - The private recall searches `("agora", "private", "facts")`; Agora's searches `("agora",)` (`agent.py`).
- **The persona moves:** `agents/moves.py` has `KINDS = ("ask", "disagree", "plan", "story")`, `WEIGHT_VARS` and
  `persona_move(..., has_plans, has_life)`. «answer» is what is left.
- **The prompt order:** `logic.build_instruction` follows canon → life → world → memories → plans → today → mood →
  pastlife → nudge → facts → agora_now → recalled → summary → private → move → asked → voice → rules.
- **The day memory:** `chronicle.day_memory_request(..., events=)` (v5.11) gets a `thoughts` part the same way.
- **The idle watcher** runs mood → events → nudge → news → facts → embed → private nudge → summary, each guarded. The
  reading and the reflection join it the way `ensure_events` does: a lock and a 10-minute back-off.
- **The capabilities:** `roster.CAPABILITIES`, `PERSONA_ONLY` and `NEEDS` (`events` needs `life` and `today`). The
  TOML's `natal` file is required with `mood` (`config.py`); the new `reading` file is required with `thoughts` the
  same way.
- **The usage kinds:** `usage.KINDS` ends with `event`, and the panel's `KIND_ORDER` mirrors it.

### AGORA-132 — What she reads: Zaxid.net's full text, the Guardian's sections, the seen items

**Description:**
A new `agents/reading.py` holds the reading's sources, pure where it decides and with an injectable fetch:
- Zaxid.net's day items, from v5.11's `state/kira.citynews.json`, with the full text from the article page;
- the newest items of the Guardian's sections of her interests;
- the items she has seen, so nothing is read twice.

`agents/guardian.py` gets one small change.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Zaxid.net's full text.** `article_body(html) -> str | None` (pure):
  - It reads each `<script type="application/ld+json">` block with `json`.
  - It finds an object (or an item of a list or `@graph`) with a string `articleBody`.
  - It returns that body with its whitespace collapsed, cut at `ARTICLE_MAX_CHARS` (4,000).
  - It returns None when no block holds one, or a block does not parse.
  - **The fetch:** `citynews.http_text(link)`, only for an https link on `zaxid.net` (pure `zaxid_link_ok`). Any
    other link is skipped.
- **The Guardian's sections.**
  - `guardian.search_url` drops `q` when the query is empty, so a section's newest items need no query (the
    change).
  - `guardian_fresh(fetch, key, sections, today) -> list[Pick]`: one search per section, from the last day, at most
    `MAX_RESULTS` each, de-duplicated by id. `guardian.results` turns each hit into an item.
  - The full text: `read_url` + `article()` (`bodyText`, cut at the same cap).
  - **Without `GUARDIAN_API_KEY`** the Guardian is skipped, and this is logged once.
  - **A failed call** is logged by its type only: the URL carries the key.
- **One shape for both sources:** `Item(source, id, title, summary, link, published)`.
  - `source` is `zaxid` or `guardian`.
  - `id` is the link for Zaxid.net and the content id for the Guardian.
  - `summary` is the feed's description or the Guardian's trail text.
- **The seen items:** `state/kira.reading.json` (0600, atomic, as `citynews.save`).
  - It keeps `{"seen": {id: ms}, "last_reading_ms": n, "reflected": {place: ms}}` and no texts.
  - Seen ids older than 7 days are pruned on save.
  - A missing or corrupt file means a fresh start, never a crash.
  - `fresh(items, seen) -> list[Item]` (pure) drops the seen ones.
- **The setting `READING_SECTIONS`:** comma-separated Guardian section ids, default
  `science,world,environment,technology,culture`. A malformed id is dropped, using `search_url`'s own check.
- **The network fixture:** the autouse conftest fixture also fails a test that reaches `reading`'s real fetchers.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Given canned pages and payloads, `agents/reading.py` gives her fresh items from both sources and their full texts,
and an item once shown is never fresh again.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `article_body` on a saved Zaxid.net page gives the body. A page with no `articleBody`, a broken JSON-LD block or
  no block gives None.
- [ ] A non-https or non-Zaxid link is never fetched.
- [ ] `search_url` with an empty query has no `q`, and with a query it is unchanged (Claude's tool still works).
- [ ] The Guardian sections are read with canned payloads and de-duplicated. With no key they are skipped and logged
  once. A failure is logged by its type, and the key never appears in a log.
- [ ] `state/kira.reading.json` is 0600 and atomic, prunes ids older than 7 days, and survives a corrupt file.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_reading.py` covers the parsers, the links, the sections, the seen items and the state
  file, with canned fetches. The network fixture makes a real fetch fail a test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-133 — Her thoughts kept: the capability, the day file, the archive's places, «я думала…»

**Description:**
A new capability `thoughts` and a new module `agents/thoughts.py` for her thoughts' day files. The archive gets two
new places, `thought` and `thought_private`, and her recall renders a thought as hers. The reading paragraph goes in
her private canon. Files: `agents/roster.py`, `agents/config.py`, `agents/thoughts.py`, `agents/archive.py`,
`agents/agent.py` (the recall places), `agents/kira.toml`, ARCHITECTURE.md.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The capability `thoughts`:** in `CAPABILITIES` and `PERSONA_ONLY`, and `NEEDS["thoughts"] = "recall"`.
  `kira.toml` switches it on (`thoughts = true`).
- **The TOML key `reading`:** the file of what she reads, next to her canon (`agents/canon/kira.reading.md`, private).
  - It is required with `thoughts`, like `natal` with `mood`. A missing or empty file is a `ConfigError` at startup.
  - Its `#` lines are comments.
  - It reaches only the reading's pick call (AGORA-134).
  - **The draft:** Claude drafts the paragraph from her canon (her work, Lviv, Ukraine, what she cares about; on
    politics and war she reacts and holds views as a person in Lviv does, and does not argue party politics unless
    asked). The owner reviews and edits it; it is never committed and never quoted.
- **The day file:** `state/kira.thoughts/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (0600; the folder 0700), appended and never rewritten.
  - **One block per thought:**

    ```
    <text, one line>
    <!-- title <the article's title> -->
    <!-- link <https url> -->
    ```

    The title and link lines are for news only.
  - **Pure helpers:** `parse_day(text) -> list[Thought]` (the time, kind, place, source, text, title, link), `block(t)`

- **The archive's places:** `PLACES` gains `thought` and `thought_private`.
  - A thought is appended with the key `thought:<date>:<HH:MM>:<n>`, the channel `thought` and her name as the
    speaker.
  - The idle watcher embeds it like any line.
- **The recall places:**
  - Her private recall searches `agora`, `private`, `facts`, `thought` and `thought_private`.
  - Her Agora recall searches `agora` and `thought`, so a reflection on the private chat never reaches Agora.
- **The rendering** (`render_recalled`):
  - A recalled thought reads «{ago} я думала: {text}». It never has a date or a clock time, and never reads as
    something said.
  - It is recalled alone, with no neighbours (as a fact is).
  - `RECALL_TITLE` names thoughts too: «…з давніших розмов і твоїх думок…».
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Memory: her thoughts, the day file, the places;
  - §Contracts:
    - the capability and `NEEDS`;
    - the TOML key `reading`;
    - the state folder `state/<name>.thoughts/`;
    - the archive's places;
    - the recall title.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
A thought written to her day file and her archive is recalled in her private chat as «вчора я думала: …». Agora
recalls only her `thought` place.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `thoughts` without `recall`, or on a non-person, is a `RosterError`. With `thoughts`, a missing or empty
  `reading` file is a `ConfigError`.
- [ ] The day file round-trips through `block` and `parse_day`, with a news thought's title and link. A malformed
  block is skipped. The file is 0600.
- [ ] `append` accepts `thought` and `thought_private`.
- [ ] The private recall finds both places. Agora's recall never returns `thought_private`.
- [ ] A recalled thought renders «вчора я думала: …» with no date and no hh:mm, and alone, with no neighbours.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_thoughts.py`: the day file and the rendering, with an injected clock across the bands' edges:

  - `tests/test_archive.py`: the places and the recall;

### AGORA-134 — The reading and the reflection: the schedule and the three calls

**Description:**
The idle watcher reads her news every `READING_EVERY_S` within `READING_HOURS`. It also reflects every
`REFLECT_EVERY_S` on each place with new lines, even mid-conversation and off the reply path. Three calls do the
work, all on `GEMINI_MODEL`: the pick, a thought per pick, and the reflection. Files: `agents/reading.py` (the
requests and the parsers, pure), `agents/agent.py`, `agents/usage.py`, `agents/settings.py`, ARCHITECTURE.md.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The schedule** (pure: `reading_due(now_ms, last_ms, every_ms, hour, hours)` and
  `reflect_due(now_ms, last_ms, every_ms)`):
  - `ensure_reading(now)` and `ensure_reflection(now)` run in the idle watcher after the events, each under its own
    lock with a 10-minute back-off on a failure. They never block the watcher's other steps.
  - **The reading** runs within `READING_HOURS` (default `08-01`), every `READING_EVERY_S` (default 10800). Its time
    is saved before the calls, so a restart never reads twice.
  - **The reflection** runs every `REFLECT_EVERY_S` (default 3600) at any hour. It is never awaited by an answer and
    sends nothing.
- **The pick** (usage kind `reading`), `pick_request(...)` and `parse_picks(text, n, cap)`, both pure.
  - One call gets the fresh items of both sources, numbered: the title and the description only.
  - It also gets her canon, her reading paragraph, the mood of the day and her last thoughts.
  - It answers with at most `READING_PICKS` (default 2) numbers, or `0` for none.
  - Every shown item is marked seen. Anything that does not parse means no pick.
- **A thought per pick** (kind `thought`), `thought_request(...)`:
  - **The full text** comes from AGORA-132. A pick with no full text is skipped.
  - **Recall** brings her earlier thoughts on the title, places `thought` and `thought_private`.
  - **The rules:**
    - first person, 1–3 sentences, in Ukrainian; she reads the Guardian in English;
    - only the article's facts: no number, quote or name it does not hold. The opinion is hers;
    - people in the news stay strangers;
    - she read it on her phone, never «шукала»;
    - a changed mind is a new thought, and the old one stays.
  - **Kept:** the day file (`news`, place `thought`, the source, title and link) and the archive.
- **The reflection** (kind `reflection`), `reflection_request(...)`:
  - **Which places:** each of `private` (Element and Telegram) and `agora` with new lines since the place's
    `reflected` mark (`archive.latest(place, n, since_ms)`), at least one of them not hers.
  - **The call** reads those lines with her canon, the mood of the day and her last thoughts, and writes one or two
    thoughts of hers about the talk.
  - **The rule:** it never invents Ich's or a member's words or deeds. It may say what she thinks of what was said.
  - **Where it goes:** a private reflection to the place `thought_private`, an Agora one to `thought`.
  - **The mark** moves to the last line it read.
- **Every text** is checked with the banned words (`BANNED_RE`) and the persona rule, so it never says she is a
  model or a bot. A rejected text gets one regeneration and is then dropped, logged by kind, never by text.
- **Usage:** the kinds `reading`, `thought` and `reflection` join `usage.KINDS`. All three are on `GEMINI_MODEL`
  (Flash), never her reply model.
- **Settings:** in `agents/settings.py`, group «Життя», scope `cap:thoughts`:
  - `READING_EVERY_S`, `READING_HOURS`, `READING_PICKS`, `READING_SECTIONS`;
  - `REFLECT_EVERY_S`, `THOUGHTS_IN_PROMPT` (3).
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Memory: the reading, the reflection, their schedule;
  - §Token accounting: the three kinds;
  - §Configuration: the settings.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-132, AGORA-133

**Expected result:**
With an injected clock and a mocked Gemini, she reads at most two items every three hours in her hours and writes a
thought on each. Every hour she reflects on each place that has new lines, even while a session is open, and no
answer waits for it.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The reading runs only within its hours and its period, never twice after a restart, and backs off after a
  failure.
- [ ] Picks: a number out of range, a repeat, more than the cap, `0`, or garbage, all as specified. Every shown item
  is seen.
- [ ] A thought is appended to the day file and the archive with its place, source, title and link. A pick with no
  full text is skipped.
- [ ] A reflection is written per place with new lines and none for a place without, mid-session too. Its mark moves.
  A private reflection goes to `thought_private`.
- [ ] No answer waits on a reading or a reflection (a test holds the reflection's call open while an answer is sent).
- [ ] The reflection prompt forbids invented words or deeds of Ich and the members. No prompt says she is a model or
  a bot.
- [ ] A banned word gets one regeneration, then the thought is dropped. No text and no key reach a log.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_reading.py` (the requests, `parse_picks`, the schedule) and
  `tests/test_thoughts_flow.py` (the watcher's steps with a fake Gemini, an injected clock and rng, canned sources).
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage kinds (pinned by `tests/test_usage.py`); ARCHITECTURE.md §Token accounting and
  §Configuration updated.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
