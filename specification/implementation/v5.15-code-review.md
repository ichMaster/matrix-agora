## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A message Gemini blocked as `prohibited_content` is sent to OpenRouter to be rated

**Where:**
- `agents/agent.py:980` (`judge_later` after a block);
- `agents/agent.py:1304` (`waiting_items`, the watcher).

**What happens:**
- On a block, the owner's message is marked `waiting`, and the judge sends it to OpenRouter.
- Google's `prohibited_content` reason is the narrow one, for the content its filter treats as the worst. It is
  not the ordinary sexually-explicit category.
- OpenRouter's terms say it scans requests for CSAM.

**Failure scenario:** a message Gemini blocked as `prohibited_content` because it reads as such content reaches
OpenRouter's scanner, and the owner's OpenRouter account is put at risk. On 2026-10-30 the one known line of that kind
was censored by hand before the deploy, at the owner's word. Nothing in the code keeps the next one away.

**Why defer:** the cure has a cost the owner should weigh. A prompt-stage block may come from older lines, as on
2026-10-30, so censoring the new message for good on `prohibited_content` alone could close an innocent line.

**Proposed fix (for the owner to choose):** on a `prohibited_content` block, mark the owner's new message `censored`
at once, with no rating.

### 2. 🟡 At the window's end, a failure in the judge's bookkeeping loses her answer

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:1134` (`_window_end_judged`).

**What happens:** `Judge.rate` never raises. But `judge_items` reads the marks (`_marks_of`) and writes them
(`apply_verdicts`) through the archive, the facts and the route file. An `OSError` or a SQLite error there propagates.
`_window_end_judged` only cancels the backup and re-raises.

**Failure scenario:** a locked or corrupt archive at the window's end means no answer at all. The owner's message
stays unanswered until his next one, although the backup was already writing.

**Proposed fix:** an `Exception` from the rating counts as unrated for every line. The backup's answer goes out, and
a new window opens, which is the safe side.

### 4. 🟡 A verdict on a fact re-mirrors every fact

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:1263` and `agents/archive.py:347` (`sync_facts` replaces the `facts` place whole).

**What happens:** each change of a fact's mark drops every fact's vector, and the next embed ticks make them all
again. On 2026-10-30 that was about 100 facts, twice. The cost is negligible, and recall of facts waits for a tick or
two.

**Home:** cleanup. Update one fact's mirror row in place.

**What happens:** the card shows `waiting` (and `censored` in the view) but not the backup's lines the judge reopened,
so the owner cannot see how many of the backup's lines Gemini now reads.

**Home:** backlog.

## What's solid

- **Failures never censor:** an error, the timeout or an answer that does not parse leaves a line unrated. Only a
  verdict or a refusal flags, and the tests drive each case.
- **Nothing is rated twice:** `judge_items` reads the marks first, and a line or fact with a verdict keeps it.
  The watcher, a rating after a send and the window's end can overlap safely.
- **Gemini's last word works live:** after the 20:34 block the reopened lines were closed (by hand that once, in code
  since d4bebac). The next line, written first at 20:41, went through Gemini.
- **Gemini's history from the archive:** a long talk through the backup no longer empties it. On the live system it
  went from 0 to 150 lines.
- **The mark «⚙️ backup»:** it is only in the text message. The voice, the history, the archive and the prompts never
  get it, and a line read back from the Element room drops it.
- **OpenRouter gets** the policy and the numbered lines only. Every request asks for `data_collection: deny`, and the
  key leaves every agent's environment at start.
