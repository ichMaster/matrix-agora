### AGORA-169: The Analytics view
**Status:** awaiting owner · **Commit:** 08879a6 · **GitHub:** #169 (open, `Refs #169`)
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)

**Built:**
- **Opening it:** «Analytics» in the rail and on the Tokens card; «← Dashboard» goes back.
- **The choice:** the presets or a custom From–To, the agent (a select), kept in the address (`#analytics?range=30d&agent=kira`). The panel's today is the server's.
- **Tiles:** the cost (`≥` when partial) and its change, calls (failed, blocked), tokens, the cost a day, the trend
  (± $ a week) and the month at this rate.
- **Charts:** five inline-SVG charts built by pure functions, each with native tooltips:
  - the cost a day by service, with the 7-day average;
  - the running total against the previous period;
  - the activity a day (calls or tokens);
  - the shares;
  - the agents, with the previous period's tick.
- **Tables:**
  - the services, with sparklines, the change from the previous period and expandable models;
  - what it was for;
  - the agents;
  - the subscriptions;
  - the unpriced models.
- **The CSV:** made in the browser; quotes doubled, and a leading `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` prefixed with `'`.
- **Colours:** one fixed colour per service (`--viz-1…8`) for both themes. It was checked as a set with the dataviz
  validator against the panel's card surfaces, and every check passed. Light slots 2–5 are under 3:1 on the card, so
  a legend always names them and the tables carry every number.
- **The poll:** the view never redraws under the owner's hand.

**As built:** the rendering was checked locally on a copy of the server's usage files (numbers only) in Chrome. Four
fixes followed:
- a finer axis top;
- the trend tile's text;
- the table height inside a card;
- a class name that clashed with the handoff's `.label`;
- the periods scrolling on a phone.
