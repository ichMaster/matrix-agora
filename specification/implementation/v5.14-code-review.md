## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A closed line longer than the history's entry cap stays open to Gemini until a restart

**Where:**
- `agents/agent.py:1111` (`close_lines`);
- `agents/agent.py:1086` (`gemini_history`).

**What happens:**
- `append_history` cuts a history entry at `ENTRY_MAX_CHARS` (4000) and adds «…».
- `close_lines` puts the full text into `_closed`, and so does her own line from the backup.
- `gemini_history` compares the entry `(speaker, text)` with `_closed`. For a line over 4000 characters the two never
  match, so the line stays in the history Gemini reads.

**Failure scenario:**
1. The owner sends a 4050-character message, and Gemini blocks it at the prompt stage.
2. The line is marked `waiting` in the archive, but its cut copy in her history stays open.
3. Every following Gemini call carries it, and each is blocked again until the line leaves the history or she
   restarts. After a restart the seed reads the full text from the archive, and the line closes.
4. The same happens with an answer of the backup over 4000 characters.

**Live evidence:** none so far. No private line in the archive is over 4000 characters (a count, read 2026-10-30).

**Proposed fix:** `close_lines` closes the line as the history holds it, cut the same way as `append_history` cuts it
(one shared helper in `agents/logic.py`).

### 2. 🟠 A prompt block caused by the open lines themselves marks nothing and comes back on every call

**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `_private_text` (the Gemini route), and the ROADMAP's own note «The blocked prompt's older
lines keep their default; v5.15's judge rates them at a block».

**What happens:** a prompt block marks only the owner's lines since her last answer. When the open lines that Gemini
answered one by one are what trips the filter together, nothing that caused the block is closed.

**Live evidence:** at 08:00:28 on 2026-10-30 a line she writes first (`private_nudge`) was blocked at the prompt stage,
with no new line of the owner in it. The open history then held lines that Gemini had answered at 03:36–03:46 and
04:25–04:29. The backup wrote her line, as designed.

**Why defer:** this is the gap the ROADMAP leaves to v5.15 on purpose. The judge rates the blocked prompt's older
lines, and only a verdict can close a line Gemini answered. Under `auto`, each such block opens a window, so the
owner always gets an answer.

**Home:** v5.15.

### 3. 🟡 The panel's card shows a window that has already ended as open

**Where:** `panel/static/app.js:653` (`routeLine`).

**What happens:** the route file keeps `window_until_ms` until Gemini answers after it, because that is what tells
`window_end` from `gemini`. The card prints «window until 03:52» from it with no comparison to the time now.

**Failure scenario:** a window ends at 03:52, and no message comes until 09:00. At 09:00 the card still reads
«window until 03:52», which looks like the backup still answers. `/status` says it right («вікно backup скінчилось»).
This is the same kind of confusion the owner hit with `/status` (fixed in 9e56a7c).

**Proposed fix:** `route_view` reports `window_open` (the window's end is after now). The card shows «window until
HH:MM» only while it is open, and «window ended» after.

### 4. 🟡 At the window's end the backup's lost answer still keeps a fact and counts as an answer

**Where:**
- `agents/agent.py:1068` (the backup started beside Gemini);
- `agents/agent.py:2728` (`remember` inside `_compose`);
- `agents/fallback.py:135` (the usage line's `ok` before `backup_check`);
- `agents/route.py:148` (`day_counts`).

**What happens:**
- At the window's end the backup usually finishes first (about 10 s against Gemini's 15 s). Its `_compose` has then
  already kept any «ЗАПАМ'ЯТАЙ» fact (`waiting`), even when Gemini's line wins and the backup's is never sent.
- `/status` counts every `ok` usage line as an answer. That includes a backup line that lost, and one that
  `backup_check` dropped.

**Impact:** a fact the owner did say, kept as `waiting`, and `/status` reports one backup answer too many per window
end. Neither is a leak or a wrong route.

**Home:** backlog. Apply the backup's facts only when its line is the one sent, and count answers from the sent lines.

### 5. 🟡 Blocks under a `gemini` pin count toward the day cap after `/auto`

**Where:** `agents/route.py:71` (`after`).

**What happens:** `blocks` records every block, pinned or not. After a morning under `/gemini` with 10 or more blocks
(there were about 20 on 2026-10-30), `/auto` followed by one block opens the window to the day's end.

**Why it is only LOW:** this follows the ROADMAP literally («10 blocks in her local day»), and `/status` shows the count
(«сьогодні N з 10»). Whether pinned blocks should count is the owner's call.

**Home:** backlog.

### 6. 🟡 `_closed` keeps every closed line's text for the process's life

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:1115`.

**What happens:** only `/purge` or a restart empties `_closed`. At about 200 lines of the backup's talk a day, it holds
a few MB of text after weeks of uptime, most of it long out of her history.

**Home:** cleanup. Keep in `_closed` only the lines still in `private_history`.
