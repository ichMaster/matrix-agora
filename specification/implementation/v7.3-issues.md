### AGORA-246 — Gemini beside Claude for her media and her fallback: the photos' key, the fallback's own cap

**Description:**
On Claude her photos (Google's image model), her clips (Omni Flash) and her Gemini fallback at the limit need a Gemini
client the agent can reach; the fallback's answer needs a cap of its own model. Touches `agents/claude_sdk.py`
(`claude_client_for`, `ClaudeClient`), `agents/agent.py` (the makers' construction), `agents/photo.py`,
`agents/video.py`, `agents/llm.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `claude_client_for` builds the Gemini client also when the agent makes media (`photos` or `videos`), and the client
  exposes it (`ClaudeClient.gemini`: the one Gemini client beside it — the fallback's, the embedder's, the makers').
- `photo_maker` and `clip_maker` take the Gemini SDK client to use (else they build their own, as now); `Agent` builds
  them from `self.llm` on Gemini and from `self.llm.gemini` on Claude — never from `GEMINI_API_KEY` in the
  environment, which is gone on Claude (v7.1 code review #10). Without a Gemini key on Claude, no photos and no clips,
  with the warning naming it.
- The fallback's cap (v7.2 code review #6): a call the Gemini fallback takes for a voice call runs at the cap of the
  fallback's own model — `REPLY_THINK_MAX_TOKENS` when its reply model is a thinking model (3.1 Pro), the call's cap
  otherwise — so its thinking never eats the answer.
- Her fal photos (Seedream) and her кружечки (VEED) are unchanged: their keys live in their makers.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
On Claude her photos and clips are made as on Gemini, and her Gemini fallback answers whole.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] An agent on Claude with `photos` / `videos` has its photo and clip makers, on the Gemini client beside Claude.
- [ ] A Gemini fallback on a thinking model gets `REPLY_THINK_MAX_TOKENS`, on another model the call's cap.
- [ ] **Tests:** the makers built on Claude (a fake Gemini client; no `GEMINI_API_KEY` left in the environment), none
  without a key (the warning); the fallback's cap by its model; nothing reaches the network.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
