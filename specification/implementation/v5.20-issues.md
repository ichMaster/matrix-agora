# v5.20 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.20 — Кіра's layered memory of the private chat: by the hour, by four hours, by the week**
(version **v5 — Voice**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.20 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-170 →
**AGORA-171…175**).

Кіра keeps her private chat in layers: the last `HISTORY_N` (300) messages word for word on top, as now, and below
them summaries of the open talk older than that window — by the hour for 2 days, by four hours up to 2 weeks, by the
week after — each written once by Gemini 3.1 Pro from the lines open to it. It builds on the archive (v5.8), the marks
(v5.13–v5.15.4), the time lines in Gemini's history and the probe's cap (v5.19.0). The closed talk stays archived and
is never summarized for Gemini; a re-check reopens what Gemini does not block. Agora's talk is left for later.

**Reconciled with the code (2026-11-02):**
- the archive's schema is `SCHEMA` in `agents/archive.py`, run by `executescript` at every open, with `PRAGMA
  user_version` for one-off migrations — a new table needs only `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`;
- the prompt's sections are `build_instruction`'s keyword arguments in `agents/logic.py`, filled by
  `Agent.prompt_layers`; the probe keeps every one of them as a fixed layer (`PromptParts.layers`);
- the background work is spawned by `Agent.idle_watcher` (the reading, the reflection, the judge, the embeddings);
- a Pro call with thinking is `GeminiClient.think(…, model=…, kind=…, level=…)`; its usage line names the kind;
- capabilities and their needs are `agents/roster.py` `CAPABILITIES`, `PERSONA_ONLY` and `NEEDS`; agent settings are
  `agents/settings.py` `_s(…)` rows, scoped `cap:<capability>`;
- the panel's agent card reads `panel/memory.py` `memory_view(…, with_*)`; the drawer's tabs are in
  `panel/static/app.js` (`thoughtsTabHtml` and the rest);
- the offline re-check is the 2026-11-02 one-off (`sift.py`, groups of 20 halved through `GeminiClient.probe`),
  made a command.

### AGORA-171 — The store and what is due: the archive's `talk` table, the bands, the `talk` capability and its settings

**Description:**
The foundation: where the summaries live, which periods are due, and who has them. Touches `agents/archive.py`, a new
pure `agents/talk.py`, `agents/roster.py`, `agents/kira.toml`, `agents/settings.py` and `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The table** in `SCHEMA` (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS talk`): `id`, `level` (`hour` | `4h` | `week`), `start_ms`,
  `end_ms`, `text`, `open_lines`, `closed_lines`, `left_out` (lines a block kept out), `made_ms`; `UNIQUE(level,
  start_ms)`. The file stays 0600. A row is written once and never rewritten.
- **`Archive` methods** (no text in a log):
  - `private_between(start_ms, end_ms, gemini=True)`: the private lines of a period, oldest first;
  - `private_counts(start_ms, end_ms)`: (open, closed) by `GEMINI_CLOSED`;
  - `talk_put(level, start_ms, end_ms, text, open_lines, closed_lines, left_out, made_ms)` (a held period is a
    no-op) and `talk_rows(level=None, since_ms=None, until_ms=None)`.
- **What is due, pure** (`agents/talk.py`, over plain data, the clock injected): `periods_due(now, tz, hours_open,
  held, hour_days, fourh_days)` → `[(level, start_ms, end_ms)]`, oldest first:
  - `hour`: each past local hour of the last `hour_days` days with at least one open private line;
  - `4h`: each local four-hour block (00, 04, 08, 12, 16, 20) older than `hour_days` and within `fourh_days`, with an
    open line in it;
  - `week`: each local week (Monday 00:00) wholly older than `fourh_days`, with an open line in it;
  - a period already held, or not over yet, is never due; the edges are local, so a DST day has its true length.
- **The capability** `talk` in `CAPABILITIES`, `PERSONA_ONLY`, needing `recall` and `private` (`NEEDS`); `talk = true`
  in `agents/kira.toml`.
- **The settings** (`cap:talk`, her agent settings): `TALK_HOUR_DAYS` (2, 1–7), `TALK_4H_DAYS` (14, 3–60),
  `TALK_WORDS_HOUR` (60), `TALK_WORDS_4H` (100), `TALK_WORDS_WEEK` (150) (words, 20–400).
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Memory (the layers, in a paragraph of their own), §Contracts (the `talk` table, the capability and
  its needs), §Configuration (the five settings).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Her archive has a `talk` table, a pure function says which hours, blocks and weeks are due, and only a person with
`talk` (Кіра) has them.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `talk` is created in an existing archive without touching `lines`; `talk_put` twice is one row
- [ ] `periods_due` gives the bands' periods oldest first, never an hour with no open line, a held one or one not over
- [ ] **Tests:** `periods_due` at the bands' edges, across midnight and a DST switch (Europe/Kyiv, 2026-10-25), with
      held periods; the archive's methods (counts by mark, `gemini=True` never a closed line); the roster's needs
      (`talk` without `recall` or `private` refused); the settings' bounds
- [ ] **Contract:** the `talk` table and the capability in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, pinned by `tests/test_talk.py` and
      the roster test
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests

### AGORA-172 — The writer: Gemini 3.1 Pro summarizes each period due, the backfill, the folding, a blocked period halved

**Description:**
The idle watcher writes the periods due, one at a time, on `GEMINI_REPLY_MODEL` (Gemini 3.1 Pro). Touches
`agents/talk.py` (the pure request builders), `agents/agent.py` (`ensure_talk`, the idle watcher) and
`specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The requests, pure:** `talk_request(level, name, canon, lines | summaries, max_words, when)` → (system, contents):
  - an hour from its open lines (`Ich`/her name: text);
  - a four-hour block from its hours' summaries, or from its open lines when it has no hour rows (the backfill of old
    days);
  - a week from its blocks' summaries, or from its lines;
  - first person, her memory of the talk with Ich: what was said, decided, promised, how it felt; never a date or an
    hour; never a word that she is a model or a bot (the persona rule).
- **`Agent.ensure_talk(now)`**, spawned by the idle watcher when `talk` is on and one is due (a lock; a failure backs
  off 10 minutes): the oldest period due; its open lines (`gemini=True`) and counts; one `think(…, model=reply_model,
  kind="talk_summary", level="low")`; the text capped at the level's words; `talk_put`. The log: the level, the
  period's start as an ISO hour, the counts — never a text.
- **A blocked call** (`LAST_BLOCK` at the prompt stage, or None): the period's lines are tried again in two halves, each
  summarized alone and joined; a half that still blocks is left out and its lines counted in `left_out`. With nothing
  left, the row is written with an empty text, so the period is never asked again.
- **The backfill:** the first ticks after the start write what is due from the archive's open lines (oldest first, one
  a tick), so the first days of the bands fill within the hour.
- **The folding:** an hour past `TALK_HOUR_DAYS` is summarized into its block once the block is due; a block past
  `TALK_4H_DAYS` into its week. The finer rows stay.
- **Usage:** kind `talk_summary`; the report and the analytics see it as any kind.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Memory (the writer, the block rule, the backfill).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-171

**Expected result:**
Within the hour after the start, her hours of the last 2 days, her blocks of the last 2 weeks and her weeks before
them are written from the open talk, and every new hour follows once it is over.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One period a tick, oldest first; a held one never again; a failure backs off
- [ ] A closed line never reaches a summary's request
- [ ] A blocked period is halved, what still blocks is left out and counted, and an empty row ends it
- [ ] **Tests:** the request builders (first person, no date or hour, no «model»/«bot» words, the word cap); the
      backfill over a made-up archive with an injected clock and a fake `think`; the folding (the block from its hours,
      the week from its blocks, the finer rows kept); a blocked half; the usage kind; no text in the log
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests
