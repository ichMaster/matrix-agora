# v5.21 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.21 — Blocked for good: the backup without the judge, its talk gone after a day** (version
**v5 — Voice**). They are derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.21 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-176 →
**AGORA-177…182**).

**What the phase does:**
- One closed mark, `blocked`, for good. Whatever a Gemini block touches, and everything the backup says or answers,
  is `blocked`. Only the backup reads it, as its history, and its text is emptied after a day.
- The judge and the probe go.
- The route has two modes: Gemini, and the backup for 5 minutes after a block, or for an hour after more than 10 blocks
  in an hour.
- The owner switches by hand: `/gemini` and `/backup`. A backup line shows when the backup mode ends.

It builds on the backup (v5.14), the judge (v5.15), the probe (v5.16) and the layered memory (v5.20), and it removes
or replaces their parts.

### AGORA-177 — The mark `blocked`, for good: the archive, the facts and the migration

**Description:**
One closed mark, `blocked`, that nothing turns back. It covers the archive's lines and her facts, plus a one-off
migration of the old closed marks. Touches `agents/archive.py`, `agents/facts.py`, `agents/agent.py`
(`seed_private_from_archive`), ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and CLAUDE.md.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The archive (`agents/archive.py`):**
  - `MARKS` gains `blocked`.
  - `GEMINI_CLOSED` and `BACKUP_MARKS` become `("blocked", "waiting", "censored", "reopened")`. The three old ones stay
    closed in case a row was missed.
- **`Archive.mark()`:**
  - writes only `blocked`;
  - never changes a line already `blocked` (`… WHERE key IN (…) AND mark != 'blocked'`);
  - still drops the vectors of what it closes.

  `censor()` writes `blocked`.
- **The migration:** `SCHEMA_VERSION` 2 runs once, at open, and moves every `waiting`, `censored` and `reopened` line to
  `blocked`. `clean` and `gemini` stay as they are, open (the owner).
- **The facts (`agents/facts.py`):**
  - `FACT_MARKS`, `CLOSED_MARKS` and `BACKUP_FACT_MARKS` gain `blocked`;
  - `set_mark()` writes only `blocked` and never changes a `blocked` fact;
  - a one-off `migrate_marks(folder)`, run at her start, moves `waiting`, `censored` and `reopened` facts to `blocked`
    and returns the count. Fact file names are slugs of the text and are never printed.
- **`seed_private_from_archive`** skips a row with an empty text, which the deletion leaves (AGORA-181).
- **ARCHITECTURE §Contracts** (the archive's marks, the facts' `mark:`): the marks are `gemini`, `clean` (old rows
  only, open), `blocked` (closed for good) and the old closed marks.
  - **Rollback:** never deploy an image older than v5.21 over a migrated archive. Older code reads `blocked` as open.
- **CLAUDE.md** «Never read the backup's talk»: the marks named are `blocked`, and the old `waiting`, `censored` and
  `reopened`.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Every closed line and fact is `blocked`, and no code path can turn it into another mark.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `mark()` and `set_mark()` refuse every mark but `blocked` and never change a `blocked` row or fact.
- [ ] An archive at `user_version` 1 opens at 2: its `waiting`, `censored` and `reopened` lines become `blocked`, while
  `clean` and `gemini` are untouched. A second open changes nothing.
- [ ] The facts migration moves the three old closed marks to `blocked`, and a second run moves nothing.
- [ ] Gemini's readers (`latest(gemini=True)`, `private_between`, the recall and `unembedded`) never return a `blocked`
  line.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_archive.py` (marks, the migration, the readers) and `tests/test_facts.py` (marks, the
  migration). Made-up lines only.
- [ ] **Contract:** the marks in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, pinned by the tests above, and the rollback note.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-178 — The route: two modes, the hour after more than 10 blocks, `/gemini` and `/backup`, the mark with its end

**Description:**
`agents/route.py` stays pure, with an injected clock, and holds the new rules:
- Gemini, or the backup;
- 5 minutes after a block, or an hour after more than 10 blocks in the last 60 minutes;
- `/backup` until `/gemini`.

It also produces the text of the backup mark and of `/status`. Touches `agents/route.py`, `agents/settings.py` and
ARCHITECTURE §Contracts (the route file, the settings).

**What needs to be done:**
- **`state(route, now_ms)`** returns `gemini` or `backup`:
  - `backup` under the pin `backup`, or while `window_until_ms` is ahead;
  - `gemini` otherwise, with no `window_end` state any more.
- **`after(…)`, the outcomes:**
  - **`block`:** `blocks` gains `now`. If more than `hour_max` blocks fall in the last 60 minutes, the window runs until
    `now + lock_s`; otherwise until `now + window_s`. The later end wins. Under the pin `backup` there is no window and
    the block is not counted.
  - **`gemini` / `backup`:** today's answers are counted.
  - **`pin:gemini`:** the pin becomes `auto`, the window ends, and `blocks` is emptied. The owner: `/gemini` resets the
    hour's count.
  - **`pin:backup`:** the pin `backup`, until `/gemini`.
  - **Gone:** `rated`, `flagged`, `probed`, the day cap and `pinned_blocks`. A saved file that still has them loads
    with them ignored.
  - **An old pin `gemini`** loads as `auto`.
- **`backup_mark(route, now_ms, tz)`** returns «⚙️ backup до HH:MM» (the window's end, in `tz`), or «⚙️ backup ·
  вручну» under the pin `backup`. `BACKUP_MARK_RE` matches either form, and the old «⚙️ backup», on a line of its own.
- **`status_text(…)`** shows:
  - the mode (gemini; backup until HH:MM; backup by hand until `/gemini`);
  - the blocks in the last hour, out of `hour_max`;
  - her `blocked` private lines;
  - today's answers through Gemini and through the backup.

  There is no judge line, and never a text.
- **The settings (`agents/settings.py`):**
  - `FALLBACK_HOUR_MAX` (10, 1–50) and `FALLBACK_LOCK_S` (3,600, 300–14,400) come in, scope `cap:fallback`;
  - `FALLBACK_DAY_MAX` goes;
  - `FALLBACK_HISTORY_N` defaults to 300.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-177

**Expected result:**
The route's whole behaviour is one pure module, tested without the agent.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A block opens 5 minutes. The 11th block in 60 minutes opens an hour from that block. After the window, `state`
  is `gemini`.
- [ ] `pin:gemini` ends either window and empties the count. `pin:backup` holds until `pin:gemini`. There is no day
  cap.
- [ ] An old file with a `gemini` pin, `judged`, `probed` and `pinned_blocks` loads as `auto` with those ignored.
- [ ] `backup_mark` names the end of the 5 minutes or of the hour in `TIMEZONE` (a DST day included), or «вручну».
  `BACKUP_MARK_RE` strips all three forms.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_route.py`, rewritten for the new rules: the injected clock, the edges at 60 minutes and
  at 10/11 blocks.
- [ ] **Contract:** the route file's fields and the settings in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and §Configuration, pinned by
  `tests/test_route.py` and the settings test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-181 — The daily deletion at 05:00, `/purge`, and the re-check gone

**Description:**
Once a day at 05:00 local time, every `blocked` line and fact older than 24 hours loses its text for good. `/purge`
does it to every `blocked` at once. The v5.20 re-check goes. Touches `agents/archive.py`, `agents/facts.py`,
`agents/agent.py` (the idle watcher, `purge_backup_talk`), `agents/talk.py`, CLAUDE.md and ARCHITECTURE.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`deletion_due(now, last_run_day, tz, hour=5)`**, pure: true once a day, at or after 05:00, when today's run has not
  happened. The last run is kept in her route file or a small state file (0600).
- **`Archive.empty_blocked(before_ms)`:**
  - the rows `blocked` (or an old closed mark) older than the cut keep their key, time, place, speaker and mark;
  - their text becomes `''` and their FTS entry is deleted;
  - it returns the count.
  - The rows stay, so a Matrix private line re-read at a start (`append` by key) is never added again as open.
- **The facts:** `blocked` facts older than 24 hours (their `added` date, or the file's time) are deleted as files.
- **The agent:** her in-memory history drops the emptied lines, and the log holds counts only.
- **`/purge`:** `purge_backup_talk` empties every `blocked` line, whatever its age, by the same function. The counts
  answer and the confirmation stay as they are.
- **The re-check goes:** `agents/talk.py` `sift`, `RECHECK_GROUP` and `main`, plus its tests.
- **v5.20 review #4:** after this phase only a block's own newest lines are closed, and their hour is not yet
  summarized. This is noted in ARCHITECTURE §Memory. A line closed by hand drops its period's summaries by hand.
- **CLAUDE.md and ARCHITECTURE:** «nothing lived is deleted» gains this exception: the daily emptying of `blocked` lines
  older than 24 hours, the owner's decision of 2026-11-02, besides `/purge`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-177

**Expected result:**
No `blocked` line or fact older than a day keeps its text after the morning's run.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `deletion_due` is true once a day from 05:00 and false again after the run (a DST day included).
- [ ] `empty_blocked` empties exactly the rows over 24 hours old. It keeps their keys, so `append` of the same key
  returns False and the line stays `blocked`. The word search no longer finds them.

- [ ] `python -m agents.talk recheck` no longer exists.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_archive.py`, `tests/test_facts.py`, a watcher test with an injected clock, and
  `tests/test_talk.py` without the re-check. Counts only in the log.
- [ ] **Contract:** the archive's rows that are kept emptied, in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, pinned by the tests above.

- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
