### AGORA-011 (#11) — live run, with three fixes surfaced
1. **Pending invites were never joined**: the callback sees only new events; `join_pending_invites()` now
   walks `invited_rooms` once after the first sync.
2. **Echo-storm prevention**: two echo bots would echo each other forever (turn-taking lands in v1.2);
   `should_echo()` limits v0.5 echoes to the owner. Pinned by tests.
3. **Continuwuity rejects nio's body-less `/join`** (`M_BAD_JSON`), and the failure was silent;
   `join_room()` posts to `/rooms/{id}/join` with a `{}` body and checks the status.

Live DoD: both echoes confirmed by the owner; restart → same device (`devices` API: one device per bot),
no history replay; a foreign invite logged `ignored` and left (pending invites cleared); the trap room
cleaned up.

**Architecture impact:** Continuwuity requires JSON bodies on POSTs nio sends body-less — `join_room()` is
the pattern for any such endpoint (watch for it in v1+). The v0.5 reply policy is owner-only by design;
v1.2 replaces it with turn-taking.
