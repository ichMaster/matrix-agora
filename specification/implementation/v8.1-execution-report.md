### AGORA-266: the bridge
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 4a6441e · **GitHub:** #266
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **`agents/bridge.py`:** `Bridge(Agent)` keeps the room's rules. Its own parts are:
  - **The queue (observe):** 3 s of quiet, 20 lines, or before a turn; at most 200 lines a call; resent whole after
    10, 30 and 60 s; `422` dropped; `401`/`403` stop every call; `404` waits for the space.
  - **The backlog:** today's lines since the last one heard, after a restart.
  - **The turn:** the trigger alone, posted exactly as returned; every silence status is silence.
  - **The retract:** after the persona drop and after her own 60 s timeout.
  - **The rest:** health every 60 s, `state/lili.bridge.json`, and `LUMI_ENV_FILE` read in-process.
- **`agents/agent.py`:**

  - the `lumi-http` branch, with no model of ours;
  - `main()` dispatches a bridge.
- **`agents/usage.py`:** `lumi_usage_line` (`engine` `lumi`, `billing` `external`).
