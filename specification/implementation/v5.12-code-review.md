## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A failed call consumes the reading's items and the reflection's lines
`agents/agent.py` (`_reading_tick`, `_reflect_tick`, `_think`)

**The reading.** It marks every shown item seen *before* the pick call, and `parse_picks(None)` is no pick. A failed
pick call therefore uses up the period and the items: they are never shown again, and the next reading is three
hours away.
- **Seen live:** on 2026-10-28 at 11:47, during the shared key's outage, «reading: 36 items shown, 0 picked».

**The reflection.** `_think` gives `[]` both for a failed call and for a dropped text, and the place's mark moves
either way. A failed call therefore loses those lines for good: the next reflection starts after them.

**Fix:**
- The reading marks the items seen only after the pick call answered.
- A failed pick restores the previous reading time and backs off 10 minutes, so the reading is tried again soon.
- `_think` returns None for a failed call. The reflection then keeps the place's mark, so the same lines are read
  next time.
- A text dropped for a banned word still moves the mark, as before.

### 2. 🟠 A private reflection can reach Agora
`agents/agent.py` (`_reading_tick`, `_reflect_tick`, `_recent_thoughts`)

Some calls write a thought that Agora sees (place `thought`): a news thought and a reflection on Agora. Their context
still holds her private thoughts:
- the news thought's recall searches `thought` and `thought_private`;
- the Agora reflection's «Що ти останнім часом думала» holds her last five thoughts of any place.

**Failure scenario:**
1. A reflection on the private chat («Ich сьогодні був тихий, я чекала більшого») is among her recent thoughts.
2. The Agora reflection picks up its gist and writes a `thought`.
3. That thought reaches her Agora prompt («Що ти сьогодні думала»), Agora's recall and her day memory.

This breaks the phase's DoD («a reflection on the private chat never comes up in Agora») and the discretion rule.

**Fix:**
- A call whose thought Agora sees gets only her `thought` place: the recent thoughts and the recall alike.
- Only a reflection on the private chat sees every place.

### 3. 🟠 A link in her line is read aloud by her voice
`agents/emotion.py` (`spoken`), `agents/agent.py` (`send_telegram`, `send_line`)

Since AGORA-135, a line she writes first may share a news item with its link, and her private prompt carries the
links. Every Telegram line is a voice note first.
- **What reaches the voice:** the whole line. ElevenLabs reads «https://zaxid.net/news/…» aloud, and the URL's
  characters count against the speakers' monthly cap.

**Fix:**
- `spoken` takes every `http(s)://` link out of what the synthesiser gets. The text keeps it.
- A line that is only a link is not voiceable: it goes out as text alone.

### 4. 🟡 A redirect from a Zaxid.net page
`agents/citynews.py` (`http_text`), `agents/reading.py` (`Sources.full_text`)

`zaxid_link_ok` checks the link it fetches, but `urllib` follows a redirect to any host. The links come from Zaxid.net's
own feed, and only a JSON-LD body is read from the answer.
- **Fix:** a handler that refuses a redirect off zaxid.net.
- **Home:** backlog.

## What's solid

- **The places hold where they are coded.**
  - Agora's recall searches `agora` and `thought` only.
  - The Agora prompt's block leaves out `thought_private` and every link.
  - The day memory never gets a private reflection.
  - The move `thought` counts only her `thought` place.
- **Nothing changes for the others.**
  - A member without `thoughts` gets no block and no recall place.
  - Its move falls back to a plain answer, so its draws are unchanged (pinned).
  - Its recall title is unchanged, and its key stays where it was: only a person with `thoughts` takes the Guardian
    key out of the environment.
- **Off the reply path.** The reading and the reflection are spawned by the idle watcher. A test holds a reflection's
  call open while an answer goes out.
- **Restart-safe.** The reading time is saved before the calls, and the state file is loaded, changed and saved with
  no await between.
- **Defensive sources.**
  - Only an https page on zaxid.net is fetched.
  - A broken JSON-LD block is skipped.
  - The Guardian's errors are logged by their type, never with the key.
  - The network fixture guards every default fetcher.
- **No marker in a text.** A generated text or title never carries a comment marker. This closes v5.11 review #6's
  risk for the thoughts' file.
- **The panel** escapes every text, links only https with `noopener noreferrer`, and degrades to «Thoughts
  unreadable».
