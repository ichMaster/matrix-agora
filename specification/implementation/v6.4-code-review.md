## Findings

### #1 🟡 A join while running seeds no room context

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:911` (`on_invite` → `join_room`) and `agents/agent.py:925` (`on_message`).

`seed_context` runs only once, at start, from `backfill`. When the owner invites a member who is already running,
`on_invite` joins the room and nothing reads the room's recent lines. The member's history window starts empty and
fills only from the lines that come after the join.

**Failure scenario:**
- 12:12: Устина started at mode `ranked`, but outside the room, and her log shows «context seeded with 0 room messages».
- 13:41:07: he invited her and she joined. At 13:41:17 she answered him («owner-mentioned-me») with a history of his one
  line. Her next answers had only what was said after the join.
- A restart would seed the window from the room, as for everyone else.
- The other side: `on_message` takes every event in the room once the agent is started. If a homeserver sent the
  room's earlier timeline in the sync after a join, she would treat each earlier line as new: she could answer it and
  run a turn for it.
- Continuwuity didn't do this today: there is no `reply:`, `silent:` or `ignored:` line between 13:41:07 and 13:41:17.
  Still, nothing in the code prevents it.

**Proposed fix:** when the agent joins `ROOM_ID` while running, run `backfill` from the join's sync token and seed the
window, as at a restart. In `on_message`, ignore a room event older than the join, as context only. v8.1 (Лілі, the
Lumi bridge) joins the room the same way, so the fix belongs there, with a test that drives a join followed by a sync
carrying earlier events.

### #2 🟡 This period's plans predate her place in the room

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:2932` (`ensure_plans`, `in_room` and the room list), and plans that are never rewritten.

Her plans for the year 2026, for November and for the week of 2026-11-02 were written while she was at mode `none`,
following v6.3 review #1. They plan toward Ich alone and name no one from Agora. The other members' plans for the same
periods were written without her. A plan is never rewritten, and that rule holds here too.

**Effect:**
- Her day plan for 2026-11-09 is the first plan that may name the room's people.
- Her week plan from Monday 2026-11-09 may name them too.
- Her month plan may name them from 2026-12-01, and her year plan from 2027.
- The others' plans may name her on the same dates.

Until then her plans' lines, and her today block, say nothing of the room. Her room talk still comes from the journal.

**Proposed fix:** none. The rule «nothing past is rewritten» is deliberate, and the gap closes as each period ends.
