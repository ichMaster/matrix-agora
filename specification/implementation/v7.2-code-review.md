## Findings

### 1. 🟠 Hermes never goes warm — `agents/herald.py` `_think`, `agents/digest.py` `digest_request`

His system is `canon + brief`, and the digest's brief names the period, the count and the target; `PROMPT_SLOW =
system` therefore changes every digest: every digest runs cold and starts a CLI that the next digest closes unused.
**Fix:** his slow part is his canon — the prefix of every digest's and every answer's system.

### 2. 🟡 `UnboundLocalError` hides the real error — `agents/claude_sdk.py` `_query`

If `make_options(...)` raises, `finally` reads `key` before it is assigned. **Fix:** `key = None` before the `try`.

### 3. 🟡 A CLI started for nothing — `_query`'s `finally`

After a call that found the client blocked (an API-key source) or muted, `_prewarm` still starts a CLI. **Fix:** no
prewarm while the client is blocked or muted.

### 4. 🟡 A disconnect cut short — `WarmCli._run`

`wait_for(client.disconnect(), 10)` cancels the SDK's close mid-way (its own bound is ~5 s after stdin, then SIGTERM
and 5 s, then SIGKILL), so a CLI that ignores both may outlive the worker. **Fix:** 25 s.

### 5. 🟡 A taken CLI left at the gate — `_query`

`_take_warm` ran before `async with self._voice_gate`; a call cancelled at the gate leaves the taken CLI connected,
out of `close()`'s reach. **Fix:** take it inside the gate.

### 6. 🟡 The fallback's cap — DEFER → v7.3

The Gemini fallback is called with the Claude reply's cap (`REPLY_MAX_TOKENS`, 200); on `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` its
thinking counts in it. The room's Gemini reply model is Flash (checked, no value printed), so it does not bite now;
Кіра's fallback is 3.1 Pro — v7.3 sets the fallback's cap from its own model.

### 7. 🟡 An idle CLI kept all night — `WarmCli`

`stale()` is checked only by the next call. **Fix:** the worker waits for its call at most `WARM_IDLE_S`, then ends.

## What's solid

- Two calls never share one CLI: `_take_warm` / `_prewarm` are synchronous and clear `_warm`, `WarmCli.query` refuses
  a second call.
- A warm call's timeout or early break cancels its worker (`answer()`'s `finally`).
- The warm message is the rest and the transcript; `PROMPT_SLOW` is never stale (identity, then the prefix check).
- The key's cap, effort and thinking are the same on both paths; the v7.1 checks hold on the warm path (the same
  stream-json protocol and parser; in streaming mode no `ResultError`, which `_judge` handles the same).
- Usage line v9: all eight builders write `warm`; `_valid` rejects a non-bool.
- Compose (Hermes's env order), the Files API, the models view and the JS.
