### AGORA-242 — The prompt's slow part: canon and life, told to the call

**Description:**
A voice call's prompt in two parts — its slow part (the canon and the life section, the prefix `build_instruction`
starts with) and the rest — told to the client beside the whole, so that `ClaudeClient` can give the slow part to a
CLI started ahead. Touches `agents/agent.py` (`build_prompt`, `_compose`), `agents/usage.py` (a context variable
beside `PROMPT_PARTS`), `agents/herald.py` (Hermes's brief is his slow part), `agents/claude_sdk.py` (a pure split).

**What needs to be done:**
- `build_prompt` records the slow part of the prompt it built: the canon, and the life section when there is one —
  exactly the prefix of the instruction (`build_instruction` joins `canon`, then `life`, with `\n\n`); an assistant's
  brief has none. Recorded with the prompt as `_LAST_PARTS` is (the prompt it belongs to, the prefix).
- `_compose` sets it for the call in a context variable (`PROMPT_SLOW`, `agents/usage.py`, beside `PROMPT_PARTS`)
  when the call's prompt is the one last built in this task, and resets it after; any other call has none.
- Hermes: his digest's and his answers' brief (the system of `Herald._think`) is the whole slow part; his contents
  are the rest.
- `split_prompt(system, slow) -> tuple[str, str] | None` (pure, `agents/claude_sdk.py`): `(slow, rest)` when `system`
  starts with `slow`, else None; the rest without the joining blank line.
- Gemini's calls unchanged: the whole prompt, the same order.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Every voice call of a persona, the cat and Hermes carries its slow part; the slow part and the rest join back to the
whole prompt.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The slow part is the canon plus the life section, a prefix of the prompt, for a persona and the cat; Hermes's
  brief is his whole slow part; an assistant's prompt has none.
- [ ] The context variable is set only around the call whose prompt was built last in its task, and reset after.
- [ ] **Tests:** `split_prompt` (a prefix, not a prefix, an empty slow part); for a persona and the cat with and
  without a life section, `slow + "\n\n" + rest == prompt` and the parts' set unchanged; the variable is None outside
  the call and on a call with another prompt; Hermes's think call carries his brief. nio and Gemini mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** ARCHITECTURE §Contracts (prompt order) gains the slow part — the canon and the life section, the
  prefix — and §The engine per agent names `PROMPT_SLOW`; pinned by the new tests.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-243 — The answer from a CLI started ahead: one `ClaudeSDKClient` a call

**Description:**
`ClaudeClient` (`agents/claude_sdk.py`) gives a voice call (`VOICE_KINDS`) to a `ClaudeSDKClient` connected ahead —
its system prompt the call's slow part, the rest of the prompt before the transcript in the message — which takes
exactly that one call and is closed; the next one is started in the background. Measured 2026-11-10/11: 0.9 s a call
in an open CLI against 2.2–2.4 s with a new one; one long session is unsafe (a new `session_id` still knew the
previous call, `/clear` is named aloud by the model, `set_model` is ignored, the system prompt is read once at the
CLI's start).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The waiting process** — one per client: a worker task that owns its `ClaudeSDKClient` from `connect` to
  `disconnect` (the SDK's client must stay in the task that connected it), started with the key of the call it will
  take: the slow part, the model, the effort and thinking, the cap. It takes one request (the message and a future),
  answers it, disconnects and ends; a new worker is started for the next call's key.
- **Warm or cold:** a voice call whose `PROMPT_SLOW` splits its system (`split_prompt`) and whose key equals the
  waiting worker's goes warm — the rest of the instruction, a blank line, then the transcript as the message;
  anything else goes cold (`query()`, v7.1), and the next worker is started with this call's key. The background
  calls stay cold. A worker never takes two calls.
- **The checks of every call, unchanged:** the init's `apiKeySource` and tools (wherever the client yields the init),
  the rate-limit events and the overage mute, the error result (a refusal, a 429, read after `ResultError`), the
  decline and `LAST_BLOCK`, `LAST_SERVED`, the voice gate, the prompt file 0600 and removed after its process ends,
  `answer_of`. The same `_judge`.
- **Bounded:** a connect that hangs or fails, or a warm call that hangs, closes the worker and the call goes cold once
  (never silent for that reason); the call's timeout counts from the call, the connect ahead is not the call's.
- **Its numbers:** a log line names a call warm or cold (`claude reply warm`), never a text; the usage line's
  `latency_ms` from the call to its result; the usage line marks a warm call (usage line v9 `warm`: true on a warm
  Claude call, false on a cold one, null on every other line), and Analytics · Speed shows warm and cold apart.
- The idle worker's memory (~200 MB) noted in ARCHITECTURE; a worker idle past a bound (e.g. an hour, its cache's
  life) is closed and started again at the next call.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-242

**Expected result:**
A persona's second answer goes through a CLI already open, about 1.4 s sooner, with every check of v7.1 kept.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One call per worker: after its call it disconnects; a new one is started for the next key; never two calls in
  one process, never two workers' calls at once beyond the voice gate.
- [ ] A changed slow part, model, effort or cap goes cold and starts the next worker with the new key; the background
  calls go cold.
- [ ] A failed or hung connect, or a hung warm call, closes the worker and the call goes cold once.
- [ ] The init, rate-limit, error-result, decline and overage checks hold on a warm call as on a cold one.
- [ ] **Tests:** a fake `ClaudeSDKClient` (connect, query, receive_response, disconnect; scripts of messages; a hang;
  an exception) — warm on a matching key, cold on each kind of mismatch, one call per worker and the next started,
  the hung and failed connect, the checks (a bad `apiKeySource`, a refusal, a 429 after `ResultError`, overage), the
  prompt file removed, the log line's warm/cold with no text, the usage line's `warm`; Analytics' warm/cold split.
- [ ] **Contract:** usage line v9 `warm` (ARCHITECTURE §Token accounting; `tests/test_usage*.py` pins its builders and
  a line without it reading as null); §The engine per agent describes the waiting process.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
