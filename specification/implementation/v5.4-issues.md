# v5.4 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.4 — The Agora recording, on command** (version **v5 — Voice: Hermes, the agents hear and
speak**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.4 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-094 →
**AGORA-095…098**).

On «запис …» in his room, Hermes assembles one audio file of a stretch of Agora, each line in the voice of the one
who said it: the members' voice messages and the owner's voice messages as they are, the owner's typed lines in his
cloned voice, a member's text-only line in its own voice, his own short opening, time marks and closing. He sends
it to his room as an audio file.

**Owner decisions** (2026-10-20):
- on command only (typed or spoken);
- the owner's typed lines in his cloned voice `OWNER_VOICE_ID`;
- into Hermes's room, as an audio file.

**Reconciled with the code and the live setup (2026-10-20, read-only):**
- **The room's line formats over time:**
  - from v5.3's final format, a member's voiced line is a labelled voice message («Голосове повідомлення», no
    `filename`) followed by the same text from the same sender;
  - before that, for some hours on 2026-10-18: a voice with its words as the caption (`body` beside a different
    `filename`), sometimes followed by the text, sometimes not, and for minutes the text in a thread;
  - the owner's voice messages are Element's own (`m.audio` with the voice flag); Hermes's transcripts «🎙 Ich: …»
    reply to them.
- **Hermes's reading.** `fetch()` returns text `Message`s only, and members' captions since v5.3. The recording
  needs the raw audio events too (their `mxc` URLs and mimetypes), so it has its own reader over the same paging.
- **The pieces Hermes already has:**
  - `matrix.download` (authenticated, capped);
  - `ElevenLabsTTS` per voice id;
  - `voice.encode_opus` (PCM 24 kHz → OGG/Opus) and `voice.duration_ms`;
  - `speech.voice_message`;
  - his usage file and `month_chars`.
  ffmpeg is in the image.
- **The voices.** `ADA_VOICE_ID`, `BRUNO_VOICE_ID`, `KIT_VOICE_ID`, `CLAUDE_VOICE_ID` and `OWNER_VOICE_ID` are in
  the shared `server/.env`, which Hermes reads; `HERMES_VOICE_ID` is his own.
- **The budget.** Every TTS call of a recording is Hermes's own: his usage file, `HERMES_TTS_MONTHLY_CHARS`
  (150,000). "Each agent writes only its own usage file" stays true, and the speakers' budget is untouched.
- **The homeserver's upload limit** (`/_matrix/client/v1/media/config`) is **20,971,520 bytes**. At 32 kb/s an hour
  of OGG/Opus is about 14.4 MB, so a part is capped at 60 minutes.
- **The digests** of 2026-10-19 covered 28–72 messages per 4 h. A whole day is a few hundred lines, about 40–90
  minutes of audio.

## v5.4 — The Agora recording, on command

### AGORA-095 — The recording's command and plan (pure)

**Description:**
A new `agents/recording.py` with the pure decisions: the command, the period, and the list of pieces a stretch of
Agora becomes.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`recording_request(text, now_ms, tz) -> (start_ms, end_ms) | None`:** a short owner message (at most 6 words)
  holding «запис» / «записи» / «запиши».
  - «за вчора» → yesterday 00:00–24:00 local;
  - «з HH» or «з HH:MM» → today from then until now;
  - otherwise (incl. «за сьогодні») → today 00:00 until now.
  - Anything else is None.
- **`plan_recording(events, owner, herald, members) -> list[Piece]`:** the events oldest first, in the period. Each
  `Piece` is one of:
  - `audio` — mxc, mimetype, speaker;
  - `speak` — speaker, text;
  - `gap` — the hour of the next line.

  The mapping:
  - a member's voice message → `audio`. A member's text from the same sender within 30 s after it is its words,
    consumed; a captioned voice is its own words.
  - the owner's voice message → `audio`. Hermes's transcript «🎙 Ich: …» → left out.
  - the owner's typed line → `speak` (owner).
  - a member's text-only line → `speak` (that member). An action purr (`*…*`) → left out.
  - edits and any other sender → left out.
  - more than 15 minutes between two lines → a `gap` piece first.
- **`hour_words(hour)`:** «опівночі», «о першій» … «о двадцять третій». The opening line «Агора, 19 жовтня, з
  десятої до двадцять третьої.» and the closing come from code, with no model call.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Any stretch of the room is a plan of pieces, tested on plain data.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_recording.py`):
  - the command (yes, no, the three periods; DST-safe days);
  - the plan for every line kind, the pairing (and a voice with no text, and a text with no voice), the left-out
    ones, the gaps;
  - the hour words.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-096 — The assembly: pieces to PCM, loudness, pauses, parts, one OGG each

**Description:**
Turn a planned list of sounds into OGG/Opus files: one per part, each under the homeserver's upload limit. The file
is `agents/recording.py` (its async part).

**What needs to be done:**
- **`decode_to_pcm(audio, mime)`:** ffmpeg, fixed argv: any input → s16le 24 kHz mono, with `loudnorm` (I=-18,
  TP=-2) so a phone recording and an ElevenLabs voice sound equally loud.
  - The PCM from TTS goes through the same normaliser.
  - Failure → None (the piece is left out, logged by type).
- **`assemble(pcms, gaps) -> list[bytes]`:**
  - 0.5 s of silence between lines;
  - 1.5 s around a time mark;
  - a new part when a part would pass `PART_MAX_S` (3,600 s), cut only between lines;
  - each part through `encode_opus`.
- **Pure helpers:** `silence(seconds)` and the part split (`split_parts(durations, max_s)`).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-095

**Expected result:**
Pieces in, one or more OGG files out, each under an hour and so under 20 MiB.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (ffmpeg mocked; a real run skipped without ffmpeg):
  - the decode argv;
  - silences of the right length;
  - the split never cuts a line;
  - a failed piece is left out;
  - the parts are encoded.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-097 — Hermes records: the command in his room, the reading, the voices, the delivery

**Description:**
Hermes's side, in `agents/herald.py`: the command, typed or spoken, and the recording sent to his room.
ARCHITECTURE §The herald and VISION's non-goal note are updated.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`route`:** in his room, `recording_request` comes before «підсумок» and the chat. The same applies to a spoken
  command (v5.2's hearing).
- **`record(start, end)`** under the busy lock, typing on:
  1. Read Agora's raw events of the period: the same paging as `fetch`, bounded (`MAX_PAGES`), text and audio. An
     incomplete read means no recording, with a short line.
  2. `plan_recording`.
  3. Each `audio` piece: `matrix.download` (cap 20 MB).
  4. Each `speak` piece: TTS in the speaker's voice:
     - the owner's from `OWNER_VOICE_ID`;
     - a member's from `<NAME>_VOICE_ID`;
     - a gap's and the opening's and closing's from `HERMES_VOICE_ID`.
     All within Hermes's monthly budget: past it, a `speak` piece is left out. A `tts` usage line goes in his file.
  5. `assemble`.
  6. Upload each part as `m.audio`, not a voice message: «Агора 2026-10-19.ogg», or «… (1 з 2)», with
     `info.duration` and `size`.
  7. Then one text line: «Агора, 10:05–23:40 · 42 хв · 186 реплік (озвучено: 12)».
- **An empty period:** «У цей час Агора мовчала.» and no file.
- **Never a word or a sound in a log:** `recording: sent (N parts, M min, K lines, J spoken, L left out)`.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §The herald: the recording;
  - §Configuration: `OWNER_VOICE_ID`;
  - §Contracts: the env name.
- **VISION:** the raw-archive non-goal notes that the recording is assembled from the room on demand and nothing is
  kept but the sent file.
- **The env examples:** `OWNER_VOICE_ID=` (empty).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-095, AGORA-096

**Expected result:**
«запис за сьогодні» brings the day's Agora as audio in his room.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_herald.py`; nio, TTS, ffmpeg and the downloads mocked):
  - the command routes, typed and spoken;
  - the plan's pieces are fetched or spoken in the right voices;
  - the owner's voice is the cloned one;
  - the budget leaves pieces out;
  - the upload content (an audio file, not a voice);
  - parts;
  - an empty period;
  - a partial read;
  - no text in logs.
- [ ] **Contract:** `OWNER_VOICE_ID` in the env examples and ARCHITECTURE, pinned by `tests/test_compose.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
