### AGORA-274: the bridge speaks first
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** d0abfd2 · **GitHub:** #274
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **The loop:** `Bridge.initiative_loop` ticks every 15 s and polls at most every 60 s. `maybe_initiative` first pays
  the owed acks. It polls only when all of these hold:
  - Lumi is open;
  - `health.agora ≥ 2` (otherwise «no initiative: agora N», logged once);
  - no reply of hers is on its way;
  - `initiative_due` holds.
- **A `say`:**
  - One usage line, kind `initiative`, with Lumi's compose time.
  - Then the room's rules once more: the last line unchanged since the poll, her hours, the cap, the offer's age
    counted from the ask, no reply pending. Then the persona rule.
  - A drop is never posted, never acked, and logged by its reason only.
  - Otherwise the offer is posted exactly as written and acked with its event id.
- **The ack:**
  - An ack Lumi never got goes to `pending_acks` and is retried a minute apart, across a restart.
  - It is given up after a day. A 422 or an unreadable 200 is given up at once.
- **State:** `initiatives {day, posted, dropped}`, `last_poll {at_ms, outcome}`, `pending_acks`.
- **Usage:** the kind `initiative` joins `KINDS` and the panel's kind order.
- **The simulated day** (25 seeds):
  - never past the cap;
  - never inside a running wave (her line opens it);
  - never twice in a row;
  - never outside her hours.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - «Her initiative» moved from planned to built;
  - the initiators;
  - the bridge's state row;
  - the usage kind;
  - the turn-taking contract.
