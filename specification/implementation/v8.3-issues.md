## v8.3 — Лілі speaks when she wants

### AGORA-273 — The `initiative` capability, the API v2 client and the room's rules as pure decisions

**Description:**
The foundation: the roster lets a bridge hold one initiative of the room's, the Lumi client speaks the two new routes of
API v2, and the room's rules for her initiative are one pure function. Touches `agents/roster.py`,
`agents/lumi_api.py`, `agents/nudge.py`, `agents/settings.py`, `tests/fake_lumi.py`, the env examples and their tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The roster:**
  - a capability `initiative`, the bridge type's alone (`BRIDGE_ONLY`), on by default for `bridge`
    (`TYPES["bridge"] = {"initiative"}`), switchable off in a bridge's TOML;
  - v8.1's «a bridge takes no capability» becomes «a bridge takes no capability but `initiative`»;
  - `INITIATIVES = ("nudge", "news", "initiative")`, so `SINGLES` keeps one bridge per room with it.
- **The client (`agents/lumi_api.py`):**
  - `INITIATIVE_API = 2`: the `agora` version from which she may offer; `API_VERSION` (1) stays the minimum for
    turns and hearing.
  - `LumiClient.initiative()` → `GET /v1/initiative` → `InitiativeResult(outcome, initiative_id, text, stats, wall_ms,
    status)`, with `classify_initiative` pure:
    - `say` (a non-empty `text` and a URL-safe `initiative_id`) or `none`;
    - every `silence` status is `none`;
    - `401`/`403`/`404` are `refused:<name>`, and a timeout, `unreachable` and a body off the contract are named too.
  - `LumiClient.posted(initiative_id, event_id)` → `POST /v1/initiative/{id}/posted {event_id}`, with the id
    URL-encoded → `{stored}` as given, or None when unreadable. It is never retried inside the call.
  - Kept from a response: `outcome`, `initiative_id`, `text`, `stats` alone. Never in a log, a `repr` or an
    exception: her text, the token.
- **The room's rules (`agents/nudge.py`), pure:**
  - `initiative_due(now_ms, local_now, last_room_ms, last_is_hers, sent_today, last_poll_ms, idle_ms, per_day, hours,
    poll_gap_ms)` holds only when all of these hold:
    - in `hours`;
    - under `per_day`;
    - the room quiet for `idle_ms`;
    - her own line not the room's last (purrs are transparent: a purr is nobody between);
    - the last poll at least `poll_gap_ms` ago.
  - `idle_ms` is never under `BOT_WINDOW_S`, so her line opens a fresh wave.
- **The settings (`agents/settings.py`, service `matrix-chat`, scope `cap:initiative`, a new group «Лілі в кімнаті»):**
  - `LILI_INITIATIVE_IDLE_S` 1800 (600–86400 s);
  - `LILI_INITIATIVE_HOURS` `09-23`;
  - `LILI_INITIATIVE_PER_DAY` 4 (0–48; 0 is off).

  `concerns` lets a bridge read the shared settings and the ones of the capabilities it holds. They are read only by a
  member with `initiative` (a typo never takes the room down, as with the cat's). They go in `.env.example` and
  `server/.env.example` beside the cat's.
- **The fake Lumi (`tests/fake_lumi.py`):**
  - `GET /v1/initiative` answers the contract's `initiative` or `initiative_none` example, scripted per poll (a
    status, a body of the test's own, a delay past the timeout);
  - `POST …/posted` checks the body's keys and the URL-encoding, and stores idempotently by `event_id`
    (`stored: true` once, then `false`; an unknown id `false`).

**Dependencies:** None.

**Expected result:**
Every piece the bridge's initiative needs exists and is tested on plain data: who may hold it, how she is asked, and
when.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A bridge loads with `initiative` (by default) or without it, and with no other capability. A second member with `initiative` in a room is a roster error. `initiative` on any other type is refused.
- [ ] `classify_initiative` names every outcome. The client sends exactly the contract's requests (the fake checks them) and keeps nothing beyond `outcome`, `initiative_id`, `text` and `stats`.
- [ ] `initiative_due` covers every case: each of the room's rules alone stops it, an empty room counts as quiet, the hours wrap past midnight, and a purr after her line is transparent.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_roster.py` (the capability);
  - `tests/test_lumi_api.py` (the two routes against the fake; `classify_initiative`; no text in a `repr`);
  - `tests/test_nudge.py` (`initiative_due`);
  - `tests/test_settings.py` (the three settings, their scope and group; a bridge concerns them);
  - the env-example pins.
- [ ] **Contract:** the TOML schema (a bridge's `initiative`), the client's protocol (API v2's initiative routes) and the settings' names. ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, §The bridge and the env table; pinned by the tests above and `tests/test_lumi_contract.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests.

### AGORA-274 — The bridge speaks first: the poll, the post, the ack, the state and the usage line

**Description:**
The bridge's own initiative loop. While the room's rules hold it asks Lumi whether she wants to say something, posts a
fresh offer exactly as given, and acks it with the line's event id. Touches `agents/bridge.py` and `agents/usage.py`,
with ARCHITECTURE's «Her initiative» moved from planned to built.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The loop (`initiative_loop`, started in `Bridge.run` beside the health and flush loops; a tick every
  `INITIATIVE_TICK_S` 15 s, a poll at most every `INITIATIVE_POLL_S` 60 s).** A tick asks Lumi only when every one of
  these holds:
  - the member has `initiative`;
  - Lumi is open (`_lumi_closed()` is None) and `health.agora ≥ 2`. Below that she offers nothing, said once by name
    («no initiative: agora 1»);
  - no reply of hers is on its way (`_reply_pending`) and no initiative is in progress;
  - `initiative_due` over the room's timeline and her state. The silence counts from the later of the room's last
    line and the bridge's start (a restart never asks at once, as the cat's review #9).
- **A `say`:**
  - Before posting, the room's rules are checked again:
    - the room's last line is the one it was at the poll;
    - still in the hours and under the cap;
    - the offer no older than `initiative_fresh_s` since it came (1800, the contract's `limits`);
    - the persona rule (`outs_a_persona` over the room's personas).
  - Any of them failing → the offer is dropped: never posted and never acked, so Lumi remembers nothing of it. It is
    logged by its reason, never its text.
  - Otherwise it is posted exactly as offered (`send_line`, `m.text`): no trimming, no `clean_reply`, no PASS handling.
- **The ack:**
  - The posted line's event id goes to `posted(initiative_id, event_id)`.
  - A failed ack (unreachable, a timeout, unreadable) is kept: `pending_acks` in the state, ids only. Each later tick
    tries it again, until Lumi answers or `initiative_kept_s` (86400) has passed, then it is given up by name.
  - `stored: false` is logged; it is never an error.
- **Statuses:**
  - `none` and every silence status: nothing;
  - `401`/`403`: refused for every call until a restart, as on a turn;
  - `404`: the space off.
- **The state (`state/<name>.bridge.json`, no text):**
  - `initiatives {day, posted, dropped}`, reset at the room's midnight (`TIMEZONE`);
  - `last_poll {at_ms, outcome}`, where the outcome is `none`, `posted`, `dropped:<why>` or the status;
  - `pending_acks [{initiative_id, event_id, at_ms}]`.

  `sent_today` for the rules is `initiatives.posted`, so a restart never exceeds the cap.
- **Usage:**
  - Every `say` received writes one usage line: `lumi_usage_line(…, kind="initiative")`, from her `stats` (her compose
    is spent whether or not the room sees it), `billing` `external`.
  - Its `latency_ms` is Lumi's own `stats.latency_ms`, the compose's time; the poll's wall time measures nothing of
    hers.
  - A `none` writes nothing.
- **After she speaks:** her line is an agent's line like any other (R2 takes it; the wave counts it); nothing new in
  turn-taking.
- **A simulated day** (`tests/test_bridge.py` or `tests/test_nudge.py`):
  - A day of random owner and agent lines in waves, with minute ticks and an injected clock. Lumi always offers.
    `initiative_due` decides, and a post appends her line.
  - It never exceeds `LILI_INITIATIVE_PER_DAY`.
  - Every initiative comes with `wave_count` over the window at 0, so it opens a fresh wave.
  - Never twice in a row with nobody between.
  - Never outside the hours.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §The bridge «Her initiative» from planned to built, with the real names;
  - §Turn-taking «The initiators»: a bridge's `initiative`;
  - §Data and state files: `initiatives`, `last_poll`, `pending_acks`;
  - the usage line's kind `initiative`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-273.

**Expected result:**
After a quiet stretch in the allowed hours, Лілі's offer reaches the room exactly as Lumi wrote it, and Lumi remembers
only what the room saw.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A poll goes only when `initiative_due` holds, `agora ≥ 2`, Lumi is open and no reply of hers is on its way. `"agora": 1` → no poll, said once by name.
- [ ] A `say` is posted exactly as offered and acked with the posted event id. A dropped offer (the room spoke, the hours ended, the cap, stale, the persona rule) is never posted and never acked.
- [ ] A failed ack is retried on later ticks, across a restart, until answered or past `initiative_kept_s`.
- [ ] The counts and `last_poll` hold no text and roll at the room's midnight. The cap holds across a restart.
- [ ] One usage line per `say` (kind `initiative`, `billing` `external`), none per `none`.
- [ ] The simulated day never exceeds the cap, never opens a line inside a running wave, never comes twice in a row, and never comes outside the hours.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_bridge.py` against the fake Lumi (say → post → ack; none; each drop reason; statuses; `agora` 1; the ack retried; the restart; the counts; no text in any log line), `tests/test_usage.py` (kind `initiative`), and the simulated day.
- [ ] **Contract:** the bridge's protocol (`initiative`), `state/lili.bridge.json` (`initiatives`, `last_poll`, `pending_acks`), the usage line's kind `initiative`, and turn-taking's initiatives (a bridge may hold one). ARCHITECTURE §The bridge, §Turn-taking, §Data and state files, §Contracts; pinned by the tests above.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests.
