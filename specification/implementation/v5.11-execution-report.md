### AGORA-126: Known events in the life story
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** b52c0c7 · **GitHub:** #126
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **The format.** A chapter's `### Події` subsection holds lines `- YYYY-MM-DD · known|surprise · text`. `agents/life.py`
  cuts it out of `Chapter.body` into `Chapter.events`, so no prompt that reads a chapter's body ever sees the list.
- **The queries.** `events_due(story, day)` gives the events dated that day. `known_ahead(story, today, until)` gives
  the known ones ahead and never a surprise.
- **The guard.** A bad line, kind or date is a `LifeError` at startup.

### AGORA-127: Events of her life
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 9c2a67c · **GitHub:** #127
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **The capability.** `events` (persona-only) needs `life` and `today`, and `kira.toml` switches it on.
- **The roll.** Once per local hour within `EVENT_HOURS` (08-23), the idle watcher rolls at `EVENT_P` (0.12). The
  kind is drawn by `EVENT_WEIGHTS` (`everyday:6,joyful:2,inspiring:2,heavy:1`). The hour is marked rolled before
  the call, and missed hours are not caught up.
- **The call.** The event call (usage kind `event`) gets one regeneration when the truth guard rejects its text.
  The guard rejects the Ich forms, the members' names and the banned words; a second rejection keeps nothing.
- **Her story's events.** Each one enters once, on its date.
- **The day file.** `state/<name>.events/YYYY-MM-DD.md` is append-only and 0600. The hidden markers never reach a
  prompt.
- **Settings.** The event settings are in the panel's «Життя» group, with a `weights` kind validated by
  `parse_weights`.

### AGORA-128: Lviv's news
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 6de7e9b · **GitHub:** #128
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **The feed.** Zaxid.net's RSS (`https://zaxid.net/rss/all.xml`) holds about 50 items, roughly eight hours.
  - It is fetched at most every three hours and merged by link into `state/<name>.citynews.json` (0600, atomic).
  - A failed fetch is retried in an hour and never blocks the event roll.
  - A document with a DOCTYPE or an entity is refused.
- **The roll.** Each item is rolled once at `CITY_NEWS_P` (0.01), and an item naming Lviv at five times that. At
  most one item is woven an hour and `CITY_NEWS_MAX` (2) a day.
- **The weaving.** Each woven item touches her life in one of five ways, under `NEWS_RULES`: a real person stays a
  stranger, and no one from her life becomes a victim.
- **Deviations from the issue** (recorded on #128 and in ARCHITECTURE.md):
  - the feed is refreshed every three hours and merged, instead of fetched once a day, because it covers only
    about eight hours;
  - the `CITY_NEWS_P` default is 0.01, not 0.03;
  - an item naming Lviv rolls at five times the probability.
- **The network fixture.** An autouse conftest fixture makes any test that reaches the real weather or news fetch
  fail. This closes v5.10 review #5.
