## Findings

### 1. 🔴 `PURR_RE` backtracks exponentially
**Where:** [agents/turns.py](../../agents/turns.py) `PURR_RE`, called from `on_message` and `seed_context` in [agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py).

**Scenario:** the whitespace between purr words can be matched either by the group's leading `\s*` or by the previous word's trailing `[\s.,!…]*`, so rejecting a near-purr doubles in time with every word. Measured: `"мур " * 18 + "котику"` 0.05 s, `* 25` 6.4 s; 30–35 words take minutes to hours. `is_purr` runs synchronously in `on_message` for every room message — before the allowlist — so Ada, Bruno and Кіт all freeze, and `seed_context` runs it again over the last 40 messages before `sync_forever`, so a restart hangs on the same message. The owner, or an unpredictable model reply, can trigger it.

**Fix:** drop the leading `\s*` (the trailing class and `.strip()` already cover whitespace), and cap the length a purr may have. Regression test: the adversarial inputs return within a bound, and every `PURRS` entry still matches.

### 2. 🟠 The owner's message can be flagged as a purr
**Where:** `on_message` and `seed_context` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)), `_ambient` ([agents/turns.py](../../agents/turns.py)).

**Scenario:** the purr flag is computed for any sender. The owner's «Мур!» or «*гладить кота*» becomes invisible to the turn rules: `wave_count` does not reset at it (the next agent-to-agent reply pauses up to `BOT_WINDOW_S`), `pending_answers` cannot find the owner's event and reserves nothing, `fallback_due` never fires, and the cat stays silent on an unnamed owner «мур».

**Fix:** only a non-owner line is a purr (the same rule `decide_reply` already uses); in `_ambient`, the "never purrs at a purr" check applies only to agent messages. Regression tests at the agent and in the pure functions.

### 3. 🟠 A purr cancels a paused wave-limit resume
**Where:** `resume_later` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)).

**Scenario:** a wave at the limit pauses (R4). If the cat purrs at the blocked message (p 0.24, 0.48 when it names him), `self.timeline[-1][2] != event_id` drops the resume as "the conversation moved on". Without the purr it is sent. ARCHITECTURE says purrs are transparent.

**Fix:** compare against the last non-purr entry. Regression test: a paused resume followed by a purr still sends.

### 4. 🟠 The outgoing guard misses second-person and plural outing
**Where:** `BANNED_RE`, `outs_a_persona` ([agents/logic.py](../../agents/logic.py)).

**Scenario:** the guard fires only when a persona's name and a banned word share a sentence. «Ти теж бот.», «Ми тут усі штучні.», «Ви всі — LLM.», «Ада? Вона — бот.», «Бруно — робот.» (`\bбот` misses «робот»), «Адо, ти нейромережа.» are all sent. The cat usually answers whoever spoke, in the second person; the line reaches Ada's and Bruno's day memories, which are never deleted.

**Fix:** a banned word counts as outing when the line names a persona *or* addresses/points at someone (ти, ви, ми, всі/усі, вона, він, вони and their case forms); add робот and нейро(мереж). The cat speaking about himself («я штучний») stays allowed. Regression tests for each line above and for the allowed self-description.

### 5. 🟠 The cat's death year reaches his prompt from 2028
**Where:** `life_section` ([agents/life.py](../../agents/life.py)), [agents/canon/kit.life.md](../../agents/canon/kit.life.md).

**Scenario:** on 2028-01-01 the hidden chapter «2028–2038 · останні цикли» becomes current and is printed as `Зараз (2028–2038 · останні цикли):` — the end year is his death year (`"2038" in life_section(story, date(2028, 1, 1))` is True). Ada's and Bruno's last chapters end with their death years too, decades away. ARCHITECTURE §Canon: "the year never reaches a prompt". The chapter titles also carry «(зараз)», which goes stale when the chapter turns past.

**Fix:** the current chapter is printed without its end year (`Зараз (з 2028 · …)`), and «(зараз)» leaves the titles. Regression test: no agent's death year in `life_section` for any year up to the death.

### 6. 🟠 `split_resolution` and Gemini's Markdown
**Where:** `split_resolution` ([agents/mood.py](../../agents/mood.py), copied from Lumi).

**Scenario:** `**РЕЗОЛЮЦІЯ:**\nСонний…` → `'**\nСонний…'` (the card shows `**`, the prompt gets `Настрій дня: **`). The first line containing «резолюц» wins, so a transit paragraph that echoes the prompt («…це відіб'ється в РЕЗОЛЮЦІЇ.») turns most of the reading into the «resolution».

**Fix:** the last line that *starts* with «резолюц» (after stripping `*#_` and spaces); `*_#` stripped from the text after the colon. Regression tests for both.

### 7. 🟠 The horoscope is not cast at the day boundary
**Where:** `idle_watcher`, `world_tick` (a creature returns early) and `reply` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)).

**Scenario:** `ensure_mood` runs at startup and before a spoken line. A container that runs past midnight casts the new day's mood only on the cat's first spoken line — with the defaults about 6 % of messages — so the panel can say "No horoscope yet" all day, against the DoD.

**Fix:** `idle_watcher` calls `ensure_mood` for an agent with `mood` (a no-op once cast; the back-off holds). Regression test: a tick past midnight casts the day's mood without a reply.

### 8. 🟠 A cut horoscope is cached for the day
**Where:** `ensure_mood` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)), `MOOD_MAX_TOKENS` ([agents/mood.py](../../agents/mood.py)).

**Scenario:** the prompt asks for several paragraphs and the resolution last; with a 1200-token cap a Ukrainian reading can be cut. `generate()` drops the finish reason, so a cut reading is logged, reused after a restart, and its half-paragraph becomes «Настрій дня».

**Fix:** use `complete()`; a reading cut by the cap (`max_tokens`) is a failure (not logged, back-off); the cap rises to 2000. Regression test with a fake that reports `max_tokens`.

### 9. 🟠 A torn or unwritable mood log
**Where:** `_read` ([panel/memory.py](../../panel/memory.py)), `_read_raw` and the append in `ensure_mood` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)).

**Scenario:** the log is appended, not written atomically; a disk-full or SIGKILL mid-write can leave invalid UTF-8. The panel's `_read` catches only `FileNotFoundError` → `/api/agents/kit/memory` is 500 on every poll ("never a 500" is the module's own rule). The agent's `_read_raw` catches only `OSError`, so `ensure_mood` raises before the back-off is set; the same happens when the append fails — and every later spoken line repeats the paid mood call.

**Fix:** both readers decode with `errors="replace"`; a failed append keeps the reading in memory for the day (logged as an error, never the text); any failure sets the back-off. Regression tests: a log with invalid bytes (panel 200, agent mood still found), a failing append (one call, mood kept).

### 10. 🟡 "The full reading" folds shut every poll
**Where:** the Mood tab in [panel/static/app.js](../../panel/static/app.js).

**Scenario:** the dashboard re-renders on every poll and the `<details>` loses its open state; day memories already keep theirs in `S.expandedDays`.

**Fix:** keep the open state in `S` and render `<details open>`. Regression: a static test pins the state.

### 11. 🟡 The mood log's mode and its documentation
**Where:** `ensure_mood` ([agents/agent.py](../../agents/agent.py)); ARCHITECTURE §Data and state files.

**Scenario:** the log is created with the default umask (0644); the journal and usage files are 0600 ("private from birth"). The state-files table has no `<name>.mood.log` row.

**Fix:** create it 0600 like the usage file; add the table row. Regression test on the mode.

### 12. 🟡 Kit's `init: true` is not pinned
**Where:** [tests/test_compose.py](../../tests/test_compose.py) `test_python_is_never_pid_1`.

**Scenario:** the test lists ada, bruno and usage-report; dropping `init: true` from kit would make every stop wait 30 s and end in SIGKILL, and the suite would stay green.

**Fix:** the test covers every agent service (the registry's agents).

### 13. 🟡 A purr-like spoken answer holds the reservation — DEFER → v4.3
If the model's answer to the owner is just «Мрр.», every agent marks it a purr; `pending_answers` counts arrivals over the non-purr timeline, so the cat never "arrives" and the wave stays held until `FALLBACK_S` (30 s). Bounded and rare; v4.3 reworks the cat's turns (nudges open their own waves), so it is fixed there.

### 14. 🟡 The whole mood log on every poll — DEFER → backlog
About 8 KB a day, read and regex-scanned on every 10 s poll. Read only the tail (today's block fits in the last 64 KB) or cache by mtime and size.

### 15. 🟡 `.plain` has no CSS rule — DEFER → cleanup
The biorhythm list renders with bullets and an indent. One rule appended after the handoff CSS.

### 16. 🟡 The token chart's colours — DEFER → backlog
Five tints cycle from a sixth agent, and the `.b` segment is shaped as the top one. v4.6 brings the roster to five agents, which still fits.

### 17. 🟡 Weak tests — DEFER → cleanup
The cat's out-guard test inherits persona capabilities, so `build_prompt` reads the repo's `state/`; `memory_view(...)["mood"] is None` passes because `with_mood` defaults to False; `test_panel_static` substring checks would accept a wrong condition. `owner_speakers` is pinned only through `simulate` (#2 adds a direct test).

### 18. 🟡 Ada's and Bruno's last chapters narrate their deaths — DEFER → backlog
Found while fixing #5: «Ада тихо відходить уві сні восени 2079 року…», «Відходить навесні 2072 року…». When the last chapter becomes current (2066) its body enters the prompt. The header no longer carries the year (#5); the bodies are the owner's canon, and the edit can wait for a year closer than 2066. The cat's last chapter has no such sentence, and a test pins his whole section for every year to his death.

## What's solid

- **Every agent computes the same cat.** The reaction draw, the purr choice and the reservation are sha256 draws over the `event_id` and the shared settings; no shared state or coordination was added.
- **The purr path** runs after the first-sync guard and the allowlist, makes no model call, sets no typing, and cannot purr at itself or at a purr — no loops.
- **The hidden future, today:** the mood request carries only the natal text (comment lines stripped), the cat has no plans (`next_chapter` is never read), and in 2026–2027 no prompt sees 2028, 2038 or the death date.
- **The panel:** no request data reaches a path; the Bearer middleware covers the memory route; mood text always goes through `esc()`; a creature's card hides Forget and the summary; `KIT_PASSWORD` never reaches the panel's whitelisted environment.
- **The `kit` service** matches ada and bruno (image, command, init, env_file, TZ, user, the `state` mount, restart, the grace period), and a broken other member's TOML no longer stops an agent.
