# v9.1 — Issues

Issues for phase **v9.1 — When it was: a time on every memory and every plan** (version **v9 — Time and her plans**),
derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v9.1 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-276 →
**AGORA-277…281**).

Every memory and every plan in Кіра's and Устина's prompts gets its age and its date, and she still speaks without
dates. The phase builds on v7.5's start-and-tail prompt (the cached start must not change more often than now) and
v7.6's `lumi` memory (her window, her day's digest, her gists, her own `recall` and `day_talk`). Her checklist is v9.2.

**Reconciled with the code (2026-11-18):**
- **Labels today.** Her prompt's time labels come from several helpers:
  - `world.ago` (coarse: «цього тижня», «минулого місяця», «якось») — recall's rows and the window's time lines;
  - `world.relative_when` — «Востаннє ви говорили …»;
  - `talk._label`, `talk.day_when`, `talk.week_label` — the summaries, the gists and `day_talk`;
  - `chronicle.layer_label` → `world.day_label`, `week_label`, `month_label`, `year_label` — her memories, which
    already carry dates but no age;
  - `thoughts.thought_rows` — a part of the day only;
  - `plans_section` (agent.py) — «На цей тиждень» with no date.
  Facts carry no time in the prompt (`Fact.added` is a day, ISO).
- **Start and tail** (`logic.instruction_and_start`, v7.5):
  - the start: canon → life → memories → plans → mood → last summary → facts → talk;
  - the tail: world → today → digest → thoughts → … → Agora today → recalled → private brief → move → asked →
    voice → rules.
  A label in the start may change at most once a day; an hour-level age lives only in the tail and the history.
- **The window's time lines** (`world.history_times`, a pause of `HISTORY_GAP_MS` 3 h) are her private history's
  only. Her Agora history (`agora_lines`, with `history_ts`) has none.
- **The day's digest** (`lumi.talk_digest_request`) is written «Без годин», and its batch's lines carry no time.
- A capability is a TOML line under her capabilities (`recall = true`; `roster.CAPABILITIES`, `PERSONA_ONLY`).

## v9.1 — When it was: a time on every memory and every plan

### AGORA-277 — `stamp`: the age and the date, the `stamps` capability and the date finder — pure

**Description:**
One pure label for every memory and plan, in `agents/world.py`, with an hour-level form for the tail and a day-level
form for the cached start. A pure finder of a date said in an answer. The capability `stamps` in `agents/roster.py`
and `agents/config.py`, read by nothing yet.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`stamp(then, now, fine=True)`** in `agents/world.py`: the age, then the date in brackets.
  - **Past, `fine`:**
    - within an hour in minutes: «40 хвилин тому (14:20)»;
    - within a day in hours: «6 годин тому (сьогодні, 08:15)», «12 годин тому (вчора, 21:40)»;
    - then by calendar days: «вчора ввечері (17.11, 21:40)», «позавчора (16.11)», «5 днів тому (13.11)»;
    - then weeks, months and years: «тиждень тому (11.11)», «3 тижні тому (28.10)», «місяць тому (18.10)»,
      «5 місяців тому (18.06)», «рік тому (18.11.2025)».
    - The hour is shown only within two days. The year is shown only when it differs from now's.
  - **Past, not `fine`** (the start): the day is the smallest unit: «сьогодні (18.11)», «вчора (17.11)», «3 дні тому
    (15.11)». The text changes only when the date does.
  - **A time ahead**, the same way: «через 40 хвилин (15:00)», «через 3 години (17:00)», «завтра (19.11)», «у суботу
    (22.11)» (within the week), «через 10 днів (28.11)».
  - Ukrainian plural forms come from tables:
    - the existing `_hours_word` and `_days_word`;
    - new tables for minutes, weeks, months and years;
    - the accusative after «тому» and «через» («1 хвилину», «2 хвилини», «5 хвилин»).
- **`span_stamp(start, end, now, fine)`** for a period:
  - an hour's summary: «сьогодні, 08:00–09:00 (18.11)», «3 години тому (11:00–12:00)»;
  - a day: «вчора (17.11)»;
  - a week: «тиждень тому (10–16.11)»;
  - a month: «місяць тому (жовтень 2026)»;
  - a year.
- **`says_date(text) -> int`**: how many dates an answer names.
  - Counted: «17.11», «17.11.2026», «17 листопада», «2026-11-17».
  - Not counted: an hour («14:20»), a count («17 разів»), a year alone, a version-like or decimal number («3.5»).
- **The capability `stamps`:**
  - in `roster.CAPABILITIES` and `PERSONA_ONLY`, with no `NEEDS`;
  - accepted by `agents/config.py`;
  - not yet in any TOML.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
`stamp`, `span_stamp` and `says_date` exist and are tested, and a TOML may name `stamps`. Nothing reads it yet.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `stamp` gives the forms above for minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years, past and future, `fine` and not,
      with the plural forms.
- [ ] Across a DST switch in `Europe/Kyiv` and in her southern zone (`America/Santiago`) the age is counted in local
      calendar days.
- [ ] `stamp(…, fine=False)` returns the same text for every `now` within one local day.
- [ ] `says_date` counts the four date forms and none of the near-misses.
- [ ] A persona TOML with `stamps = true` loads; another type with it is refused, as `recall` is.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_world.py` (stamp, span_stamp, says_date; injected `now`, two zones) and
      `tests/test_roster.py` / `tests/test_config.py` (the capability).
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-278 — The tail and the history stamped by the hour: window, Agora, digest, thoughts, recall, `day_talk`

**Description:**
For a member with `stamps`, everything her prompt reads anew on each call carries an hour-level `stamp`: the window's
time lines (private and, new, Agora), «Востаннє ви говорили», her day's digest, her thoughts of today, her recall,
`day_talk`, and the «Сьогодні» header. This touches `agents/world.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/lumi.py`,
`agents/thoughts.py`, `agents/archive.py` and `agents/memory_tools.py`. Without `stamps` every text is as now.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The window's time lines** (`history_times(…, stamped)`):
  - with `stamps` the line reads «— 6 годин тому (08:15): —» and, after a pause, «— через 3 дні, вчора ввечері
    (17.11, 21:40): —»;
  - still at the window's start and after every pause of `HISTORY_GAP_MS`;
  - softened spans (v5.24) keep their place.
- **Her Agora history** gets the same time lines, from `history_ts`.
  - Only in her own prompt, as entries with an empty speaker, as in her private history.
  - The shared timeline, the ranking and the turn rules read nothing new.
  - The other members' Agora prompts are unchanged.
- **«Востаннє ви говорили»** (`last_talk_line`): `stamp(…, fine=True)`.
- **The day's digest** (`lumi`):
  - `talk_digest_request` gives each batch line its time («[09:12] Ich: …»);
  - with `stamps`, the request asks each part of the day to begin with its time «[HH:MM]» in place of «Без годин»;
  - `talk_digest_section` turns each «[HH:MM]» into «[HH:MM, 6 годин тому]», and the title gets «(сьогодні,
    18.11)»;
  - a digest written before this phase, with no marks, shows as it is.
- **Her thoughts of today** (`thought_rows`): «- 5 годин тому (09:12), прочитавши «…»: …», in place of «вранці».
- **Recall** (`render_recalled`):
  - each row's `ago` becomes `stamp(…, fine=True)`: «6 годин тому (08:15), в особистому чаті — Ich тоді: «…»»;
  - «вчора ввечері (17.11, 21:40) я думала: …»;
  - a fact row: «(те, що ти знаєш про Ich, з 17.11) …»;
  - the v8.3.7 form «тоді: «…»» stays.
- **`day_talk`** (`memory_tools.day_result`): the label becomes `span_stamp` of that day, with its date («вчора
  (17.11):»). A day not yet come is still nothing.
- **«Сьогодні»** (`today_section`): the header «Сьогодні (вівторок, 18.11):».
- **Agora today** (`render_agora_now`): unchanged, since its lines already carry today's «14:05».
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Prompt assembly and §Memory: the tail's labels, the Agora time lines;
  - §Contracts: the transcript's time line for a `stamps` member.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-277

**Expected result:**
With `stamps`, a prompt's tail and history say «N годин тому» with the hour and «вчора / N днів тому» with the date.
Without `stamps`, nothing changes.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Private window: the time line at the start and after a 3-hour pause carries `stamp`; without `stamps` it is
      as before (the existing tests still pass unchanged).
- [ ] Agora: her prompt's history has the time lines; Ада's, built from the same timeline, has none; the turn
      decisions are unchanged on the same inputs.
- [ ] Digest: the batch lines carry their time; «[HH:MM]» renders with its age; an old unmarked digest renders as it
      is.
- [ ] Thoughts, recall rows (line, thought, fact) and `day_talk` carry their labels; recall keeps «тоді».
- [ ] No label in the tail says «якось», and none is in the start (AGORA-279 owns the start).
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_world.py` (history_times stamped), `tests/test_recall.py`, `tests/test_thoughts.py`,
      `tests/test_lumi_memory.py` / `tests/test_lumi_recall.py` (digest, `day_talk`), and an Agora prompt test for a
      `stamps` member and a plain one. Gemini mocked, clock injected.
- [ ] **Contract:** the time line's form for a `stamps` member: ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, pinned by a test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-279 — The start stamped by the day: memories, plans, facts, talk, the last summary

**Description:**
For a member with `stamps`, what lies in her cached start carries a day-level `stamp`: her day memories and digests,
her plans of the year, month, week and day, the facts about him, her talk's summaries and gists, and the last
session's summary. The label changes at most once a day, so the cache is rebuilt no more often than now. This
touches `agents/agent.py` (`memories_section`, `plans_section`, `prompt_layers`), `agents/chronicle.py`,
`agents/facts.py` and `agents/talk.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Memories:**
  - each layer is labelled by its age, then what it is now: «3 дні тому — субота, 15 листопада: …», «тиждень тому —
    тиждень 3–9 листопада: …», «місяць тому — жовтень 2026: …»;
  - in `lumi` mode, her one-line memories the same;
  - `layer_label` stays as it is for the generators' own prompts.
- **Plans:**
  - each horizon names its period with its dates and says when it was written (the plan file's time, day-level):
    «На цей тиждень (17–23.11), складений позавчора:», «На сьогодні (вівторок, 18.11), складений сьогодні:», «На
    листопад 2026:», «На 2026 рік:»;
  - a weekday named in an item of the week's or the day's plan gets its date in that period: «у четвер» → «у четвер
    (20.11)» (pure; a weekday outside the period is left as it is);
  - the hidden mutation tags are still stripped first.
- **Facts:** «- (з 17.11, вчора) …» — `Fact.added` as a day-level stamp. The order and the cap are as now.
- **Talk:**
  - `talk_section` (`layered`) and `gist_section` (`lumi`) label each row by `span_stamp(…, fine=False)`: «сьогодні,
    08:00–09:00 (18.11)», «вчора (17.11)», «тиждень тому (10–16.11)»;
  - they no longer say «у вівторок» or «минулого тижня» without a date.
- **The last summary:** «Що ти пам'ятаєш з минулої розмови (вчора, 17.11, 21:40): …», from `summary_ms`, day-level
  with the hour of the session's end.
- **The cache:** the start's text, built from the same files at 09:00 and at 23:59 of one day, is the same; built after
  midnight it differs only in its labels.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Memory (memories, plans, facts, talk labels) and §Prompt assembly (the start's labels are
  day-level).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-277

**Expected result:**
With `stamps`, every memory and plan in the start carries its age and its date, and the start stays byte-for-byte the
same within a day.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Memories, plans (headers and in-item weekdays), facts, talk rows and the last summary carry their labels, and
      without `stamps` nothing changes.
- [ ] The weekday-to-date pass dates «у четвер» inside the week's period and leaves a weekday outside it as it is.
- [ ] A plan's «складений …» comes from the file's time, day-level.
- [ ] The cache rule: the start's text (`instruction_and_start(...)[1]`) is equal at two hours of one day for the same
      files, and changes after midnight.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_memory.py`, `tests/test_plans.py`, `tests/test_facts.py`, `tests/test_talk.py` /
      `tests/test_lumi_gists.py`, and `tests/test_prompt_slow.py` (the start unchanged within a day). Clock injected.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-280 — The rule, the count of dates said, her card, the switch on for Кіра and Устина

on her card, and `stamps` is switched on for Кіра and Устина. This touches `agents/private.py`, `agents/logic.py`,
`agents/agent.py`, `agents/usage.py`, `panel/memory.py`, `panel/static/app.js`, `agents/kira.toml`,
`agents/ustyna.toml` and ARCHITECTURE.md.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The rule (`STAMPS_RULE`)**, for a `stamps` member:
  - alone with him, in her private brief;
  - in Agora, in the rules line beside the discretion line.
  - It says: the dates and hours beside her memories and plans are there so she knows when each thing was; she says
    it as people do — «вчора», «кілька годин тому», «минулого місяця», «у четвер» — never a date or an hour in
    numbers, unless he asks for one.
  - It never names a model, a bot or a tool.
- **The count:**
  - every answer she sends (private, Telegram, Agora, writing first; Gemini's or the backup's) is passed through
    `says_date`;
  - a count above 0 goes into her answer's log line (`dates=N`, never the text) and into her `answers.jsonl` row as

  - `panel/memory.py` sums today's `dates` from `answers.jsonl` for an agent with `stamps`;
  - the card shows «Dates said today: N» (the panel's UI is English).
- **The switch:** `stamps = true` in `agents/kira.toml` and `agents/ustyna.toml`.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Contracts: the capability `stamps`, the `answers.jsonl` field `dates`, the rule's place in the prompt order;
  - §The panel: the card line;
  - §Memory: a summary of v9.1;
  - the v9.1 paragraph in §Prompt assembly.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-278, AGORA-279

**Expected result:**
Кіра's and Устина's prompts carry the labels and the rule, a date she names is counted, and the owner sees the count on
her card.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The rule is in her private brief and her Agora rules with `stamps`, and nowhere without it; the banned-words test

- [ ] A sent answer with «17.11» writes `dates: 1` to `answers.jsonl` and `dates=1` to the log; one without writes no

- [ ] The card shows today's sum; a corrupt or old row counts as 0.
- [ ] Both companions' TOMLs load with `stamps`; the room's other members do not have it.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_answers.py`, `tests/test_panel_memory.py`, the TOML test in `tests/test_character.py`,
