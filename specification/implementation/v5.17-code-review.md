## Findings

### 1. 🟠 The photo line is cut from every agent's every reply — `agents/agent.py` `_compose`

**Scenario:**
- `_compose` runs `split_photo` on every reply of every agent, and `MARK_LINE_RE` matches a line that begins «Фото:»
  in any case.
- Мія, the archivist, answers in Agora: «Фото: 1934 рік, архів на Підвальній». That line is cut from her message and
  the log says `photo: dropped (reply)`.
- The phase is Кіра's private chat only; Agora must be untouched.

**Fix:**
- cut only in her private answers: Gemini's (`shots` given) and the backup's (`responder is self.fallback_responder`);
- a test: an Agora reply that begins «Фото:» is sent whole.

### 2. 🟠 The kept note escapes the cut — `agents/photo.py` `split_photo`

**Scenario:**
- After a photo, her line is kept as «…\n[селфі: я під куполом]», and Gemini reads it in its history.
- Models echo such patterns. A next answer «Ось я знову [селфі: я на балконі]» has the note inside the line, in lower
  case. `MARK_ANY_RE` is case-sensitive, so the text goes out with «[селфі: …]» in it and no photo is made.

**Fix:**
- `split_photo` also cuts the note's bracketed form anywhere in a line, in any case (`[фото: …]`, `[селфі: …]`), and
  takes it as the shot;
- a test with an echoed note.

### 3. 🟡 No faces is logged at INFO — `agents/agent.py` `__init__`

**Scenario:**
- The first live deploy logged `photos: 0 faces`: `deploy.sh` had made `kira.face/` 600 (fixed in 6dfaeb7).
- Every selfie would then be `blocked` and quietly redrawn without a photo, and nothing stood out in the log.

**Fix:**
- with `photos` on and no faces, log a WARNING: `photos: no faces — a selfie will not come out`;
- a test.

### 4. 🟡 The faces in memory (DEFER → backlog)

`load_faces` reads all of `kira.face/` at start, about 30 MB for 21 photos, while a selfie uses 4. Reading the chosen
four per selfie would save the memory. It costs a disk read per selfie and changes nothing visible.

### 5. 🟡 `photo_sent` as an attribute (DEFER → backlog)

`send_telegram` reports the photo through `self.photo_sent`. Her private replies are serialised (`_private_pending`),
so no second send runs meanwhile today. A return value would be sturdier, but it changes `send_telegram`'s signature
for every caller.

  - a failure draws her answer again without the skill, so she never says «дивись» with nothing.
