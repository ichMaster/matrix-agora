## v5.17 — Кіра's photos in Telegram

### AGORA-160 — The photo seam: the markers, the request and the image model (`agents/photo.py`, usage `photo`)

**Description:**
A new module `agents/photo.py` holds four things: the photo marker lines, the pure request builder, and the image
call with its references and one retry. Its usage line is the new kind `photo` (`agents/usage.py`,
`panel/static/app.js`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **Markers (pure):** `split_photo(text) -> (text, Shot | None)` cuts a line `ФОТО: …` or `СЕЛФІ: …` (any case;
  the last one if several; mid-line too, as `split_marker`), and the line is never sent. `Shot(kind="photo" |
  "selfie", description)`; an empty description is none.
- **The request (pure):** `photo_request(shot, moment) -> str` builds an English instruction from:
  - her description (in her language);
  - the moment: the part of the day, the day's weather line, the season, the city (Lviv unless she names another
    place);
  - for a selfie, «the young woman is the same person as in the reference photos: keep her face and hair», and «she
    wears what suits the weather and the scene as described — not the clothes of the reference photos»;
  - «a casual smartphone photo sent in a chat: natural light, slightly imperfect framing, no studio look», «no other
    identifiable person in focus, no text or watermark».
- **The call:** `PhotoMaker(client, model, size, faces, sink)`, with `async make(shot, moment) -> Made`.
  - **The request:** `generate_content` with the reference parts for a selfie (`pick_faces`: up to 4, `kira.png`
    first) and the prompt; `response_modalities=["IMAGE"]`, `ImageConfig(aspect_ratio="3:4", image_size=size)`.
  - **The result:** `Made(image bytes | None, mime, why)`, with `why` one of `ok`, `blocked` (a prompt-stage block,
    or a finish without an image) or `failed` (an error after one retry).
  - **One retry** on a `ServerError` or a timeout.
  - **The log** holds the outcome and the type only, never the description or the request.
- **Faces:** `load_faces(folder) -> list[(name, bytes, mime)]` reads the top-level png/jpg of `kira.face/`.
  `places/` is not read in this phase. It holds the owner's examples of her places (2026-10-31: the 12 generated photos
  with her face joined the folder as `gen-*.jpg`, and the 6 without it went to `places/`).
  - `pick_faces(faces, rng)` (pure) gives `kira.png` always, plus 3 others drawn per selfie with an injected rng, so
    the references' clothes and light vary.
  - A missing or empty folder gives no faces, and a selfie then fails as `blocked`.
- **Usage:** each call is a usage line of kind `photo` on its model (the tokens from `usage_metadata`). `KINDS` gains
  `photo`, and the panel's `KIND_ORDER` lists it.
- **ARCHITECTURE §Token accounting:** the kind `photo`, priced by its pair in `PRICE_MODELS`
  (`gemini-nano-banana-2.1=1.50/30`).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
The markers come out of her answer, a request is built from her words and her moment, and the image model's answer
comes back as bytes or a reason, each call counted under `photo`.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `ФОТО:` / `СЕЛФІ:` cut (last line, mid-line, any case, several → the last), never in the text. A text without
      one is unchanged.
- [ ] The request holds the description, the moment and the style. For a selfie it holds the face line and the
      clothes line, and for a photo neither. No Ukrainian template words leak into it.
- [ ] `make`: references only for a selfie; one retry on a 503 then `ok`; two 503s → `failed`; a prompt-stage block or
      a finish without an image → `blocked`.
- [ ] `pick_faces`: `kira.png` always and 3 others drawn by the rng (scripted in the test). `places/` is never read.
- [ ] Usage line `photo` per call. No description, request or key in a log.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_photo.py` with a fake `genai` client (no network); made-up descriptions.
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage kind `photo` — `KINDS` and its pinning test, the panel's kind order, and ARCHITECTURE
      §Token accounting.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-161 — Telegram `send_photo` and the capability `photos`

**Description:**
Her bot sends a photo with a caption. A person's TOML can switch on `photos`, which needs `telegram`
(`agents/telegram.py`, `agents/roster.py`, `agents/kira.toml`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **`TelegramBot.send_photo`:** `send_photo(chat_id, image: bytes, caption: str | None, mime="image/jpeg") -> bool`,
  multipart like `send_voice`. A caption over 1024 characters is the caller's to split: `send_photo` refuses one,
  returns False and logs that.
- **`send_chat_action`** takes `upload_photo`.
- **The capability:** `photos` joins `CAPABILITIES` and `PERSONA_ONLY`, with `NEEDS["photos"] = "telegram"`.
  `agents/kira.toml` gets `photos = true`.
- **ARCHITECTURE §Contracts:** the capability, and the canon's `kira.face/` folder (the owner's reference photos,
  gitignored, synced by `deploy.sh`, mounted read-only).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Her bot can send a photo with her words under it, and only a person with `telegram` can have `photos`.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `send_photo` posts multipart `sendPhoto` with `chat_id`, `photo` and `caption`; True on ok. A caption over 1024
      is refused.
- [ ] `photos` without `telegram` is a roster error; on a non-person it is an error too.
- [ ] Кіра's TOML loads with `photos`.
- [ ] **Tests:** the Telegram client against a fake session (as `send_voice`'s tests); the roster rules.
- [ ] **Contract:** the capability list (its pinning test) and ARCHITECTURE §Contracts (`photos`, `kira.face/`).
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-162 — The photo in her private answer: the skill, the send, the redraw, the archive, the settings

**Description:**
This issue wires the photo into her private answer in Telegram, through the skill, the generation, the send, the
redraw without a photo, the archive and the file, the day cap and the settings (`agents/agent.py`,
`agents/private.py` or `agents/photo.py` for the skill text, `agents/settings.py`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The skill:** `PHOTO_SKILL`, in her words.
  - **What it says:** if Ich asks for a photo, or you want to show him something from your life, add a separate last
    line: `ФОТО:` and what is in the shot, or `СЕЛФІ:` when you are in it (a selfie, or someone took you), one
    sentence, as you would describe the snapshot. He sees the photo, not the line. You may refuse if you do not want
    to, or cannot.
  - **It never says** that a picture is made.
  - **When it is offered:** in her private brief for Gemini, when the door is Telegram, `photos` is on and the day's cap
    is not reached; for an answer and for a line she writes first alike.
  - **Never** to the backup, never in Element.
- **The marker:** `split_photo` runs on Gemini's answer in her private chat. A marker in the backup's answer is dropped,
  and so is one in Element or under the cap; the log says so by kind.
- **The photo:**
  - `upload_photo` shows while it is made;
  - `PhotoMaker.make(shot, moment)`, the moment from her clock, her day's weather line and the season.
- **The send** (`send_telegram` gains a photo):
  - the voice note as now;
  - then `send_photo` with her text as the caption when it is 1024 characters or fewer;
  - else the photo with no caption, then the text.
  - A failed `send_photo` sends the text alone.
- **The redraw:** a `blocked` or `failed` photo draws her answer again once on Gemini, without the photo. The brief adds
  a line that the photo did not come out this time and not to mention or promise it, with the skill not offered. If
  the redraw fails too, her first text goes without the marker.
- **What is kept:**
  - her archive line is her text plus `\n[фото: <опис>]` or `\n[селфі: <опис>]`, so her recall and her history know
    what she sent;
  - the file `state/kira.photos/YYYY-MM-DD/HHMMSS.jpg` (file 0600, folders 0700).
- **The cap:** `PHOTO_DAY_MAX` (40) counts today's files. Past it, no skill and no photo (`photo: off — the day's
  cap`).
- **Settings:**
  - `PHOTO_MODEL` (`gemini-nano-banana-2.1`; choices `gemini-3-pro-image`, `gemini-3.1-flash-image`);
  - `PHOTO_SIZE` (`1K`; `2K`);
  - `PHOTO_DAY_MAX` (40, 0–200);
  - all in the registry, read with `photos`.
- **The log:** `photo: sent (selfie|photo, telegram)`, `photo: blocked — her answer without it`, `photo: failed
  (<type>) — her answer without it`, `photo: dropped (backup|element|cap)`. Never the description.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Message flow: the photo in her Telegram answer;
  - §Contracts: `state/kira.photos/`;
  - §Configuration: the settings.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-160, AGORA-161

**Expected result:**
In Telegram she sends a selfie or a photo of her life when she writes such a line, after her voice note and with her
words under it. A photo that does not come out never leaves her saying «дивись» with nothing.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **A selfie line in Gemini's answer:**
      - the photo is made with the faces;
      - the order is the voice note, then `sendPhoto` with the text as the caption;
      - the archive line ends with `[селфі: …]`;
      - the file is 0600.
- [ ] A long text: the photo with no caption, then the text.
- [ ] A marker in the backup's answer, in Element or past the cap: dropped, the text unchanged, no image call.
- [ ] A blocked or failed photo: her answer drawn again once without the skill, with the «did not come out» line. A
      failed redraw sends the first text without the marker.
- [ ] Writing first can carry a photo.
- [ ] The skill text never says she makes pictures (the persona test words). It is offered only on Telegram with
      `photos`, under the cap.
- [ ] No description, request or path in a log.
- [ ] **Tests:** the private route with the Gemini fake (a marker in its answer), a fake `PhotoMaker`, the Telegram
      fake recording the call order, the backup fake; an injected clock; made-up lines only.
- [ ] **Contract:** `state/kira.photos/` in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts; the settings in §Configuration.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** in Telegram he asks for a photo of her. She sends a selfie with her face from the
      references, her words as the caption, and her voice note.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** she sends a photo of her life on her own, in a line she writes first or in an answer.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** the token report shows `photo`, and her log `photo: sent` (no text).
