# v5.11 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.11 — Events of her life: known, generated every hour, and Lviv's news** (version
**v5 — Voice: Hermes, the agents hear and speak**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)
§v5.11, the design in [features/voice-emotion.md](../features/voice-emotion.md) §Events of her life and §Decided, and
the contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous
phase (AGORA-125 → **AGORA-126…131**).

Things now happen to Кіра that she did not plan. They come from three sources:
- the dated turning points of her current chapter (known ahead, or a surprise on its date);
- events rolled at each local hour of her waking day;
- Lviv's real news from Zaxid.net, woven in rarely.

Each event is written once to `state/kira.events/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. From there it reaches «Сьогодні», the day memory and
her next plans, and the panel lists it. Built on v2.2's plans, day memories and hourly «Сьогодні» block, and on
v5.10's mood. Left for later: her reading of the news with full text and thoughts (v5.12); a numeric state, feelings,
open threads and anticipation (v8).

## v5.11 — Events of her life

### AGORA-126 — Known events in the life story: a dated list in a chapter, hidden from every prompt

**Description:**
A chapter of the life story may carry a dated list of events, each known ahead or a surprise. `agents/life.py` parses
the list and keeps it out of the chapter's body. Every prompt that reads the chapter (`life_section`, the plan, the
«Сьогодні» block, the day memory) therefore never sees it. Pure selectors give the code the events due on a day and
the known ones ahead. The list for Кіра is drafted in her private canon for the owner to edit.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The format** (the canon layout contract): inside a chapter, a `### Події` subsection with one line per event,
  `- YYYY-MM-DD · known|surprise · <one sentence>`.
  - The subsection runs to the next `###`/`##` heading or the chapter's end.
  - A malformed line, an unknown kind, or a date outside the chapter's years raises `LifeError` at startup.
- **`Chapter`:** `body` no longer contains the `### Події` subsection; a new `events: tuple[LifeEvent, ...]`, where
  `LifeEvent(date, kind, text)` and kind is `known` or `surprise`.
  - `opening` and every existing reader of `body` are unchanged otherwise.
  - A story without the subsection parses exactly as before.
- **Pure selectors with an explicit date:**
  - `events_due(story, day)`: every event dated `day`, known or surprise.
  - `known_ahead(story, today, until)`: the **known** events with `today < date ≤ until`; never a surprise.
- **Her list:** draft about 8–12 dated events across the rest of her current chapter in `agents/canon/kira.life.md`
  (the private canon; never committed or quoted):
  - mostly known: a deadline, a conference, a birthday of one of her people, a trip, an observing run;
  - a few surprises;
  - one surprise dated within the next days, so the owner can check its arrival.

  The owner edits the list; the format is all the code relies on.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Canon (the life story's `### Події` list: visible only to the code that delivers it, a surprise
  never before its date) and §Contracts (the canon layout).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
A chapter's dated events are parsed and reachable by date, and no prompt that shows the chapter shows the list.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `parse_life` reads the list and strips it from `body`; a story without it is unchanged.
- [ ] `life_section` never contains an event line, and so never a surprise, on any date.
- [ ] `events_due` and `known_ahead` select by date; `known_ahead` never returns a surprise.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - parsing (good lines, a malformed line, an unknown kind, a date outside the chapter → `LifeError`);
  - the body stripped while the following `###` subsections remain;
  - `life_section` free of every event line;
  - the selectors at the date edges.

  A test marked `canon` parses her real life story with its list.
- [ ] **Contract:** the life story's canon layout (the `### Події` list), pinned by the parsing tests;
  ARCHITECTURE.md §Canon and §Contracts updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-127 — Events of her life: the capability, the hourly roll, the event call and `state/<name>.events/`

**Description:**
A new `agents/events.py` (pure where it decides) and the agent's wiring for a new persona-only capability, `events`.
Once in each local hour of her waking day, code rolls for an event of a weighted kind. A hit means one call writes
the event inside her life's frame, and the event is appended to the day's file. A known event or a surprise from her
chapter is entered on its date. The roll lives in the idle watcher, not in the reply path, so events fall whether
or not anyone talks to her.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The capability:**
  - `events` joins `CAPABILITIES` and `PERSONA_ONLY`, and `NEEDS` it with `life` and `today`;
  - no type has it by default; `kira.toml` switches it on.
- **Settings** (`agents/settings.py`, scope `cap:events`, shown in the panel):
  - `EVENT_P`: float 0–1, default 0.12;
  - `EVENT_WEIGHTS`: text such as `everyday:6,joyful:2,inspiring:2,heavy:1`, validated by the agents' own parser;
  - `EVENT_HOURS`: hours, default `08-23`; her waking hours, parsed by `agents/nudge.parse_hours`.
- **Pure decisions** (`agents/events.py`, rng and clock injected):
  - `parse_weights`;
  - `roll_event(rng, p, weights) -> kind | None`;
  - `hour_due(now, hours, rolled_hours)`, true once per local hour inside the window. There is no catch-up: an hour
    the agent was down for is never rolled.
- **The event call** (`event_request`, kind `event`, on `GEMINI_MODEL`, never the reply model). Its inputs:
  - her canon and the current chapter's body;
  - today's plan, with its hidden tags stripped;
  - the mood of the day as she reads it (`person_mood`);
  - the last events (today's and yesterday's);
  - the next chapter's opening as a silent direction, worded as the plans word it.

  Until v8.2 the "open threads" are the ones her chapter names. The call asks for one or two sentences in the first
  person, past tense, of what just happened to her, of the given kind:
  - everyday;
  - joyful;
  - inspiring;
  - heavy: an illness, a loss, a real setback, rarely.
- **The truth guard** (`event_ok`): an event is dropped (one regeneration, then none that hour) when it
  - names Ich or a room member (their `name_forms`, and «Ich»/«Іч» in any case);
  - matches `BANNED_RE`.

  An event involves only her, her people outside the room and the city.
- **Storage** (`state/<name>.events/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, 0600, append-only, never rewritten):
  - one block per event: `## HH:MM · <kind> · <source>` (source `known`, `surprise`, `rolled` or `news`), then the
    text;
  - every roll, hit or miss, appends a hidden `<!-- rolled HH -->` marker, so a restart never rolls the same hour
    twice;
  - pure `parse_day(text)` returns the events, and `events_for_prompt(events)` gives `[HH:MM] text` lines only: no
    kind, no source, no marker.
- **Known events and surprises:** at the first waking-hour tick of a day, `events_due` (AGORA-126) is entered into
  that day's file with its source. Each one is entered once (a marker per event); none is entered before its date.
- **Wiring:**
  - the idle watcher calls `ensure_events(now)` for a member with `events`;
  - it holds its own lock and backs off for 10 minutes after a failure;
  - it never blocks the watcher.
- **Usage:** `event` joins `agents/usage.py` `KINDS`.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Memory (an events layer between plans and memories);
  - §Data and state files (`<name>.events/`);
  - §Configuration (the three settings);
  - §Contracts (the capability and its needs).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-126

**Expected result:**
Through her waking hours one roll an hour lands about two events a day of weighted kinds. Her chapter's dated events
arrive on their dates. All of them go into an append-only day file, and none names Ich or a member.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One roll per local hour inside `EVENT_HOURS`; none outside; none twice after a restart; no catch-up.
- [ ] The kind follows the weights; `EVENT_P=0` rolls nothing.
- [ ] A surprise enters the file on its date, never before; a known event enters on its date.
- [ ] An event naming Ich or a member, or with a banned word, is never stored.
- [ ] The day file is appended only; `events_for_prompt` carries no kind, source, link or marker.
- [ ] Another persona's behaviour, and the cat's, are unchanged (no `events`, no file, no call).
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - the hourly roll with an injected rng and clock (the window's edges, the past-midnight window, a restart within
    the hour);
  - the weights' distribution over a seeded rng;
  - `parse_weights` (bad specs refused);
  - a surprise hidden before its date and entered on it;
  - the guard: Ich, a member's name form in another case, a banned word;
  - append-only storage and `parse_day`;
  - the capability rules (`PERSONA_ONLY`, `NEEDS`);
  - the settings validation.

  Gemini is mocked throughout.
- [ ] **Contract:** the `events` capability and its needs, the `<name>.events/` file format, the three settings.
  Pinned by the roster, settings and storage tests; ARCHITECTURE.md updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-128 — Lviv's news: Zaxid.net's feed once a day, woven into her life rarely

**Description:**
A new `agents/citynews.py`. Once a local day it fetches Zaxid.net's RSS (`https://zaxid.net/rss/all.xml`) and keeps
the day's items in `state/<name>.citynews.json`. At each hourly tick the not-yet-rolled items are rolled at
`CITY_NEWS_P`, with at most `CITY_NEWS_MAX` a day and one an hour. A hit picks one of five ways the item touches her
life, and AGORA-127's call writes it as her event (source `news`) under the real-person and tragedy rules.
v5.12 reuses this fetcher and adds the full text.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The fetch:**
  - stdlib only, with a 10 s timeout and no key;
  - parsed with `xml.etree`; a document with a `<!DOCTYPE` or `<!ENTITY` is refused;
  - each item keeps its title, link, short description (tags stripped, capped) and publication time;
  - kept once a day in `state/<name>.citynews.json` (`{date, items}`), atomic, 0600; it holds public news only;
  - a failed fetch means no news that day: it is logged with the exception's type only, and retried at most hourly.
- **Settings** (`cap:events`):
  - `CITY_NEWS_P`: float 0–1, default 0.03 (about one or two of a day's ~50 items);
  - `CITY_NEWS_MAX`: int 0–5, default 2.
- **The roll** (pure `roll_news(items, rolled, woven_today, p, cap, rng)`): each item published by the tick is rolled
  once. It returns at most one hit per hour and never one past the day's cap. Each rolled item appends a hidden
  `<!-- news-rolled <link> -->` marker to the day's events file.
- **The way** (rng, equal weights):
  - she was there;
  - she heard it from one of her people;
  - it touched her work;
  - it touched one of her people;
  - it changed her plans.
- **The call:** `event_request` gets the item's title and description and the way. The prompt states:
  - a real person in the news stays real and never becomes one of her people;
  - a real tragedy never gets a victim from her life; she may be affected (a night of alerts, a closed street), never
    own someone else's loss.

  The truth guard of AGORA-127 applies. The stored block keeps the item's link on a hidden `<!-- link: … -->` line
  (for the panel and v5.12), never in a prompt.
- **Tests stay off the network:** an autouse fixture in `tests/conftest.py` makes the default fetchers
  (`citynews`, and `weather`'s, which closes v5.10 review #5) raise if a test forgets to inject one.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Data and state files (`<name>.citynews.json`), §Configuration (`CITY_NEWS_P`,
  `CITY_NEWS_MAX`), §Prompt assembly (the news rules of the event call).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-127

**Expected result:**
Most of Lviv's news passes her by. On a typical day one or two items touch her life in one of five ways, and none
gives her a victim or a real person as her own.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One fetch a day, kept and reused across a restart; a failure means no news and no blocked event roll.
- [ ] Each item rolls once; at most one woven an hour and `CITY_NEWS_MAX` a day; `CITY_NEWS_P=0` weaves nothing.
- [ ] A woven item is stored as a `news` event with its hidden link; no prompt carries the link.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - the parse of a canned feed (titles, links, descriptions with tags, dates; a DOCTYPE refused; a broken document);
  - a failed fetch;
  - an item rolled in or out with an injected rng;
  - the daily cap and the hourly one;
  - the ways' choice;
  - the call's rules in the request;
  - the autouse no-network fixture.

  Gemini and the fetch are mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** `<name>.citynews.json` and the two settings; ARCHITECTURE.md updated in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-129 — Where events go: «Сьогодні», the day memory and the next plans

**Description:**
The events reach what she reads, through `agents/chronicle.py`, `agents/plans.py` and the agent's I/O:
- the «Сьогодні» block tells today's events once they have happened;
- the day memory tells them with the details;
- the next plans answer them, and a known event ahead feeds the plans before its date.

A member without `events` gets byte-for-byte the requests she gets today.

**What needs to be done:**
- **«Сьогодні»:**
  - `today_request` gets the day's events so far, as `events_for_prompt` lines under «Що з тобою сьогодні
    сталося», and the task asks for them in «Сьогодні вже…»;
  - `ensure_today` regenerates the block when the hour changes **or** an event has fallen since it was written: the
    tag carries the count of the day's events, and `today_section` still reads any tag of the day.
- **The day memory:** `day_memory_request` gets that day's events and tells them with their details, the heavy ones
  too.
- **The plans:**
  - `plan_request` gets the recent events: yesterday's and today's for the day plan, the period's so far for the
    coarser horizons. A setback may lead to a plan to set it right.
  - It also gets `known_ahead` (AGORA-126) within the horizon: the next 7 days for the day and week plans, the rest of
    the month or year for the coarser ones. They come as dated intentions she expects, never a surprise.
- **Every request:** events reach a prompt only through `events_for_prompt`, never the kind, the source, a marker or a
  link.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Memory (where events go) and §Prompt assembly (the new parts of the today, day-memory and plan
  requests).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-126, AGORA-127

**Expected result:**
An event that falls at 14:00 is in «Сьогодні вже…» at her next answer, in the next day's memory, and in the plan made
after it. A known event shapes her plans ahead of its date, and a surprise never does.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The today block is regenerated after a new event even within the same hour, and only then.
- [ ] The day-memory and plan requests carry the events; the plans carry `known_ahead` and never a surprise before
  its date.
- [ ] No request carries a kind, source, marker or link.
- [ ] A member without `events` sends unchanged requests.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - each request with and without events;
  - the today tag and its refresh;
  - a surprise absent from the plans until its date;
  - no event naming Ich or a member reaches a request (the guard holds end to end);
  - no banned word.

  Gemini is mocked, the clock injected.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** over a day she meets about two events of different kinds, and at most two news items from
  Lviv touch her life. They show in her «Сьогодні», in her answers and in the next day's memory.
