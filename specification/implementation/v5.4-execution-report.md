  and 35 s became 22 s. It also could have cut v5.2's transcripts of long voices.

- **62bd5ca — no spoken time marks** (the owner: «не додавай час перед реплікою голосом Гермеса»). A long silence is
  1.5 s more of quiet; the opening and closing stay.

  - The chat brief carries `RECORD_SKILL` and the time now.
  - Asked in any words, his answer ends with «ЗАПИС: … — …». `recording_mark` reads that line: parsed, its end no
    later than now, at most a day. The line is never sent.
