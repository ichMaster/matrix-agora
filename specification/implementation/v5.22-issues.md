## v5.22 — Analytics: the answer time, the prompts' length, the photos and videos

### AGORA-183 — The call's time: `latency_ms` on every usage line, and the video's frame as its own kind

**Description:**
No usage line holds how long its call took. Every client measures its call on a monotonic clock and every usage line
gains `latency_ms` (usage line v7). The image call for a video's first frame becomes the kind `frame`, so the `photo`
lines are the photos she sent. Touches `agents/usage.py`, `agents/llm.py`, `agents/photo.py`, `agents/video.py`,
`agents/fallback.py`, `agents/claude_sdk.py`, `agents/agent.py` (the sinks, TTS and STT), `agents/herald.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The line:** every `*_usage_line` in `agents/usage.py` takes `latency_ms` (default None) and writes it; it joins
  `COUNT_FIELDS` (an int or null); an older line without it parses as null; `frame` joins `KINDS`.
- **The clients**, each timing its own call from the request to the last byte, failed and blocked calls too, on an
  injectable monotonic clock (`time.monotonic` by default):
  - `GeminiClient` (`agents/llm.py`): `complete`, `probe`, `embed` — passed through `_report` to the sink;
  - `PhotoMaker` (`agents/photo.py`): each `make`, the retry included in its own line; the kind `frame` when the call
    makes a video's first frame (`agents/video.py` asks for it), else `photo`;
  - Omni's clip (`agents/video.py`) and fal's кружечок (from the submit to the result);
  - `FallbackClient` (`agents/fallback.py`): the whole stream;
  - the Claude SDK responder (`agents/claude_sdk.py`): the query to its result;
  - ElevenLabs and Deepgram: timed by their callers in `agents/agent.py` (`record_tts`, the STT record) and
    `agents/herald.py`.
- **The sinks** (`record_usage`, `record_video_note_usage`, `record_fallback_usage`, `record_sdk_usage`, the TTS and
  STT records, Hermes's) take `latency_ms` and pass it on.
- **ARCHITECTURE** §Token accounting: usage line v7 (`latency_ms`), the kind `frame`; §Contracts: the usage line's
  shape.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Every new usage line says how long its call took, and a video's frame no longer counts as a photo.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Each client writes `latency_ms` from its injected monotonic clock, on an ok, a failed and a blocked call.
- [ ] A video's first frame is a `frame` line; a photo or selfie she sends is a `photo` line.
- [ ] An older line without `latency_ms` parses, as null; a non-int is corrupt (skipped).
- [ ] No text in any line or log.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_usage.py` (the v7 shape, the old line), the clients' tests with a fake monotonic clock
  (`tests/test_llm*.py`, `tests/test_photo.py`, `tests/test_video*.py`, `tests/test_fallback.py`,
  `tests/test_claude_sdk*.py`, the TTS and STT paths); google-genai, NovelAI, fal, ElevenLabs and Deepgram mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage line v7 and the kind `frame` in ARCHITECTURE §Token accounting and §Contracts, pinned by
  `tests/test_usage.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-184 — The answer's time: `state/<name>.answers.jsonl`, a line per answer sent

**Description:**
An answer leaves no record. Each answer an agent sends — an Agora line, a private answer in Matrix or Telegram — adds a
line to `state/<name>.answers.jsonl` (0600, no text). Touches `agents/agent.py` (`reply`, `private_reply`, the
private line's time), a small writer and its line in `agents/usage.py` (or `agents/answers.py`).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The line** (pure builder): `ts` (when it went, local ISO), `agent`, `place` (`agora` | `private`), `door`
  (`matrix` | `telegram`), `answered` (`owner` | `agent` | `none` when she writes first), `wait_ms` (from the answered
  message's own time — the Matrix event's `server_timestamp`, Telegram's `date_ms` — to her line sent; null for
  `none`), `delay_ms` (the deliberate pause: v1.2's reply delay, the private chat's «answering (in N s)»), `by`
  (`gemini` | `backup` | `claude`), `parts` (`voice`, `photo` — what went with it).
- **Agora:** `reply()` writes it after `send_line`, from the answered event's time in the timeline (`trigger`, or the
  owner's last message) and the pause it waited.
- **Private:** `private_reply()` writes it once the answer is delivered (Telegram: after the voice and the photo; a
  кружечок sent later is its own `video_note` call, not the answer), `wait_ms` from his latest unanswered line's time,
  `by` from `_private_text`; writing first is `answered: none`.
- **The writer:** appends one line, 0600, never raises into an answer (a failure is logged by type).
- **ARCHITECTURE** §Contracts: `state/<name>.answers.jsonl` in the state files' table.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Every answer an agent sends says how long the owner (or the other agent) waited for it, and how much of that was the
deliberate pause.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A line per answer: Agora and private, Matrix and Telegram, Gemini and the backup; none for a silence.
- [ ] `wait_ms` and `delay_ms` from injected clocks; writing first has `answered: none` and `wait_ms: null`.
- [ ] 0600; no text; a failed write never stops an answer.
- [ ] **Tests:** the builder (pure); the Agora reply and the private answer (nio, Telegram and Gemini mocked, the clock
  injected) each write their line with the right fields.
- [ ] **Contract:** `state/<name>.answers.jsonl` in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, pinned by its test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-185 — The analysis: the calls' and the answers' time, the prompts, the photos and videos; the CLI

**Description:**
A pure analysis over the usage lines and the answers' lines for a period and an agent (or all), beside v5.19's
`analyze` in `agents/usage.py`; and the CLI's new columns in `agents/usage_report.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`speed`:** the calls with a `latency_ms`, by service (`engine`) and model and by kind: calls timed, the median, p95,
  max and mean; by day (by hour when the period is one day); the previous period's median for the change; the slowest
  service's p95.
- **`answers`:** by agent, by place and door, by `by`, his messages and the agents' lines apart: the median, p95 and max
  of `wait_ms` and of `wait_ms − delay_ms`; by day (by hour for one day); the previous period's median.
- **`prompts`:** the mean and the max `prompt_tokens` a call by service, model, kind and agent, by day; NovelAI (no
  tokens) left out; the previous period's mean.
- **`media`:** the ok `photo` calls (photos, her selfies with them) and the ok `video` + `video_note` calls (videos) —
  in all, by day, by hour for one day, on average an hour (the count over the period's hours, today's up to `now`),
  and their cost (from the v5.19 prices); `frame` lines with the videos' cost, never counted as photos.
- Pure: the days and `now` given, nulls skipped, no clock inside; an empty period gives empty blocks.
- **The CLI:** `--by service|model|kind|day|agent` adds the median and p95 call time and the mean prompt tokens.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-183, AGORA-184

**Expected result:**
One function gives the panel and the CLI the period's speed, answers, prompts and media.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The median, p95, max and mean on made-up lines; nulls skipped; by day and by hour for one day; the previous
  period.
- [ ] The answers by agent, place, door and `by`, with the pause apart.
- [ ] The prompts' mean and max by service and kind; NovelAI absent.
- [ ] Photos and videos in all, by day, by hour, and the hourly average with today up to `now`; frames never photos.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_usage_analytics.py` (made-up lines, no clock), `tests/test_usage_report.py` (the new
  columns over a made-up file).
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
