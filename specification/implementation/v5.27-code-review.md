### 1 — The panel's weather view (fixed)
**Scenario:** on 10 November at Paranal the agent saves its weather under «2026-11-10 paranal»; the panel's
`weather_view` asks `load_weather(..., "2026-11-10")` and shows no weather on her card.
**Fix:** `load_weather(..., any_place=True)` takes the day's line wherever it was; the panel uses it. The agent's own
reads stay by day and place, so a move fetches the new place's weather.

### 2 — A day memory across a move (deferred)
**Scenario:** her day memory for 8 November is written at Kyiv's midnight; after she lands in Santiago her days end
at Santiago's midnight, five hours later. The lines between the two midnights belong to a day already written and
enter no day memory. One move, a few hours, and her recall and session summaries still hold them. **Proposed:** let
`days_due` re-open a day whose end moved — only if the owner sees a gap.

### 3 — A hashable config (deferred)
**Scenario:** none today — nothing hashes an `AgentConfig`. A frozen dataclass with a dict field raises on `hash()`.
**Proposed:** a tuple of places, if a cache ever keys by the config.

## What's solid

- A person without places keeps `TIMEZONE` and `LOCATION` exactly (the full suite unchanged, the default place is
  Lviv with its coordinates and news).
- `place_at` reads every move in its own place's zone and a block by the night place's noon; the place is looked up
  once a clock minute and its change is logged («place: road → santiago»).
- His hours stay his: `PRIVATE_HOURS` reads `TIMEZONE`; her waking hours read her place.
- Talk hours are whole hours of UTC, so they stay aligned across Kyiv and Santiago; a `day` overlapping a written day
  by more than two hours is held, a DST neighbour never.
- The new subsections never match the other people's headings («### Місця й враження» stays in the body).
