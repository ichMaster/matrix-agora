## Findings

### #1 🟠 A person not yet in the room is still in the room for the plans

**Where:** `agents/agent.py:2950` (`ensure_plans` → `plan_request(..., others)`), `agents/plans.py:87` (`areas`).

The plan's room is built as `[m.name for m in self.roster.values() if m.user_id != self.cfg.user_id and m.type !=
"herald"]`: it leaves out a herald by his **type**, while every other room list (`self.others`, `members_phrase`,
`named`, the turn pools, the moves) goes by `speakers()` — by **mode** `none`. AGORA-223 made `none` a persona's too,
and the plan's list was not moved with it.

**Failure scenario:**
- Кіра's next day plan (and Ada's, Bruno's, Леон's, Мія's, Ірма's) is asked with «Ich і люди з Агори (Ада, Бруно, …,
  Устина, …) теж можуть бути в планах». A plan item about Устина goes into Кіра's today block and her conversational
  prompts. The canon says she doesn't know of Устина. The phase DoD says Кіра is unchanged and nothing of one reaches the
  other. Plans are never rewritten, so such an item would stay.
- Ada's or Bruno's plan item about Устина would surface in Agora's talk before v6.4, which is where «Agora meets her» belongs.
- Устина's own plan is asked with the room's people, Кіра among them, as people she may plan toward. She has never been
  in the room.

**Proposed fix:** take the plan's room from `speakers()`, as every other list does. For a member whose own mode is `none`,
pass no room at all: `plan_request(..., in_room=False)`, and `areas` then says «Ich теж може бути в планах…»
alone. Regression tests:
- in a roster with a mode-`none` persona, another member's plan prompt never names her;
- her own plan prompt names neither the room nor Агора.

## What's solid

- **The `play` path is gated end to end.** `ГІТАРА:` counts only in capitals, so «Гітара — моє друге хобі» stays a line
  of hers. `_video_closed` turns `play` off for anyone whose TOML names no instrument, so Кіра is never offered it and
  her stray line would be dropped as `off`. Her playing shares the video day and window caps, and the busy flag.
- **The request after the live fix holds no words of hers.** `play_request` is English only: the moment's line, without
  the Ukrainian weather line, and `PLAY_STYLE`. The city comes from `name_en`.
- **Mode `none` for a persona is fenced everywhere else.** `speakers`/`named` drop her from R1/R2, the ask move, the
  name calls and the group address. `self.others` and `members_phrase` leave her out of the room's prompts. Hermes's
  briefs and voice ids go by `speakers`. The events' guard still forbids her names, which is protective.
- **Separation of the two companions.** Her own `ustyna.env` is read by her service alone, never `kira.env`, and
  `tests/test_compose.py` pins that. Her private room, archive, facts, talk and usage files are all keyed by her
  localpart. The deploy's env loop syncs her file. Her password and voice id are in the env examples.
- **The roster validates `play`.** It must be an instrument of `PLAYS`, on a persona with `videos`.

## Fixes applied

### #1 — d7f0f69
- **Change:**
  - `ensure_plans` takes the plans' room from `self.others`, the speakers without herself, as every other room
    list does.
  - A member whose own mode is `none` passes no room and `in_room=False`.
  - `plan_request` and `areas` take `in_room`. When it is False, the plan's line is «Ich теж може бути в планах — як
    твої наміри щодо нього, але нічого не обіцяй за нього.», with no room and no Агора.

  - `test_a_person_not_yet_in_the_room_is_in_no_other_members_plan`: in a roster with a mode-`none` persona, every
    plan prompt of another member names the room without her.
  - `test_a_person_not_yet_in_the_room_plans_toward_ich_alone`: her own plan prompts name neither Агора nor anyone
    from the room.
