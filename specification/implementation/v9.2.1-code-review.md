### 1. Compared before her swaps — `agents/agent.py` `_compose`

**The defect:** her lines enter her window as sent — «ой» cut (`never_open`) and her swaps applied (`swap`, e.g.
«ваще» → «взагалі»). The check ran before both. In a short line where every 8 words in a row hold a swapped word, the
copy did not match.

**Fix:** the check runs after `cut_openings` and `swap_words`, on the text in the form her history holds.

### 2. A deliberate quote of her own (deferred, accepted)

«Я ж казала: «…»» with 8 or more of her own words in a row is dropped as a repeat. That is rare in a live room, and
far cheaper than the loop.

### 3. The private chat (deferred)

Her private answers run on 3.1 Pro with thinking, and no stitching was seen there. `_compose` takes `said` only from
the Agora reply.

## What's solid

- **What is said, not what is known.**
  - `said` holds her own window's lines and the recalled lines that were said.
  - A recalled fact or thought is never in it, so sharing her thought stays hers to do (tested).
  - Another speaker's line in her window is not hers, and quoting it is no repeat (tested).
- **Voice tags are never words.** A tag between copied words does not hide the copy, and a part left with only a tag
  is nothing of hers (tested).
- **The breaks stay.** The kept parts keep the line breaks they had, so her voice and her paragraphs are unchanged.
- **Counts only.** The log says `repeat: N parts dropped`, `repeat: composing once more` and `silent: only what it
  already said`, never a word of the text.
- **One more try, then silence.** A reply that is all repeats is composed once more (one Flash call, about $0.01), and
  a second one is silence, as with the cat's verbatim thesis.
- **The flag between the two compositions.** `_repeated` is set in `_compose` right before it returns and read right
  after, with no await between, so a private answer composing meanwhile cannot flip it.
- **The cat is untouched.** A purr has fewer than 8 words.
