## Findings

### 1. 🔴 A capped probe proves nothing and nobody closes the cause

**Where:** `agents/agent.py` `_private_text` (the window and the window's end: `blocked = … if not probing else []`,
`older = [] if probing else …`) and `probe_blocked`.

**Failure scenario:** a prompt-stage block caused by an older line deep in her 300-line history. The probe spends its
12 calls and, since e76ff2d, closes nothing (`probe: stopped — cap`). The judge rated only his line and her backup
answer, because it left the older lines to the probe. At the window's end Gemini blocks again on the same line, and
the cycle repeats until `PROBE_DAY_CALLS` (60) are spent. Measured with the tests' oracle at cap 12, one blocking line
among 150 items is not proven in 86 positions of 150, and any cause of two lines among 150 never is (it needs 21
calls).

**Fix:** when the probe ends at its cap or on a failed call with nothing closed, `probe_blocked` hands the blocked
prompt's older lines (her open history, the lines recalled in that prompt, her pinned facts) to the judge, as when no
probe runs. A regression test drives a capped probe and checks that the judge gets the older line.

### 2. 🟠 Today's unfinished day and the days before the first line

**Where:** `agents/usage.py` `analyze` (`per_day = t_cost / n`, `trend([x["cost"] for x in day_series])`).

**Failure scenario:** a steady $1 a day, viewed at a time when today has $0.30 so far. «7 days» shows «falling,
−$0.525 a week» and the month at this rate is $27 instead of $30; until about 13:30 every day «7 days» reads
«falling» on a flat spend. An agent that started five days ago reads «rising» on «30 days», since the 25 days before
its first line are zeros in the slope (the moving average already starts at the first line).

**Fix:** `analyze(…, today=None)`. With `today` given, a day not over yet is left out of the cost a day, the month at
this rate and the trend, unless it is the period's only day; and those three start at the agent's first line. The
route and the CLI pass today. Without `today` nothing changes.

### 3. 🟠 Typing a custom date

**Where:** `panel/static/app.js`, the `change` listener on `[data-an]`.

**Failure scenario:** the owner types «2025» into From. After the first «2» Chromium fires `change` with
`0002-11-02`, which passes `ISO_DAY`; the page asks for a span over 366 days (a 400), re-renders and drops the focus,
so the year cannot be finished. The calendar picker still works.

**Fix:** a date field acts only once it is left (`focusout`) or on Enter, never on a `change` while it has the
focus, and a year before 2000 is ignored.

## What's solid

- **The route:** the Bearer middleware runs before routing; the agent resolves only through the registry and never
  reaches a path; a missing day, `since` after `until` and a span over 366 days are 400 with their reason; an
  unreadable `state/` is `{"available": false}`.
- **The numbers:**
  - the previous period has the same length;
  - the 7-day average reads the days before `since` and stops at the first line;
  - the running totals and the least-squares slope (n ≥ 2, no division by zero);
  - the proration across months;
  - a subscription is None, never $0, and each atom is priced alone.
- **The page:**
  - every service, model, kind, agent, error and tooltip value goes through `esc`;
  - the colours come from a fixed table;
  - the hash is validated (`ISO_DAY`, the presets, the agent encoded);
  - the request counter keeps only the latest answer;
  - no NaN or Infinity reaches a path;
  - the panel's today is the server's.
- **The time lines:** they have no id and no name, so they stay out of the probe's ids, the judge's items,
  `gemini_history()`, `_blocked_items` and the recall's query; a probe part drops one with its lines; a pause is
  measured in milliseconds, so a DST switch is right; the hour and day words agree with the number; `TIME_LINE_RE` is
  narrow enough to leave a real reply line alone.
- **The probe at the cap:** only the sets QuickXplain finished proving are closed, a failed call keeps the sets found
  before it, and the route's counter and the log line read `closed=()` rightly.
