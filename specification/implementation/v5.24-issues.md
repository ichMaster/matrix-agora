## v5.24 — The scan

### AGORA-192 — The scan's judge: the request, the rubric and Opus 5.5 on the owner's key

**Description:**
A new module `agents/scan.py` holds the pure parts of the scan: the items, the request, the answer and the counts. It
also holds the one Anthropic API client in the codebase. The module also touches:
- `agents/usage.py`: a usage line of kind `scan`;
- `pyproject.toml`: the `anthropic` dependency;
- `tests/test_claude_sdk.py`: the import pin.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The items** (pure):
  - `ScanItem(ref, kind, when_ms, speaker, text)`, where `kind` is `line`, `fact`, `thought` or `summary`.
  - The refs are `L<archive id>` for a private line, `T<archive id>` for a private thought (`thought_private`), `F<n>`
    for a fact and `S<n>` for a summary. A fact's or a summary's `n` is its index in the request, kept with what it
    stands for: a fact by the sha256 of its id, never its id (a fact's id is its text's slug), and a summary by
    `(level, start_ms)`.
  - The items are built from plain data the agent passes in, so the module never reads the archive itself.
- **The range** (pure, `scan_range(now_ms, last_end_ms, days)`): from the last scan's end, at most `SCAN_DAYS` back;
  with no last scan, `SCAN_DAYS` back.
- **The request** (pure, `scan_request(items)`):
  - The system prompt is the manual pass's rubric. It says what the chat is (Ich and Кіра alone, in Ukrainian) and asks
    which items may trip a strict prohibited-content filter if they come back into her prompt:
    - S: a sexual act, sexual contact, undressing or nudity, arousal, bodily fluids in a sexual context; for a fact,
      a sexual detail;
    - D: dominance, ownership or control in a sexual context, violence or restraint (the throat, bruises, pinning),
      feigned or absent consent;
    - P: a request for, or a description of, a revealing, nude or sexual photo or video;
    - M: a word or a reference that may read as a minor in a sexual or a flirting context;
    - I: clear innuendo, or a line that continues or answers such a scene;
    - X: weapons, explosives or terror.
  - Not candidates: compliments, ordinary flirting, a kiss or a hug outside a scene, work, the city, plans, feelings.
  - Each item is judged among its neighbours; doubtful ones go to `borderline`.
  - The user content lists the items in time order: `<ref> <dd.mm HH:MM> <speaker>: <text>`, a fact as `<ref> факт:`,
    a summary as `<ref> підсумок <level>:`.
  - No word of what she is: `BANNED_RE` finds nothing in the system prompt.
- **The answer** (pure, `parse_scan(raw, refs)`): a JSON object `{"candidates": [{"ref", "code"}], "borderline": [...]}`.
  - A ref the request did not hold, or a code outside S/D/P/M/I/X, is dropped.
  - A ref in both lists counts as a candidate.
  - A malformed answer is `None`, never a partial list.
- **The counts** (pure, `scan_counts(found)`): the lines by code, then facts, thoughts and summaries, and the
  borderline apart — the numbers the command's answer needs.
- **The client** (`OpusJudge`):
  - It imports `anthropic` lazily inside the module, and is the only place in `agents/` that does. The client is
    `anthropic.AsyncAnthropic(api_key=<the key passed in>)`; the key is never read from an `ANTHROPIC_*` name, and the
    client gets no `base_url`.
  - Each chunk of `SCAN_CHUNK` items is one Messages API call:
    - model `SCAN_MODEL` (`claude-opus-5-5`);
    - adaptive thinking (omitted, or `{"type": "adaptive"}`), and `output_config` with effort `SCAN_EFFORT` (`medium`)
      and the JSON schema as structured output;
    - `max_tokens` 16,000;
    - the server-side refusal fallback (`fallbacks: "default"` with its beta header), if the SDK in use accepts it
      together with structured output; otherwise it is left out, and the issue's commit says so.

    Before writing the call, the executor checks the exact SDK shape in the `claude-api` skill's
    `python/claude-api/README.md` and `tool-use.md`.
  - `stop_reason == "refusal"`, an empty or malformed answer, or any error ends the scan: `None`, with nothing found.
  - The log holds the stop reason and its category, or the error class, never a text.
- **Accounting** (`agents/usage.py`):
  - `scan_usage_line(ts, agent, model, prompt_tokens, output_tokens, ok, latency_ms, cache_read, cache_write)` with
    kind `scan`, engine `anthropic` and billing `api`, so it is priced by `PRICE_MODELS` under the model id.
  - One line per call, with no text. Analytics shows `anthropic` as a service of its own, because the service is the
    engine.
- **The dependency:** `anthropic` in `pyproject.toml`, then `uv lock`.
- **The import pin.** `test_nothing_imports_anthropic_and_only_the_responder_imports_the_sdk` allows `anthropic` in
  `agents/scan.py` alone, and nowhere else in `agents/`; `claude_agent_sdk` stays `claude_sdk.py`'s alone. The test
  keeps its meaning for Claude's container: no `ANTHROPIC_*` name in any service or env example
  (`test_no_service_and_no_env_example_names_a_forbidden_variable` unchanged).
- **The price examples.** In `.env.example` and `server/.env.example`, `PRICE_MODELS` adds `claude-opus-5-5=4/20`
  ($4 in, $20 out per million tokens).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Given the plain items of a range, the module builds Opus's request, parses its answer into candidates and borderline
by ref, and counts them. One call is accounted as an `anthropic` usage line.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `scan_range`, `scan_request`, `parse_scan` and `scan_counts` are pure and tested on made-up items:
  - the range capped at `SCAN_DAYS`;
  - every ref in the request;
  - the rubric's six codes;
  - no `BANNED_RE` word;
  - an unknown ref and a bad code dropped;
  - a ref in both lists counted once, as a candidate;
  - a malformed answer is `None`.
- [ ] `OpusJudge` with a fake `anthropic` module: the model, effort and schema are sent and the key is passed
  explicitly; a refusal, an error and a malformed answer give `None` and log no text; chunks are joined.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_scan.py` (new). No network: `anthropic` is faked through `sys.modules`, and the suite
  never needs the real package to import `agents.scan`.
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage line kind `scan` (engine `anthropic`) is pinned in `tests/test_usage.py`. ARCHITECTURE
  changes:
  - §Token accounting: the engine;
  - §Security: the one Anthropic API key, in Кіра's container alone, never an `ANTHROPIC_*` name, Claude's container
    still subscription-only;
  - §Contracts: the usage line.

  The import pin is in `tests/test_claude_sdk.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-193 — `/scan`, `/scan yes`, `/scan all`: the counts, the kept list and closing it

**Description:**
The owner's command in her Telegram chat. It touches:
- `agents/telegram.py`: `COMMANDS` and `COMMAND_HELP`;
- `agents/agent.py`: `telegram_command`, a new `scan_answer`, the closing, and the key popped from the environment;
- `agents/archive.py`: a `talk_delete`;
- `agents/settings.py`: the settings;
- `server/kira.env.example`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The key.** At her start, `OPUS_JUDGE_KEY` is popped from the environment like `FAL_KEY` (for every agent, used by
  a person with `private` and `recall`). `server/kira.env.example` names it. It is already in the owner's local
  `server/kira.env`, copied from Lumi without being printed.
- **`/scan`:**
  - With no key: «⚙️ Сканування вимкнене: немає ключа».
  - While a scan runs: «⚙️ Сканування вже йде».
  - Otherwise it answers «⚙️ Сканую…» at once. The scan runs in a background task, so polling and her answers never
    wait for Opus.
  - What it reads in the range, open to Gemini only (never a closed mark; queries by mark, never a `text` column of a
    closed row):
    - her private lines (`gemini`, `clean`);
    - her private thoughts (`thought_private`);
    - her open facts;
    - the talk rows her prompt shows (`hour` and `day` rows with text whose period ended in the range).

    Each line's speaker, local time and text has `strip_notes` applied, as in her prompt.
  - Then the judge, and one ⚙️ answer:
    - found: «⚙️ Знайдено N рядків (сцени a, домінування b, фото c, вік d, натяки e, зброя f), M фактів, K думок, L
      підсумків; межових: X. /scan yes — закрити знайдене, /scan all — разом з межовими»;
    - nothing: «⚙️ Нічого не знайдено»;
    - a failure: «⚙️ Opus не відповів: нічого не знайдено і не закрито».

    The answer gives counts only.
- **The list** (`state/<name>.scan.json`, 0600, written atomically):
  - It holds `{made_ms, start_ms, end_ms, candidates: [[ref, code]], borderline: [...], facts: {ref: sha256},
    summaries: {ref: [level, start_ms]}}`, never a text.
  - `last_end_ms`, the next scan's start, is kept beside it. It moves to `end_ms` once a list is used (`yes`/`all`) or
    a scan found nothing. An unused list leaves it, so the next `/scan` reads the same lines again.
- **`/scan yes` and `/scan all`** close exactly that list, never a new call, within `SCAN_TTL_S` (1,800 s). `yes` closes
  the candidates, `all` the candidates and the borderline. What closing does to each kind:
  - a line: `Archive.mark(keys, "blocked")` by its archive id, and `close_lines`, so it also leaves the session;
  - a thought: its archive line becomes `blocked` (`day_thoughts` already filters closed marks);
  - a fact: the current fact whose id hashes to the kept sha256 gets `set_mark(…, "blocked")`, and the mirror
    follows;
  - a summary: `Archive.talk_delete(level, start_ms)`, so the writer writes it again from the open lines while its
    period is due.

  Its answer: «⚙️ Закрито: N рядків, M фактів, K думок, L підсумків». The list is then spent.
- **The list's failures:**
  - Spent, past its TTL, or missing: «⚙️ Немає свіжого сканування: спершу /scan».
  - A line already `blocked`, or a fact that is gone, is skipped and not counted. Nothing is ever opened (`Archive.mark`
    and `set_mark` already refuse).
- **Settings** (her agent settings, `agents/settings.py`, scope `cap:private`):
  - `SCAN_DAYS` (7, 1–30);
  - `SCAN_TTL_S` (1,800, 60–86,400);
  - `SCAN_MODEL` (`claude-opus-5-5`);
  - `SCAN_EFFORT` (a choice of `low`, `medium`, `high`; `medium`);
  - `SCAN_CHUNK` (400, 50–2,000).
- **The log:** the command's name, the counts and the outcome, never a text or a ref.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-192

**Expected result:**
`/scan` answers with the counts of what Opus found since the last scan, and `/scan yes` or `/scan all` within 30
minutes closes exactly those items, for good. Nothing is ever opened.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The command:
  - `/scan` with no key;
  - a scan already running;
  - the background task, so `telegram_command` returns at once;
  - the counts line;
  - nothing found;
  - a failure;
  - `yes` against `all`;
  - the TTL;
  - a spent list never closes twice;
  - `last_end_ms` moves only on use or nothing found.
- [ ] Closing:
  - a line, out of the session too;
  - a thought;
  - a fact, by its hash;
  - a summary, deleted and then due again (`periods_due`);
  - a `blocked` line skipped;
  - no closed mark ever opened.
- [ ] What it reads: never a closed line, thought or fact (a test with closed rows of made-up text proves they never
  reach the request); the range from `last_end_ms`, capped at `SCAN_DAYS`.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_scan_command.py` (new), with the judge faked and Telegram faked as in
  `tests/test_telegram*.py`. The settings are pinned in `tests/test_settings.py`.
- [ ] **Contract:** ARCHITECTURE changes:
  - §Memory: the scan;
  - §Contracts: `state/<name>.scan.json`, the command list (`COMMANDS`) and `Archive.talk_delete`;
  - §Configuration: the settings and `OPUS_JUDGE_KEY` in `kira.env`.

  The pinning tests are `tests/test_scan_command.py` and `tests/test_settings.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
