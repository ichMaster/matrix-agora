### AGORA-122: The Lviv weather
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** fa70bb8 · **GitHub:** #122
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **The source.** Open-Meteo's forecast for Lviv, with no key: two days in local time, so the night runs past midnight.
- **The line.** `parse_weather` gives the day's word (from the WMO code), the temperature range, rain, wind, sunrise,
  sunset and the mean night cloud cover (21:00–05:00); a missing field is left out, and no temperature is no line.
- **Once a day.** `ensure_weather` reads `state/<name>.weather.json` (0600, atomic) or makes one request; a failure
  is logged by its type and retried after 10 minutes, and never blocks the mood.
