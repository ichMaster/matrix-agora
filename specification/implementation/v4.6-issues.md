## v4.6 — Ada and Bruno: curiosity, plans and stories

### AGORA-070 — The persona's move: answer, ask, disagree, plan or story — drawn from the event, one prompt line

**Description:**
A pure draw picks how a persona replies this time. It lives in a new module `agents/moves.py`; its prompt line goes
into `build_instruction` (`agents/logic.py`), and `Agent.reply` (`agents/agent.py`) wires it in. The settings are
read only by a `persona`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`agents/moves.py`, pure:**
  - `persona_move(event_id, me, roster, owner_name, weights, *, has_plans, has_life) -> Move(kind, addressee)`.
  - `kind` is one of `answer` / `ask` / `disagree` / `plan` / `story`, chosen by one
    `uniform(event_id, "move:" + me.localpart)` draw over the cumulative weights `ask`, `disagree`, `plan`, `story`;
    the rest is `answer`.
  - `ask` carries an addressee: one of the roster's other members or the owner (`Ich`), never `me`, drawn evenly
    by `uniform(event_id, "ask:" + me.localpart)` over a stable, sorted list.
  - `plan` without plans and `story` without a life fall back to `answer`.
- **`move_line(move) -> str | None`** returns the Ukrainian «Цього разу: …» line, or `None` for `answer`:
  - **ask:** answer briefly, then ask the named member one question from your curiosity, addressing them by name;
    about what interests you in them, never about who or what anyone is;
  - **disagree** (the owner, 2026-10-11: «додай … ще один тип ходу "заперечити"»): don't agree with the message you
    answer — take the other side from your own view, with an argument, with respect, without giving in too easily;
  - **plan:** mention something from «Твої плани» or «Сьогодні» that fits the talk;
  - **story:** tell a short story (2–3 sentences) from «Твоє життя досі» or your memories — only what is there, no
    new fact about the room or the past.
- **The prompt:** `build_instruction(..., move=…)` places the line after the last-session memory and before the
  rules (the freshest instruction). `Agent.build_prompt(move=…)` passes it only for a `persona`.
- **Wiring:** `Agent.reply` draws the move for the event it answers: the trigger for an agent message, the owner's
  latest message otherwise. It logs the kind (`move: ask`), never a text.
- **Settings:** `PERSONA_ASK_P` (0.25), `PERSONA_DISAGREE_P` (0.15), `PERSONA_PLAN_P` (0.1), `PERSONA_STORY_P`
  (0.1), read only by a `persona`; a plain answer (0.4) stays the most common move.
  Their sum is capped at 1; a bad value falls back to the default and is logged by name. Add them to `.env.example`
  and `server/.env.example`. The persona services read the shared `.env`, so compose is unchanged.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Prompt assembly (the move line's place and its three texts), the env table, the
  turn-taking note (a question names its addressee, who always answers; the wave limit and the resume coin bound
  chains).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
About a quarter of their replies ask someone by name and some take the other side; others mention a plan or tell a
story from their life. Every agent would draw the same move for the same event.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The move is deterministic per (event, persona). Over 5000 event ids its rates are within ±2% of the weights.
- [ ] The addressee is never the persona itself, and every other member and the owner are reached.
- [ ] `plan` and `story` fall back to `answer` without plans or a life.
- [ ] The line sits between the summary and the rules, only for a persona. A creature or an assistant never gets
  one, and `answer` adds nothing.
- [ ] No move line names anyone's nature (`BANNED_RE`), and the human-belief rule holds.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_moves.py` covers the distribution, the addressee, determinism, the fallbacks, the lines
  and the settings. `tests/test_logic.py` covers the prompt order with a move. An agent-level test checks that a
  persona's prompt carries the move line for the answered event and that Claude's and the cat's do not. nio and
  Gemini are mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** the prompt order (`tests/test_logic.py`) and the env names (`.env.example` /
  `server/.env.example`, the env test). ARCHITECTURE §Prompt assembly, §Configuration and §Turn-taking are updated.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-071 — Curiosity in Ada's and Bruno's canon — opposite views on the same things

**Description:**
Each persona's canon (`agents/canon/ada.md`, `agents/canon/bruno.md`) gains a «Цікавість» paragraph: what each wants
to know from each member of the room. Their curiosities and views are **opposite**, so the same situation gets two
views and a shared conflict arises (the owner, 2026-10-11: «зроби цікавості Ади і Бруно протилежними щоб виник
спільний конфлікт. на ту саму ситуацію у них різні погляди»). It also gains a line that they share their plans and
tell stories from their own lives.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Opposite lenses** (a «Погляди» line in each canon):
  - **Ада looks inward:** what a thing means, what it remembers, what it costs; she trusts the slow, the old, the
    doubt, the quiet; «навіщо поспішати?».
  - **Бруно looks outward:** how it works, what comes next, where it leads; he trusts action, speed, the new, the
    road; «спершу зробити — потім думати».
  - **Each other:** on the same situation they usually take opposite sides, and each says so. They argue with
    respect and without giving in too easily, and change their mind only for a good reason.
  - **The topics they ask about mirror each other.** The same member gets the inner question from Ada and the
    outer one from Bruno.
- **«Цікавість» for Ада:**
  - the cat: what it is like to remember another life, what he saw as a sysadmin;
  - Claude: his thoughts on language, books, unusual ideas and the future;
  - Bruno: his travels, his music, why he is always in a hurry;
  - Ich: what he reads, how his day went, the stories of people he knows.
- **«Цікавість» for Бруно:**
  - the cat: his sysadmin stories, the technology of the 90s, Linux;
  - Claude: his take on technology, sound, science and far countries;
  - Ada: what she is reading and editing, where her odd ideas come from;
  - Ich: his plans for the weekend, where to go, his music.
- **A shared line in both canons:** you like to share what you plan, and to tell a story from your own life when it
  fits.
- **Keep the voice:** Ada asks one apt, slightly unexpected question; Bruno asks directly, with a joke. Never about
  anyone's nature, never lecturing.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-070

**Expected result:**
The move's «спитай» has something real to ask each member about, in each persona's own voice, and Ada and Bruno meet
the same situation from opposite sides.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Each canon has a «Цікавість» paragraph that names Кіт, Клод, Ich and the other persona, and a «Погляди» line
  with the opposite lens (Ада inward and slow, Бруно outward and fast) and the habit of taking the other side.
- [ ] No canon word calls anyone a model, a bot or an agent (`BANNED_RE`).
- [ ] **Tests:** a canon test checks the «Цікавість» paragraph and its four addressees, and the «Погляди» line, in
  both files, and that the banned vocabulary is absent.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
