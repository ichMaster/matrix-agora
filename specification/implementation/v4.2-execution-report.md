### AGORA-052: The cat's speech: purrs, the ambient reaction, the word cap
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** b2cbbae · **GitHub:** #52
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [x] compose
- **The ambient mode:** named by the owner, the cat answers. Otherwise he reacts on his own deterministic draw
  (`CAT_REACT_P`, doubled when an agent names him), and a reaction is a purr with `CAT_PURR_P`.
- **Purrs** are produced in code at zero tokens. Every agent marks them in its timeline: they don't count in a
  wave, never make anyone answer, are transparent to R3 and the fallback, and collapse in the context.
- **A spoken line** obeys the wave and is capped at `CAT_MAX_WORDS`.
- **Found by the simulation, fixed in the same commit:**
  - **The bug:** a follow-up could overtake the cat's spoken answer to the owner.
  - **The fix:** `owner_speakers` reserves it in the wave like R1's answers.
