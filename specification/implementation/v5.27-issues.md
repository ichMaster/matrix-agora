## v5.27 — Кіра far away

### AGORA-214 — Her place: places and moves, her clock, Lviv's clock, the southern seasons

**Description:**
A person's place moves with her life story. `agents/place.py` (new, pure), `agents/life.py` (two more subsections cut
from a chapter), `agents/config.py` (the TOML's `[places]`), `agents/world.py` (the second clock, the seasons by
hemisphere), `agents/agent.py` (the zone and the location from the place), `agents/kira.toml`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `[places.<key>]` in a TOML: `name` (Ukrainian, her world line), `name_en` (photos), `lat`, `lon` (optional
  together), `tz` (an IANA zone), `news` (bool, default false); `[places] night = "<key>"`; loaded into
  `AgentConfig.places` and `night_place`; a bad zone, a missing name or an unknown night key refuses to start.
- `agents/life.py`: a chapter's `### Місця` (`- YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM · <key>`) and `### Ночі на телескопі`
  (`- YYYY-MM-DD – YYYY-MM-DD`, the first and last night) are cut from the body like `### Події` and kept on the
  chapter (`moves`, `nights`); a bad line refuses to start; config checks every move's key against the places, and
  nights against a night place.
- `agents/place.py`: `Place`; `place_at(story, places, night_place, default, now_ms)` — within a block (from the
  first night's 12:00 to 12:00 after the last, in the night place's zone) the night place; else the latest move at or
  before `now_ms` (its time read in its own place's zone); else `default`. `night_of(story, day)` and
  `next_block(story, day)` for AGORA-216.
- The agent: `self.tz` and `self.location` become the current place's (a property over the clock); `TIMEZONE` stays
  the home clock (`self.home_tz`), used for Lviv's line.
- `agents/world.py`: `now_line(now, location, home=None)` adds «У Львові, де Ich, зараз HH:MM.» (with «вже
  <weekday>» / «ще <weekday>» when Lviv's date differs) when the zones differ; `season(month, south=False)`.
- `agents/kira.toml`: the places `lviv`, `road`, `santiago`, `paranal`, `valparaiso`, `chiloe`; `night = "paranal"`.
- ARCHITECTURE: §World awareness, §Canon (the life story's `### Місця` and `### Ночі на телескопі`; the TOML's
  places).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
On a date of her story's move her prompt's place, clock and season are the new place's, with Lviv's time beside them.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `place_at`: before any move, after a move (in its own zone), within a block and after it.
- [ ] The two subsections are cut from the body; a bad line, an unknown key or a bad zone refuses to start.
- [ ] The world line: one clock at home, two away, the weekday when Lviv's date differs; the southern seasons.
- [ ] A person without places keeps her `TIMEZONE` and `LOCATION` exactly as before.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_place.py`, extended `tests/test_life.py`, `tests/test_world.py`, `tests/test_config.py`.
- [ ] **Contract:** the TOML's places and the life story's two subsections — ARCHITECTURE §Canon, §World awareness.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** after she lands in Santiago, her world line is Chile's time with Lviv's, and spring.

### AGORA-215 — Her world by her place: the weather, her photos' city, the city's news

**Description:**
The world around her follows her place. `agents/weather.py`, `agents/photo.py`, `agents/agent.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `fetch_weather(day, place)` — the place's coordinates, zone and name as the line's label («Параналь: ясно, …»); a
  place without coordinates has no weather; the agent's cache and file are keyed by the day and the place.
- `Moment.city` is the place's `name_en`; the selfie line no longer says «from Lviv».
- Lviv's news is woven into her events only at a place with `news`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-214

**Expected result:**
In Chile her mood reads Chile's weather, her photos are set in Chile, and Lviv's news no longer happens to her.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The weather's URL and label by place; none without coordinates; a new place on the same day fetches anew.
- [ ] The photo's moment names the place's city; the selfie line names no city.
- [ ] No news woven at a place without `news`; Lviv weaves as before.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_weather.py`, `tests/test_photo.py`, `tests/test_events.py` extended.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** her selfies are in Chile; her mood's weather is Paranal's or Santiago's.

### AGORA-216 — Nights at the telescope, when she writes first, and her talk across a move

**Description:**
Some nights she works, and she writes first only when both are awake. `agents/place.py`, `agents/private.py`,
`agents/talk.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/settings.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- Settings `AWAKE_HOURS` (default empty: no check — every other person as before) and `AWAKE_NIGHT_HOURS` (default
  `14-07`), in her clock; on a night of a block she is awake by the second.
- Writing first: `PRIVATE_HOURS` read in his clock (`TIMEZONE`), her awake hours in hers; both must hold.
- Her world line: «Сьогодні в тебе ніч на телескопі.» on a night of a block (and after midnight till 07:00 «Ти зараз
  на нічній зміні біля телескопа.»); otherwise the next block, «Наступні ночі на телескопі — з 8 по 11 грудня.»,
  when one is within 30 days.
- Her talk summaries: a `day` period that overlaps a `day` row already written is held (a move between zones would
  write a second, shifted day).
- ARCHITECTURE: §Configuration (`AWAKE_HOURS`, `AWAKE_NIGHT_HOURS`), §Memory (the talk's held days).

**Dependencies:** AGORA-214

**Expected result:**
On a night at the telescope she writes in his morning; on a normal day never before his 13:00; no doubled day.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Writing first: his hours and her awake hours together; a night of a block opens his morning.
- [ ] The world line's night and next-block sentences.
- [ ] A talk day overlapping a written day is held.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_private.py`, `tests/test_place.py`, `tests/test_talk.py`, `tests/test_settings.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** on a night of a block she writes in his morning; on a day without one, never before his
  13:00.

In Chile she reads and lives the world's news; at home in Lviv, as before.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_place.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
