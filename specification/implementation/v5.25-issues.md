their evening moved into the middle of her messages. In the prompt of her last answer, 8 of the 25 facts about him
were that evening's meeting. It touches `agents/style.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/private.py`, `agents/facts.py`,

### AGORA-198 — Her topics: Flash lists them after each answer, she picks which to go on with, `TOPICS_LINES`

**Description:**
The owner's decision of 2026-11-04: «зроби 2 і додай меседж що вона має вибрати сама тему для продовження. також додай
параметрт на панел щоб визначати зі скільком останіх меседжів брати теми, я пропоную 20». A new pure module
`agents/topics.py`; the call and the list in `agents/agent.py`; the setting in `agents/settings.py`; tests in
`tests/test_topics.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The request** (`topics_request(name, lines)`, pure): the last `TOPICS_LINES` lines of her private chat open to
  Gemini, both sides (`Ich: …`, `Кіра: …`, notes stripped). The instruction asks:
  - the topics their talk is about, at most 6, each a few words as she would say it;
  - for each, how many of her messages and how many of his touch it;
  - their closeness is never a topic;
  - JSON only.

  It names them as two people, never what she is.
- **The answer** (`parse_topics(raw, n)`, pure): `{"topics": [{"topic": "…", "her": 7, "his": 1}]}`. At most 6;
  a topic at most 60 characters; the counts whole numbers from 0 to `n`, a topic with both 0 dropped; a topic that
  `BANNED_RE` catches dropped. A malformed answer is None.
- **The section** (`topics_section(topics, n)`, pure): «Про що ваша розмова (з останніх N повідомлень):», a line per
  topic («- його робота і звіти — ти 7 разів, він 1;»), then `TOPICS_CHOOSE`: «Тему для продовження вибери сама:
  відповідай на те, що він щойно написав, а далі говори про те, що тобі самій зараз цікаве; до того, про що ти вже
  сказала багато, сама не повертайся.» None with no topic.
- **The call** (`Agent.ensure_topics`): after each private answer she sent (Gemini's or the backup's), spawned, one at
  a time; `GEMINI_MODEL` (`llm.generate`, thinking off), kind `topics`. A failure, a block or a bad answer keeps the
  list before it. The log: «topics: N from M lines», never a topic.
- **In her prompt:** her private brief, after the style line, Gemini's and the backup's alike; none before the first
  list or with `TOPICS_LINES` 0.
- **The setting:** `TOPICS_LINES` (20, 0–100; 0 — no list), agent settings, «Особистий чат», `cap:private`.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §Prompt assembly, §Token accounting (`topics`), §Configuration.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Her prompt shows what their last messages are about and who raised each topic, and she picks herself which to go on
with.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The request: open lines only, both sides, the last `TOPICS_LINES`; no word of what she is.
- [ ] The answer: at most 6, the counts bounded, a banned topic dropped, a malformed answer None.
- [ ] A failure keeps the list before it; nothing before the first list; nothing with `TOPICS_LINES` 0.
- [ ] The section and `TOPICS_CHOOSE` in her private brief.
- [ ] No topic in the log.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_topics.py` (fake `generate`, made-up lines only), `tests/test_settings.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests; compose n/a.
