Кіра and Устина keep a checklist of what they arranged with the owner and what they mean to do themselves. They add
and mark items with two new functions of their own on Gemini, their life's day plan gets its outcome after the day, and
the list sits in their prompt with a rule, so an arrangement read in the chat is never taken for a thing done. The
phase builds on v7.6's function calling (`memory_tools`, `ToolSet`, the Gemini cache's tools key) and v9.1's labels
(`stamp`).

**Reconciled with the code (2026-11-19):**
- **Her functions today.** `agents/memory_tools.py` declares `recall` and `day_talk` (`declarations()`, `TOOLS_KEY
  "recall+day_talk/1"`).
  - `Agent._memory_tools(kind)` offers them only for `private` / `private_nudge`, only in `lumi` mode, and only with
    her archive.
  - `llm.tools_for` is that method; the Gemini client runs the rounds (`_recall_rounds`, at most
    `RECALL_TOOL_ROUNDS` 3) and puts `ToolSet.key` in the cache's fingerprint.
  - `_run_memory_tool(name, args)` dispatches by name and never raises.
- **Her answer's route.** The private answer is sent in `private_reply` (then `record_answer("private", door, …,
  by, …)`).
  - `by` is `gemini` for Gemini's answer, `backup` for NovelAI's, and an engine name for the other models.
  - A Gemini answer that blocks goes to the backup, so nothing it asked for may stay.
- **Her life's plans.**
  - Each is a file under `state/<name>.plans/`; the day's is `<date>.md`.
  - Each holds `- item` lines with the hidden `<!-- mutation: kind -->` (`plans.render_plan`; kinds `spontaneous`,
    `place`, `postponed`, `cancelled`, `whim`).
  - `ensure_day_memory(d)` writes a past day's memory in the background tick.
- **The panel** edits her state already: facts are pinned and deleted through `PUT`/`DELETE
  /api/agents/{name}/facts/{id}`, resolved through the registry (`facts_folder`).
- **Prompt order** (`logic.instruction_and_start`): the tail is world → today → digest → thoughts → … → rules.
  v9.1's labels: `world.stamp`.

### AGORA-282 — `agents/checklist.py`: the log, the fold, the statuses, the section and the day plan's outcomes — pure; the `checklist` capability and its settings

**Description:**
The checklist's data and every decision about it, as pure functions over plain data with an injected clock: the
append-only log's entries, folding them into the list, the derived «time passed» state, the prompt section's lines,
and the outcomes of a past day plan from its hidden tags. The agent and the panel only read and append. It also adds
the persona capability `checklist` (needs `private`) and the panel settings `CHECKLIST_DAYS` and `CHECKLIST_N`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The log** `state/<name>.checklist.jsonl`: one JSON object per line, appended, 0600.
  - An `add` entry: `{"ts", "op": "add", "id", "what", "who", "ich", "due", "made_ms", "from", "by"}`.
    - `id` is the next number (the fold's max + 1).
    - `ich` is whether it concerns Ich.
    - `due` is `"YYYY-MM-DD"`, `"YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM"` or null.
    - `from` is the archive key of his line it came from, or null.
    - `by` is `her`, `plan` (her life's day plan, with `"plan_day"`) or `owner`.
  - A `mark` entry: `{"ts", "op": "mark", "id", "status", "due", "by"}`.
    - `status` is one of `done`, `missed`, `cancelled`, `moved`.
    - `due` is the new time for `moved`.
  - Nothing in the log is ever rewritten. A corrupt line is skipped and counted.
- **`fold(entries) -> list[Item]`.** An item has its last status (`planned` until marked) and its current due.
  - A mark of an unknown id is ignored by the fold.
  - `mark_problem(items, id, status, due)` returns why a mark cannot be made (no such number, a bad status, `moved`
    with no time, already closed), in words, for her or the panel.
- **`state_of(item, now)`.** `planned` whose due has passed reads `overdue`: «час минув — не знаєш, чи це було».
  - A due with a day only passes after that day ends.
- **`parse_due(date, time, today)`** reads her function's arguments into `due`. It accepts `YYYY-MM-DD` and
  optionally `HH:MM`; anything else is null and the item stays without a time.
- **`section(items, now, private, days, cap)`** renders the section's lines, newest first within each group:
  - every open item (`planned`, `overdue`, `moved`);
  - then what closed in the last `days` days;
  - at most `cap` lines, the open ones first;
  - each line: «№3 [заплановано] сходити з Ich у кіно — завтра (19.11, 19:00)».
    - The status words: заплановано · час минув — не знаєш, чи це було · перенесено · зроблено · не відбулося ·
      скасовано.
    - The time comes from `world.stamp(fine=True)`: the due, or the made time when there is no due.
  - In Agora (`private=False`) no item with `ich`.
  - None when nothing is left.
- **`plan_outcomes(text, day)`** turns a past day plan's file into entries: each `- item` with its tag, giving
  `postponed` → `moved`, `cancelled` → `cancelled`, anything else → `done`. All carry `by: "plan"` and
  `"plan_day"`. An item's text has its tag stripped.
- **The capability** `checklist`: in `roster.CAPABILITIES` and `PERSONA_ONLY`, with `NEEDS["checklist"] =
  "private"`.
- **The settings** in `agents/settings.py`, scope `cap:checklist`, group `private`:
  - `CHECKLIST_DAYS` (int, 7, 0–60, days);
  - `CHECKLIST_N` (int, 20, 1–100, items).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
The checklist's whole logic exists and is tested, and a TOML may name `checklist`. Nothing reads it yet.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The fold: add, mark, move (new due), cancel, a second mark after a close refused by `mark_problem`, and an
      unknown id ignored.
- [ ] A corrupt log line is skipped and counted.
- [ ] `overdue` derived with an injected clock: a timed due passes at its minute, a day due after its day.
- [ ] The section: open items always, closed ones dropped past `days` and by `cap`, the line format, and in Agora no
      `ich` item.
- [ ] `plan_outcomes` by tag; an untagged item is done; the tag is never in the text.
- [ ] `checklist` without `private` is refused; a non-persona with it is refused.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_checklist.py`, `tests/test_roster.py`, `tests/test_settings.py` — pure, clock injected.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-283 — Her own ways to keep it: `plan_add` and `plan_mark` on Gemini, written only when her answer goes out; the day plan's outcomes after the day

**Description:**
Two more Gemini function declarations in her own words, offered beside `recall` and `day_talk`:
- in both memory modes for a member with `checklist`;
- run by `_run_memory_tool`;
- their writes kept in the answer and appended to the log only when her Gemini answer is sent.

The day plan's outcomes are appended once its day has ended. This touches `agents/memory_tools.py`, `agents/agent.py`
and `agents/llm.py` (only if the tools key needs it).

**What needs to be done:**
- **The declarations** (`memory_tools`), in a person's words, never a tool's, a function's or a model's:
  - `plan_add` — «Запам'ятати, про що ви домовились або що ти сама задумала зробити: що, з ким і коли».
    - Arguments: `what` (required); `who`; `ich` (boolean: whether it is with or about Ich); `date` («РРРР-ММ-ДД»);
      `time` («ГГ:ХХ»).
  - `plan_mark` — «Позначити, як вийшло з тим, що ти планувала: зроблено, не вийшло, скасовано чи перенесено (і на
    коли)».
    - Arguments: `number` (required); `status` (an enum of those four words); `date`, `time` for «перенесено».
- **The tool set.** `_memory_tools(kind)` gives:
  - in `lumi`, `recall` + `day_talk` + the two with `checklist`;
  - in `layered`, the two alone with `checklist`;
  - else as before.
  - Each set has its own `key`, so a cache made with one set never serves another: `recall+day_talk+plans/1`,
    `plans/1`.
- **The runner.**
  - `plan_add` answers «Записала: №N.».
  - `plan_mark` answers «Позначила №N: <status>.», or `mark_problem`'s words.
  - Both never raise.
  - The log line names the call and its outcome (`checklist: add` / `mark done` / `mark refused`), never `what`.
- **Written only when the answer goes out.**
  - Her calls' entries wait in the answer (`self._checklist_pending`).
  - They are appended when her private answer is sent with `by == "gemini"`, and dropped when it is not: blocked,
    the backup, silence.
  - So a closed exchange never adds or marks an item.
  - A mark of an item added in the same answer works, because the fold sees the pending entries.
- **The day plan's outcomes.**
  - In the background tick, for each past day of the last `CHECKLIST_DAYS` whose plan file exists and has no
    `plan_day` entries yet, `plan_outcomes` is appended once.
  - Today's plan never: she never learns an outcome ahead.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-282

**Expected result:**
On Gemini she can add and mark her items while she answers, nothing a blocked or a backup answer asked for is kept,
and yesterday's plan items appear with their outcome.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The declarations: names, arguments, the enum, and no banned word (`BANNED_RE`, no «інструмент», «функці»,
      «модел»).
- [ ] `_memory_tools`: the four sets by mode and capability, and their distinct keys.
- [ ] The runner: add → «Записала: №N.», mark → the status, an unknown number → words, bad arguments → words, never a
      raise.
- [ ] Buffered:
  - [ ] a Gemini answer sent appends the entries;
  - [ ] a blocked answer that went to the backup appends none;
  - [ ] a silent one appends none.
- [ ] The day plan: yesterday's outcomes appended once (a second tick adds nothing), today's never, a day with no
      file nothing.
- [ ] No `what` text in a log line.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_checklist_tools.py` (Gemini mocked: the function-call rounds faked as in
      `tests/test_lumi_recall.py`), clock injected.
- [ ] **Contract:** the declarations and the tools keys — ARCHITECTURE §Memory (her own recall's paragraph) and
      §Contracts, pinned by the test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
