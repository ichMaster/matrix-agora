## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A concurrent call casts the day without its sky and weather — `agents/agent.py:1795`

`ensure_mood` awaits `ensure_sky` and `ensure_weather` **before** it takes the mood's lock. The sky lookup is a
grounded Gemini call of several seconds, and it holds only the sky's lock.

**Failure scenario:** after midnight the watcher calls `ensure_mood`; it is inside the sky lookup. A reply in Agora (or
the private answer) calls `ensure_mood` too:
1. `ensure_sky` sees its lock held and returns None;
2. `ensure_weather` runs, or returns None the same way;
3. the mood's lock is free, so this call takes it and casts the reading with `NO_SKY_RULE` and, possibly, no weather.

The reading is logged and reused all day; the first call then finds the lock held and returns. The cat (v4.2) had
the same race over the sky alone; v5.10 extends it to the weather.

**Fix:** take the mood's lock first and look up the sky and the weather inside it. A call that comes during the
lookups finds the lock held and returns, as it does during the reading itself. The cat keeps his day's sky for his
prompt once the mood exists.

### 2. 🟠 The guard's false negatives and positives — `agents/mood.py:87`

`HOROSCOPE_RE` names the planets but lets the Sun and the Moon through as everyday words. It has no zodiac sign,
aspect, degree or house.

**Failure scenario:**
- «Місяць у Скорпіоні робить тебе глибшою.», «Аспекти дня м'які.», «Сонце на десятому градусі.», «Ти сьогодні
  справжній Стрілець.», «Кон'юнкція тисне.», «У п'ятому домі свято.» all reach «Настрій дня» unchanged;
- `уран\w*` drops «Уранці ти повільна.» and «Уранішнє світло м'яке.», which are ordinary words for the morning.

PERSON_RULE asks the model for none of this, so the guard is the backstop the phase promised («no horoscope word»).

**Fix:**
- add the signs in the form a horoscope uses them (`у/в` + the locative: «у Скорпіоні», «в Овні», …) and the sign
  names that are rare in everyday speech (Стрілець, Скорпіон, Козеріг, Водолій);
- add «аспект», «кон'юнкц», «опозиц», «градус», «планет», «десцендент», and an ordinal before «дім/будинок»;
- the planet Uranus never followed by «ц» or «ішн».

### 3. 🟡 A failing source is retried all day after the mood exists — `agents/agent.py:1799`

The sky and the weather are looked up before the "mood done" check. Her prompt reads only the feeling, never the
sky, so once her mood exists neither is needed.

**Failure scenario:** the sky lookup fails, and her mood is cast without it. The watcher (every ≤30 s) and every
reply call `ensure_mood`, and every 10 minutes the retry gate opens. That is a paid grounded call each time, all day.
A hanging Open-Meteo adds up to 10 s to the reply that triggers its retry.

**Fix:** the fix for #1 does this: a person whose mood exists looks up nothing; the cat still keeps his sky.

### 4. 🟡 The panel shows the raw resolution — `panel/memory.py:80`, `panel/static/app.js` `moodLine`

The card and the Mood tab show the resolution as the reading has it. For Кіра, a sentence the guard drops still
shows, and the owner cannot see what she actually reads.

**Fix (backlog):** for a persona, add the guarded feeling to the view and show it on the card, with the raw
resolution kept in the tab.

### 5. 🟡 No network guard in the tests — `agents/agent.py:372`

`Agent.__init__` sets `self.weather_fetch = fetch_weather`. Every v5.10 test passes a canned fetch, but a future
test of a person with `mood` that forgets it would reach Open-Meteo. The failure is swallowed, so the gate would
stay green.

**Fix (backlog):** an autouse fixture in `tests/conftest.py` that patches `agents.agent.fetch_weather` to fail
loudly.

**Already addressed (v5.11, 6de7e9b):** the autouse fixture `_no_network_fetchers` in `tests/conftest.py` patches
`agents.agent.fetch_weather` and `fetch_citynews` to raise and fails any test that reaches them.

## Fixes applied

Final gates: lint ✓, tests ✓, compose ✓ (unchanged).

### #1 and #3 — the lookups inside the mood's lock (efdac8c)

**The change.** `ensure_mood` checks the day's mood and the lock first, then looks up the sky and (for a person) the
weather inside the mood's lock, after the log is read:
- a call that comes during the lookups finds the lock held and returns;
- a person whose mood exists looks up nothing;
- the cat still ensures his sky once his mood exists (his prompt carries it), so his failed lookup is retried as
  before.

**The tests** (`tests/test_mood.py`):
- `test_a_call_during_the_sky_lookup_never_casts_the_day_without_it` holds the sky lookup open, runs a second call
  meanwhile, and asserts no reading until the lookup ends; the one reading carries the sky and the weather.
- `test_once_her_mood_exists_a_failed_sky_or_weather_is_never_retried`: with both sources failing, an hour later
  there is no second lookup of either.

Both tests fail without the change. The cat's `test_a_failed_lookup_never_blocks_the_mood_and_retries_later` still
passes.

### #2 — the guard (f3a6da4)

**The change.** `HOROSCOPE_RE` gains:
- десцендент, аспект, кон'юнкц (any apostrophe), опозиц, градус and планет;
- the signs in the locative after «у/в» (Овні, Тельці, Близнюках, Раку/Раці, Леві, Діві, Терезах, Рибах);
- the rare sign names (Стрілець, Скорпіон, Козеріг, Водолій) in any form;
- an ordinal before «дім/дому/будинку».

Uranus is `уран(?!ц|ішн)`.

**The tests** (`tests/test_mood.py`):
- 16 new sentences in `test_a_person_never_reads_the_horoscope_a_number_or_a_banned_word`;
- `test_the_mornings_and_homes_words_stay` («Уранці…», «Уранішнє…», «Перший домашній вечір…», «додому», «в русі»,
  «Сонце в очі»).

17 cases fail without the change.
