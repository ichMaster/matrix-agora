### AGORA-117 — The palette and the split: `agents/emotion.py`, the `emotion` capability, `voice_spec`, her prompt block

**Description:**
The pure parts and the prompt: what she may write in brackets, how a reply splits into text and speech, and where
the palette comes from. Touches `agents/emotion.py` (new), `agents/canon/kira.voice.md` (new), `agents/roster.py`,
`agents/config.py`, `agents/agent.py` (`build_prompt`), `agents/logic.py` (`build_instruction`), `agents/kira.toml`,
`tests/test_emotion.py` (new) and ARCHITECTURE §Contracts, §Prompt assembly.

**What needs to be done:**
- `agents/roster.py`: `emotion` joins `CAPABILITIES`, a person's only, and needs `voice` (`NEEDS`); `kira.toml`:
  `emotion = true` and `voice_spec = "agents/canon/kira.voice.md"`.
- `agents/config.py`: an agent with `emotion` loads `voice_spec` (required then; a missing file is a `ConfigError`
  by name) into `AgentConfig.voice_spec` (text).
- `agents/canon/kira.voice.md`: her palette block in Ukrainian, as she is told it — the tags verified on her voice
  (`[starts laughing]`, `[laughs]`, `[laughs harder]`, `[wheezing]`, `[chuckles]`, `[surprised]`, `[thoughtful]`,
  `[hesitant]`, `[whispers]`, `[sighs]`), the budget (one or two a reply, most replies none), the placement (before
  the clause; laughter climbing over short clauses, never spelled out next to a ladder), and nothing in brackets but
  these. No word says she is a model or a bot; the brackets are «позначки для голосу».
- `agents/emotion.py` (pure):
  - `TAG_RE`, a bracket of lower-case words at most 40 characters;
  - `split_tags(reply) -> (text, speech)`: `speech` is the reply as written; `text` has every tag removed with
    nothing in its place and the spaces tidied, while a bracket that is not a tag (`[1]`, `arr[i]`, `[ОСТРІВ 3]`)
    stays in both;
  - `TAG_MODELS` = `eleven_v3`, `eleven_v3_conversational`, `eleven_v4`, `eleven_v4_turbo`.
- The prompt: `build_instruction` gains `voice` (the palette block), placed just before the rules; `build_prompt`
  passes it only for `emotion`, in Agora and in the private chat. ARCHITECTURE §Prompt assembly records the place.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Кіра's prompts carry her palette, and `split_tags` turns a tagged reply into her plain words and the speech.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `split_tags` handles a known tag, an invented one, a ladder, a non-tag bracket, extra spaces and a reply of
      tags only.
- [ ] The palette block reaches only an agent with `emotion`, in both of her prompts; it holds no banned word.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_emotion.py` (the split's cases, `TAG_MODELS`), the roster rule (`emotion` needs
      `voice`, a person's only), the config's `voice_spec` (loaded, missing → error), the prompt's place.
- [ ] **Contract:** the capability, the TOML key and the prompt order — ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and §Prompt
      assembly, pinned by `tests/test_emotion.py` and `tests/test_roster.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-119 — Tags to the voice, words to the text: the split on every path

**Description:**
One reply, two renderings, wherever her line goes: the synthesiser gets the speech, everything else the words.
Touches `agents/agent.py` (`_compose`, `send_line`, `send_telegram`), `agents/emotion.py`, `tests/test_emotion.py`
(or `tests/test_voiced_lines.py`) and ARCHITECTURE §Voice, §Message flow.

**What needs to be done:**
- `_compose` (after the marker's split, v5.8): for an agent with `emotion`, `split_tags` the reply; it returns the
  words as a `Spoken` (a `str` that also carries `.speech`), so every caller that stores or compares the line —
  history, the session, the archive, the facts' extraction, the duplicate check — gets the words alone, unchanged.
  A reply of tags only is silence.
- **The safety rule:** when the agent's model is not in `TAG_MODELS`, `.speech` is the words — a tag never reaches
  a model that would read it aloud.
- `send_line` and `send_telegram` synthesise `getattr(text, "speech", text)` and send the words as the message
  (`m.text`; Telegram's `sendMessage`); `voiceable` and the monthly budget count the speech (the characters billed).
- A line that goes out as text only (her share, the cap, no voice id) sends the words.
- Hermes's recording is unchanged: her voiced lines come as audio, her text has no tags.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-117, AGORA-118

**Expected result:**
Her voice notes laugh and sigh where she wrote so; nobody ever reads a bracket.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] In Agora, the Element private chat and Telegram, the synthesiser gets the tags and the message does not.
- [ ] Her history, the session, the archive and the other members' transcripts hold no bracket.
- [ ] A tag-less model never receives a bracket; a text-only line sends the words.
- [ ] **Tests:** with ElevenLabs and nio mocked: each path's synth input vs message; the stored line; the safety
      rule; a reply of tags only; the budget counting the speech.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
