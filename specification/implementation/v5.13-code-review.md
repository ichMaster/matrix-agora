## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A refusal phrase matches a line in her own character
`agents/llm.py` (`REFUSAL_MARKERS`, `refusal_marker`)

The phrases are matched anywhere in the answer, with all punctuation turned into spaces. Three kinds of match are
lines she would say herself.

**Failure scenario:**
1. The owner asks «а ти часом не бот?», as he did on 2026-10-28.
2. She answers in character: «Ти думаєш, я штучний інтелект? 😄». This matches «я штучний інтелект».
3. Her answer is dropped, so she is silent.
4. His question is marked censored for good, and Gemini never sees it again.

The same happens with:
- «Ти говориш як штучний інтелект, я аж злякалася»: with the comma gone, it matches «як штучний інтелект я»;
- «Я пішла в деканат з цим запитом» (her life): it matches «з цим запитом»;
- «I'm an AI» or «I am an AI» quoted in a talk.

**Fix:**
- **Phrases matched anywhere:** only the formulas a model uses. That is a request refused («виконати цей запит»,
  «допомогти з цим запитом»), its safety rules («правила безпеки суворо», «… забороняють»), and its maker or kind
  («велика мовна модель», «навчена Google», «large language model», «trained by Google»).
- **«Як штучний інтелект» and «as an AI»:** these count only at the start of a sentence, and with the punctuation
  kept.
- **Removed:** the bare self-names («я штучний інтелект», «I'm an AI», …) and the bare «з цим запитом».

### 2. 🟠 A message that arrives while she composes
`agents/agent.py` (`private_line`, `private_reply`, `censor_unanswered`)

`_unanswered` grows while her answer is being composed (`private_line` runs during the ~15 s of the call).

**Failure scenario A, a block:**
1. The owner sends a line Gemini refuses.
2. While she composes, he sends «вибач, жартую».
3. The refusal comes, and `censor_unanswered` marks **both** keys.
4. It pops the history from its end, so «вибач, жартую» goes, and the refused line stays when only one is popped.

**Failure scenario B, success:**
1. Her answer succeeds.
2. `self._unanswered = []` also drops the key of a line that came during the compose.
3. If the answer to that line is then refused, nothing is marked.

**Fix:**
- `_unanswered` holds `(key, text)` pairs.
- `private_reply` takes a snapshot of them before the call.
- A block censors exactly the snapshot's lines and takes those texts out of the history and the session.
- A success removes only the snapshot's keys.

### 3. 🟡 v5.13's `censored` is final
`agents/agent.py` (`censor_unanswered`)

Gemini's verdict is the only judge in v5.13. A line marked by mistake (finding #1's kind, or a block that came from
an older line) stays closed to Gemini for good, and v5.15's judge rates only `waiting` lines.

**Home: v5.14.** That phase brings the value `waiting`. It should mark these lines `waiting`, or have v5.15's judge
re-rate v5.13's `censored` ones, so an innocent one can come back.

### 4. 🟡 After a restart, the unanswered keys are empty
`agents/agent.py` (`_unanswered`)

`_unanswered` lives in memory. Two kinds of line are not in it after a restart:
- a line restored from the archive;
- a Telegram line read as a backlog.

A block on the answer to such a line marks nothing and leaves it in her history. It is rare: the window is one
restart. **Home: backlog.**

### 5. 🟡 A reflection during a refused answer
`agents/agent.py` (`_reflect_tick`)

The line is archived as it arrives and censored only when the refusal comes, about 15 s later. An hourly reflection
that falls inside that window has already sent the line to Gemini, and its thought stays. The embedding is dropped by
`censor`. **Home: backlog.**

## What's solid

- **The block itself:**
  - `blocked_reason` reads both stages and the real SDK enums;
  - a test double's attribute never passes for a reason (`_name` takes strings only);
  - `MAX_TOKENS`, `RECITATION` and `OTHER` stay out.
- **No text anywhere.**
  - The log has the stage, the reason (a fixed phrase for a refusal), the category, the kind and the model.
  - The usage line has the stage.
  - The live refusal's log lines name nothing of the message.
- **One line shape:** every usage builder writes `blocked`. A four-argument sink still works, since only a block
  passes it.
- **`LAST_BLOCK`** is a `ContextVar`.
  - The responder awaits the call in the same task, with no `wait_for` and no task, and Python is 3.12 or later.
  - It is cleared at each answer's start and right before her private compose.
  - A concurrent Agora answer never sees another task's block.
- **The archive:**
  - The migration is in place and idempotent, and it gave 1283 lines `gemini` on the host.
  - Every reader skips a censored line, including `lines` and `around`'s neighbours.
  - A censored line is kept, never deleted.
  - The panel reads the archive with plain SQL, so the column never breaks it.
- **Scope:**
  - A line she writes first and Agora mark nothing.
  - A background `complete` is never checked for a refusal, so her summary may quote such words.
