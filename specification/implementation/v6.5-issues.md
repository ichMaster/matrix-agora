derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v6.5 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-231 →
**AGORA-232…237**).

The owner switches Устина's photos between Google's image model and Seedream 4 on fal.ai, with fal's safety checker
off, by `/photos fal|google` in her Telegram chat. Under the switch her backup model (GLM-4.6 on NovelAI) writes the
English request for Seedream around the code's own lines (her face, her adult age, her look), and the backup's
private answers may carry her photo too. It builds on v5.17's photos, v5.18's fal client (`NoteMaker`), v5.14's backup
(`FallbackClient`) and v6.2's selfie line; the video's first frame stays on Google.

### AGORA-232 — The money: the fal photo's usage line and `PRICE_IMAGES`

**Description:**
A fal photo is paid per image, not by tokens or seconds. Add a usage line for it, a price per image by model, and the
kind of the backup's call that writes the request; the report, the panel's usage view and Analytics price and count
them. Touches `agents/usage.py`, `agents/usage_report.py`, `panel/memory.py`, `server/docker-compose.yml`,
`.env.example`, `server/.env.example`, ARCHITECTURE.md.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Usage line v8:** every line carries `images` (an int, or null): `image_usage_line(ts, agent, model, images, ok,
  blocked, latency_ms)` — kind `photo`, engine `fal`, billing `api`, no tokens, `images` 1 for a photo fal returned
  (0 or null otherwise), `blocked` `answer` for a flagged or black one. Every other builder writes `images: null`; a
  line without it reads as null; a non-int is corrupt (`COUNT_FIELDS`).
- **The kinds:** `photo_prompt` — the backup writing her photo's request (a `fallback_usage_line`, engine `novelai`,
  the subscription, never priced).
- **`PRICE_IMAGES`:** comma-separated `model=usd` a photo (`parse_image_prices`, the same shape as
  `parse_second_prices`; a malformed entry ignored with a warning, never fatal), e.g.
  `fal-ai/bytedance/seedream/v4/edit=0.03,fal-ai/bytedance/seedream/v4/text-to-image=0.03`.
- **The price:** `Row` gains `images` and `images_by_model` (the `api` images per model); `cost()` prices them by
  `PRICE_IMAGES` — a model with no price → None («—»), never $0; `PriceBook.images`; `_atom_cost` treats an atom with
  images as billable; `_Sum.totals` gains `images`.
- **Analytics' Media:** the fal `photo` lines count as photos (ok ones) and their cost joins the photos' cost.
- **Readers:** `usage_report.py` and `panel/memory.py` read `PRICE_IMAGES` from the environment as they read
  `PRICE_SECONDS`, with the same warning; compose passes `PRICE_IMAGES: ${PRICE_IMAGES:-}` to the panel; both
  `.env.example` files carry the entry above.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md:** §Token accounting (usage line v8, `photo` on fal, `photo_prompt`), the `PRICE_IMAGES` row in
  §Configuration, the env-var and compose contracts.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
A fal photo's usage line is written, counted as a photo and priced at $0.03 by `PRICE_IMAGES` in the report, the
panel and Analytics.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `image_usage_line` writes kind `photo`, engine `fal`, billing `api`, `images`, no text; every other builder writes
      `images: null`; an old line without it parses.
- [ ] `PRICE_IMAGES` parsed, malformed entries reported; a fal photo priced by it, one with no price → None.
- [ ] Analytics' Media counts and prices the fal photos with the Google ones.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_usage.py` (the builder, `_valid`, the parse, `cost` with images, an unpriced model),
      `tests/test_panel_analytics.py` (Media's photos and cost), `tests/test_compose.py` (the panel's environment and
      the example's `PRICE_IMAGES` parsed).
- [ ] **Contract:** the usage line (v8 `images`, kind `photo_prompt`) and the env var `PRICE_IMAGES` — ARCHITECTURE.md
      and the tests above in the same commit.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests, compose.

### AGORA-233 — The fal maker: Seedream 4 Edit and text-to-image

**Description:**
Her photo on fal.ai beside `PhotoMaker`: a selfie on Seedream 4 Edit with her reference faces, a photo without her on
Seedream 4 text-to-image, the safety checker off; a flagged, black or empty photo is `blocked`. The fal plumbing
(queue, upload, download, cancel, the key's host guard) is shared with her кружечок's `NoteMaker`, not copied. Touches
`agents/video.py` (the shared base), a new `agents/falphoto.py`, `tests/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The shared fal client:** `NoteMaker`'s `_queue` / `_upload` / `_again` / `_download` / `_cancel` move to a base both
  makers use; the key goes only to `queue.fal.run` and fal's upload host, never to a file URL; `NoteMaker`'s
  behaviour and tests unchanged.
- **`FalPhotoMaker(key, sink, rng, faces, main_face, timeout_s)`**, `make(shot, prompt) -> Made`:
  - a selfie: her faces picked as now (`pick_faces`: her main face and three drawn) and put on fal's storage, then
    `fal-ai/bytedance/seedream/v4/edit` with `image_urls`; no faces → `blocked`, as Google's;
  - a photo without her: `fal-ai/bytedance/seedream/v4/text-to-image`, no reference;
  - the body: `prompt`, `image_size` `{"width": 1152, "height": 1536}`, `num_images` 1, `enable_safety_checker: false`;
  - one retry on a 5xx, a dropped connection or a timeout before fal accepts the job, never after; a job that timed out
    is cancelled, as `NoteMaker`'s;
  - `blocked` when `has_nsfw_concepts` holds a `true`, when the image is black (Seedream names no flag: its mean
    brightness under 3 of 255, measured by ffmpeg as an 8×8 grey frame; ffmpeg failing is logged and the photo
    counts as not black), or when no image comes back; any other failure is `failed`, never a crash.
- **The usage:** each try one `image_usage_line` through the sink (the model's id, `images`, ok, blocked, latency).
- **The log:** the route (`fal`), the model, the outcome and the time — never the prompt, a URL or the key; `repr`
  without the key.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-232

**Expected result:**
Given a prompt, the maker returns her photo from Seedream (with her faces for a selfie) or `blocked` / `failed`, with
fal mocked in every test.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A selfie goes to the edit model with her faces' URLs, a photo to text-to-image with none; the body holds
      `enable_safety_checker: false` and 1152×1536.
- [ ] `has_nsfw_concepts: [true]`, a black image and an empty answer → `blocked`; an error after the job is accepted
      is never retried.
- [ ] `NoteMaker`'s tests pass unchanged on the shared base.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_falphoto.py` with a fake fal session (the bodies, the models by kind, the outcomes, the
      retry rule, the key only to fal's hosts, `repr`), the black check with ffmpeg mocked.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-234 — The request, by the backup

**Description:**
Under the switch her `СЕЛФІ:` / `ФОТО:` line and her moment go to her backup model, which writes the shot in English
for Seedream; the code wraps it in the lines that never depend on the model. Without the backup, or when it fails, the
request is the template. Touches `agents/photo.py` (pure builders), `agents/agent.py` (the call), `tests/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Pure, in `agents/photo.py`:**
  - `SCENE_INSTRUCTION` (English): write one description of this phone photo for an image model — the scene, her pose
    and the look on her face, her clothes named (to suit the weather and the scene unless her line names them), the
    light and the framing of a phone snapshot; plain prose, 60–150 words, no names, no lists, no text on the image;
  - `scene_input(shot, moment)`: the shot's kind, her line as she wrote it and `moment_line(moment)`;
  - `scene_check(text) -> str | None`: the model's text cleaned (a `<think>` block cut, trimmed, at most 1,500
    characters at a sentence), None when empty or not mostly Latin;
  - `fal_request(shot, moment, scene, birth, look) -> str`: for a selfie `SELFIE_HEAD`, «She is an adult woman, N years
    old.» (her age on the shot's day), her look, `SELFIE_TAIL`; for a photo the no-face line; then the scene (or,
    without one, her line and `moment_line` as `photo_request` puts them); then `STYLE`. Her age and face line are
    there whatever the scene says; without a birth date it is never built for a selfie.
- **The call, in `agents/agent.py`:** `_fal_prompt(shot, moment) -> str` — with her backup client (`FALLBACK_MODEL`
  and her token; `FALLBACK_ROUTE` governs her answers, not this) `reply(scene_input, SCENE_INSTRUCTION, 400,
  kind="photo_prompt")`, checked; else or on failure `fal_request` without a scene.
- **Never read, never kept:** what the backup writes is the backup's talk — never logged, never in a file, never in a
  test but made-up lines. The log says only `photo: the request by the backup (N chars)` or `photo: the request by the
  template (no backup | failed | not english)`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-232

**Expected result:**
`_fal_prompt` returns the backup's scene between the code's own lines, or the template, and logs only the route and
the length.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The request holds the face line, «adult woman» and her age, and her look for a selfie, whatever the scene.
- [ ] The template when there is no backup, it fails, or it answers in another script.
- [ ] The usage line of the backup's call is kind `photo_prompt`; no text of it in the log (caplog).
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_photo.py` (the pure builders and `scene_check`), `tests/test_photo_route.py` or a new
      test file (the call with `FallbackClient` mocked, made-up lines only).
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

**Acceptance criteria:**

      restart (a new agent reads it).
- [ ] Under `fal` a photo goes through `_fal_prompt` and the fal maker, a frame through Google.
- [ ] The menu holds `photos` only for a person with `photos_fal`.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_roster.py` (the capability, `NEEDS`, `natal` required, Устина's TOML with it, Кіра's
