## Findings

### 1. 🟠 The cap counts only the videos kept — `agents/agent.py` `videos_today`

**Scenario:**
- `videos_today()` counts the files in `state/kira.videos/<day>/`. A file is written only once Telegram took the video.
- Say fal makes the кружечок (billed, about $1) but `square_note` fails: an ffmpeg codec is missing, or the file is odd.
  Her voice note goes instead, and no file is kept.
- She is asked again and again. Each time fal bills, nothing is counted, and `VIDEO_DAY_MAX` never closes.
- VISION names this cap as a cost guard, the second exception to «budgets».

**Fix:**
- the cap counts what was paid: today's `ok` lines of the kinds `video` and `video_note` in her usage file, or the
  files kept, whichever is more;
- a test: a paid кружечок whose cut fails still counts toward the cap.

### 2. 🟠 A poll error resubmits a paid job — `agents/video.py` `NoteMaker._run`, `ClipMaker._run`

**Scenario:**
- `make()` wraps the whole submit → poll → fetch in one try, and retries the whole of it once on a 5xx or a timeout.
- A 502 on one status poll, after fal accepted the job, submits a second job. fal finishes and bills both. Omni Flash
  does the same with a second interaction.

**Fix:**
- once a job is submitted, a retryable error on a poll or on fetching the result polls again, within the same timeout;
- only the submission itself is retried;
- tests: a 5xx on a poll, then success, makes one submission.

### 3. 🟠 The кружечок's sound is only fal's — `agents/video.py` `square_note`

**Scenario:**
- `square_note` re-encodes fal's video with `-c:a aac`. If the input has no audio stream, ffmpeg writes a video without
  one, and nothing fails.
- fal's model page lists the output as `video` and `duration` only. Whether the mp4 carries the driving audio is not
  documented.
- If it does not, the owner gets a silent кружечок, and the DoD's «she says it in her voice» fails with no error in the
  log.

**Fix:**
- `square_note(mp4, mp3)` maps fal's video and her own mp3 (`-map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0`), so her ElevenLabs voice is
  always the sound, exact and in time;
- a test of the argv.

### 4. 🟡 No time in the log — `agents/agent.py` `_video_after`

The DoD asks for the time each video took. The log has `video: sent (note, telegram)` but not when the making began.

**Fix:** `video: sent (note, telegram, 143 s)`, the seconds from the start of `_video_after`, with no text.

### 5. 🟡 An unbounded download (DEFER → backlog)

`NoteMaker` reads `video.url` whole with `resp.read()`, from whatever host fal names. A cap of a few dozen megabytes
and fal's own storage host would bound it. Nothing visible changes.

### 6. 🟡 A restart loses her held voice (DEFER → backlog)

While a кружечок is made, her voice is held only in memory. A deploy or restart in those minutes cancels the task. Her
text was sent, but her voice note never is. Writing the held voice to disk would cover it, which is rare and minor.

### 7. 🟡 The log names a video line a photo (DEFER → cleanup)

`_compose` logs any media line in the backup's answer as `photo: dropped (backup)`. A `КРУЖЕЧОК` line reads as a
photo there. Only the log is affected.

## Fixes applied

1. **#1 — 9fcd44c** `fix(agents): her videos' day cap counts the ones paid for`.
   - **The change:** `videos_today` takes today's ok `video` / `video_note` lines in her usage file or the files kept,
     whichever is more (`USAGE_KINDS` in `agents/video.py`).
   - **The test:** `test_a_video_paid_for_counts_toward_the_cap_even_when_it_did_not_go`.
2. **#2 — 62d3e3e** `fix(agents): a server error while a video is made asks again, never resubmits`.
   - **The change:** each maker retries only its submission. Once fal or Omni Flash accepted the job, a 5xx or a dropped
     connection on a poll, the result or the download asks again within the same timeout. A job that never ends is
     not sent again.
   - **The tests:** `test_a_server_error_while_waiting_asks_again_and_never_resubmits` and
     `test_a_note_that_never_ends_is_never_resubmitted`; `test_a_clip_that_never_ends_is_a_timeout` now pins one
     submission.
3. **#3 — 0d98e65** `fix(agents): her own voice is her кружечок's sound`.
   - **The change:** `square_note(mp4, mp3)` maps fal's video and her mp3 (`-map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -shortest`).
   - **The test:** the argv test checks the maps.
   - **Checked with the real ffmpeg on the Mac:** a silent 720×1280 clip and a tone gave a 3 s 640×640 H.264 file with
     an AAC track. The agent image has `libmp3lame` and `libx264`.
4. **#4 — 874f591** `fix(agents): the log says how long her video took`.
   - **The change:** `video: sent (note|clip, telegram, N s)` and the fallbacks' lines carry the seconds since the
     making began (her injected clock).
   - **The test:** `test_the_log_says_how_long_a_video_took`.

  - for a кружечок her text goes at once and her voice is held, and any failure sends the held voice note;
  - for a clip her answer goes as before, and a failure draws one short line or nothing.
- **Gemini's alone:**
  - the skill is offered only in Gemini's prompt in Telegram, for the kinds open now;
  - the backup's line is dropped;
  - a closed door, the cap or a busy maker drops the line, and the reason is logged.
- **One at a time, in turn:**
  - `_video_busy` closes the skill while a video is made;
  - the failure line waits for her answers and hands an owner's line that came meanwhile to `private_later`.
- **The key:**
  - popped at start for every agent, kept only in `NoteMaker` (whose `repr` hides it);
  - sent to fal's queue host only, never to its storage.
- **The money is counted:** `video_ms`, `PRICE_SECONDS` and an unpriced «—», in the report, the daily report and the
  panel.
- **The privacy holds:** the files are kept 0600 in 0700 folders, the square cut's temporary folder is removed, and no
  description, request, URL or key reaches a log.
