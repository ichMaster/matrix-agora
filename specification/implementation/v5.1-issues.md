# v5.1 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.1 — Hermes: voice digests for the owner** (version **v5 — Voice: Hermes, the agents hear and
speak**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.1 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-078 →
**AGORA-079…086**).

Hermes is a new member of the Agora simulation of a new type, `herald`. He has no turns and never speaks in Agora. He
reads the room word for word from the homeserver. On a schedule, or when the owner asks, he tells the owner what
happened as a voice message in his own room with the owner, with the text alongside. He also answers the owner's
questions about the room's history in text. The voice is ElevenLabs (Lumi's key), encoded to OGG/Opus by ffmpeg and
sent as an Element voice message. Hearing voice (v5.2) and the agents speaking (v5.3) are later phases.

**Owner decisions:**
- From the ROADMAP (2026-10-13):
  - Hermes alone makes the digests;
  - the digests go to a private room with the owner;
  - he is silent in Agora;
  - every 4 hours, with `gemini-3.1-pro-preview` and thinking;
  - the length follows the talk, at most ~1,500 characters;
  - Lumi's ElevenLabs key;
  - the monthly character cap.
- At this phase's start (2026-10-17):
  - scheduled digests only in the local hours **09–23** (`HERMES_DIGEST_HOURS`); «підсумок» works at any hour;
  - the minimum of new messages is **5** (`HERMES_DIGEST_MIN_MESSAGES`).

**Reconciled with the code and the live setup (2026-10-17, read-only):**
- **The account already exists.** `@hermes:agora.lan` answers on the profile API, and the owner has invited him into
  Agora (Element shows him as *Invited*). `server/hermes.env` (gitignored, 0600) holds `HERMES_PASSWORD` only. So the
  ROADMAP's owner step "his Matrix account" is done; joining Agora is the runtime's job (the pending invite).
- **Lumi's voice code** (`~/development/lumi/voice/tts.py`) uses the `elevenlabs` SDK with Lumi's default model
  `eleven_multilingual_v2`, the SDK's default output (MP3), Lumi's voice settings (stability 0.5, similarity 0.75,
  style 0.4) and `ffmpeg … -c:a libopus -b:a 32k -f ogg` for Telegram. It computes no duration and no waveform. Its
  streaming twin calls the REST endpoint directly (`xi-api-key`, `output_format=pcm_24000`).
  - The port uses that REST shape through `aiohttp` (already installed with matrix-nio) and asks for PCM. That makes
    the duration and the waveform pure arithmetic before the Opus encode, and adds no SDK.
  - The model is the owner's `eleven_turbo_v2_5`, not Lumi's default.
- **Lumi's key names:** `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` (v5.2) are set in `~/development/lumi/.env`.
  Lumi's voice id variable is `LUMI_VOICE_ID`; Hermes gets his own voice id.
- **Gemini 3.x Pro with thinking:**
  - `agents/llm.py` sends `thinking_budget=0` on every call, and Pro models reject that.
  - `usage_line` counts `candidates_token_count` only, so thinking tokens (billed as output) would go uncounted. Lumi
    has the same gap.
  - The installed google-genai is 2.28.0.
- **The roster's turn pools** (`agents/turns.py`) select by an explicit `mode` (`ranked`, `mention-only`, `ambient`).
  A new mode value is therefore in no pool. Today `parse_member` defaults a TOML without `[turns]` to `ranked`.
- **The speaking agents' allowlist** is `{OWNER} ∪` every other roster member. Without a change, a herald would be
  allowlisted in Agora.
- **The settings schema** (`agents/settings.py`):
  - `concerns()` makes a `shared` setting concern every member, so a turn setting would restart Hermes too;
  - `engine:gemini` (`GEMINI_MODEL`) would concern him, although he does not use it.
- **Prices:** one `PRICE_*` pair (Gemini Flash's) prices every `api` row, so Hermes's Pro calls would show at Flash
  prices.
- **VISION's non-goals** still list "media (images, voice messages) — text only". v5 reverses that for voice messages.

**Description:**

- **`agents/canon/hermes.md`**, in Ukrainian, from the ROADMAP's character:
  - the messenger god and patron of the agora: quick, witty, a little vain, telling the day like news from Olympus,

  - how he tells a digest: spoken prose for the ear, no lists, no markdown, no emoji, numbers as words; he names

  transcript line shows «Гермес».
- **`agents/settings.py` `concerns()`:** a herald is concerned only by settings scoped to one of its capabilities
  (`cap:<c>`), never by `shared`, `type:` or `engine:`. A turn setting or `GEMINI_MODEL` then never restarts him.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - §Contracts: the TOML schema (`herald`, `digest`, the `none` mode), the registry's agents and the allowlist rule;
  - §Canon: Hermes's canon and why it has no `common.md`;
  - §Turn-taking: the `none` mode.
- **VISION:**
  - the glossary: Herald, Digest;
  - the Principles: Hermes is openly not human and never speaks in Agora; the personas get no description of him.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
The roster knows Hermes as a silent herald. Every speaking agent ignores his user id and never chooses him, and no

### AGORA-080 — The digest's pure core and the Pro call with thinking

**Description:**
Write the digest's decisions and prompts as pure functions in a new `agents/digest.py`, and add a thinking-capable
Gemini call to `agents/llm.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`agents/digest.py` (pure, clock injected):**
  - **The state, `state/<name>.digest.json`:**
    - `room`: the private room's id;
    - `point_event`, `point_ts`: the newest Agora message the last digest covered;
    - `last_ms`: the last digest sent, scheduled or asked;
    - `day`, `count`: digests sent that local day;
    - no texts.
    - `load_digest_state` / `save_digest_state` write atomically, 0600; a corrupt or missing file is an empty state,
      logged.
  - **`digest_due(now_ms, local_hour, last_ms, every_ms, hours, new_count, min_new)`:** at least `every_ms` since
    `last_ms` (none: due at once), inside `hours`, and `new_count ≥ min_new`. `hours` reuses `parse_hours` from
    `agents/nudge.py`: start inclusive, end exclusive, wrapping past midnight.
  - **`since_point(events_newest_first, point_event, point_ts, fallback_from_ms)`:** the Agora text messages after
    the point, oldest first.
    - Without a point (the first run) it takes the last `every_ms` window.
    - It stops at the point's event id or at an older timestamp.
    - Purrs (`is_purr`) are dropped.
    - It is bounded by `COLLECT_MAX` (1,500) messages.
  - **`digest_lines(messages, names, tz)`:** `"HH:MM Name: text"` in `TIMEZONE`. The owner is «Ich»; every other
    name comes from the roster.
  - **`target_chars(n_messages, max_chars)`:** `min(max_chars, 300 + 40 × n)`. A few lines give about half a
    minute; 30 or more give the cap. It is pinned.
  - **`trim_digest(text, max_chars)`:** a text over the cap is cut to its last full sentence within the cap (reuse
    `trim_to_sentence`); no full sentence means empty.
  - **`asks_digest(text)`:** a short owner message, at most 3 words, holding «підсумок», «підсумки» or «підсумуй»
    (any case, punctuation ignored).
  - **`DIGEST_BRIEF` and `ASK_BRIEF`** (Ukrainian), with the builders `digest_request(canon, lines, period,
    target, members)` and `ask_request(canon, lines, question, members)`.
    - Both name the members by name from the roster and say «не обговорюй, хто вони і яка їхня природа».
    - The digest tells what was talked about, who thought what, where they argued, what they asked Ich and what was
      settled, in about `target` characters, as speech.
    - The ask brief answers only from the lines, briefly, and says plainly when the lines don't hold the answer.
    - Neither brief holds a `BANNED_RE` term.
  - **`ask_window(now_ms)`:** the last `ASK_DAYS` (7) days, at most `ASK_MAX` (2,000) newest messages. The lines
    carry `DD.MM HH:MM`.
- **`agents/llm.py`:** `GeminiClient.think(contents, system_instruction, model, max_output_tokens, kind)`.
  - Thinking stays on: no `thinking_budget=0`; `include_thoughts=False`.
  - The answer comes from the non-thought parts.
  - `max_output_tokens` leaves room for the thinking (the default is 16,384).
  - The usage sink gets the call's own model, not `GEMINI_MODEL`.
  - On failure or an empty answer it returns None, logged by type.
  - The digest model is the constant `DIGEST_MODEL = "gemini-3.1-pro-preview"`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-079

**Expected result:**
Every digest and question decision is a tested pure function, and the Pro call with thinking is one method that
counts its usage under its own model.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_digest.py`; Gemini mocked in `tests/test_llm.py` or the existing LLM tests):
  - the schedule: interval, hours (incl. `09-23` and a wrap), minimum, no `last_ms`;
  - the collection: since the point, the first run's window, purrs dropped, the bound;
  - the line format and owner naming, and `target_chars`;
  - the trim to a sentence and `asks_digest` (yes: «Підсумок!», «дай підсумок»; no: a long question that mentions
    «підсумок»);
  - the state: atomic, 0600, corrupt → empty;
  - the briefs hold no banned term;
  - `think()` sends no zero thinking budget, returns the non-thought text, and reports `DIGEST_MODEL` to the sink;
    a failure → None.
- [ ] **Contract:** `state/<name>.digest.json` in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and the state table, pinned by the state
  test.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-081 — The voice: ElevenLabs TTS, Opus through ffmpeg, the duration, the waveform, the voice-message content

**Description:**
Port Lumi's text to speech into a new `agents/voice.py`, encode it as an Element voice message, and put ffmpeg into
the agent image (`agents/Dockerfile`). Update VISION's non-goal on voice messages.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`ElevenLabsTTS(api_key, voice_id, model="eleven_turbo_v2_5")`** with `async synth(text) -> bytes | None` (PCM
  s16le, 24 kHz, mono):
  - `POST https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/text-to-speech/{voice_id}?output_format=pcm_24000` with the header
    `xi-api-key`;
  - the JSON body: `text`, `model_id`, `language_code="uk"` and Lumi's voice settings (stability 0.5, similarity
    0.75, style 0.4);
  - through `aiohttp`, with a 60 s timeout;
  - any failure → None, logged by its type or HTTP status only;
  - the key and the text never reach a log.
- **`async encode_opus(pcm) -> bytes | None`:**
  - a fixed argv run with `asyncio.create_subprocess_exec`: `ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -f s16le -ar 24000
    -ac 1 -i pipe:0 -c:a libopus -b:a 32k -application voip -f ogg pipe:1`;
  - a timeout;
  - a non-zero exit → None, logged by its code.
- **Pure helpers:**
  - `duration_ms(pcm, rate=24000)`;
  - `waveform(pcm, points=100)`: the peak per bucket, scaled to 0..1024, as Element draws it.
- **`voice_content(mxc_url, size, duration_ms, wave, text, filename)` (pure)** returns the `m.audio` content:
  - `body` = the text, plus `filename` (`hermes-YYYYMMDD-HHMM.ogg`);
  - `url`;
  - `info {mimetype: "audio/ogg", size, duration}`;
  - `org.matrix.msc1767.audio {duration, waveform}`;
  - `org.matrix.msc3245.voice {}`;
  - `org.matrix.msc1767.text`.
- **`agents/Dockerfile`:** the `agent` target installs `ffmpeg` (`--no-install-recommends`, apt lists removed). Every
  agent image shares the layer, and v5.3 needs it for every speaker. The `claude` target inherits it.
- **ARCHITECTURE:**
  - the network table and the tech stack: outbound HTTPS to ElevenLabs, its credits shared with Lumi;
  - the voice-message format.
- **VISION:** the non-goal becomes "images, calls and E2EE" (voice messages arrive with v5). The Principles name
  ElevenLabs as the second paid dependency.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Text in, an Element voice message's bytes and content out. Every external step is mockable and silent on failure.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_voice.py`, `aiohttp` and the subprocess mocked):
  - the request's URL, headers and body, with the key absent from every log record;
  - a non-200 → None;
  - the ffmpeg argv is exactly the fixed one, and a failure → None;
  - `duration_ms` and `waveform` (length 100, range 0..1024; silence → zeros);
  - `voice_content` carries the voice flag, the duration, the waveform and the body text.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_image.py` pins ffmpeg in the agent target.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-083 — Hermes's runtime: the private room, the schedule, «підсумок», questions, silence in Agora

**Description:**
Write the herald's process in a new `agents/herald.py`, dispatched from `agents/agent.py` `main()` by the type. Add
his settings to the schema in `agents/settings.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **`agents/agent.py` `main()`:** a `herald` config runs `Herald(cfg).run()`. It keeps the same lock, logging and
  `settings_start`, so the compose entrypoint stays `python agents/agent.py agents/hermes.toml`.
- **The shared Matrix plumbing:** move `join_room` (the `'{}'` body workaround) and the session login into helpers
  both classes use. Behaviour is unchanged and the existing tests stay green.
- **`Herald.run()`:**
  - log in once and reuse the token;
  - the first sync is never processed;
  - join Agora on the owner's (pending) invite; leave every other invite;
  - **the private room:** from the state's `room`. When it is absent or no longer joined, `room_create` (preset
    `trusted_private_chat`, `is_direct`, name «Гермес», invite `OWNER`) and save its id.
- **Seeding the count:** after the first sync, one bounded collection since the point seeds `new_count`. The sync
  callback then counts each new Agora text message (not his own, not a purr). There is no log line per room
  message.
- **The allowlist:**
  - in the private room, only the owner's `RoomMessageText` is handled;
  - everything else is logged as `ignored` with a reason;
  - in Agora nothing is ever answered;
  - every send goes through one `send_private()` that refuses any room but the private one. A test pins that no
    path sends into `ROOM_ID`.
- **The watcher** (every 60 s): `digest_due(…)` → `digest()`. A failed attempt retries after 10 minutes (RAM), never
  in a loop.
- **`digest(asked)`:**
  1. Collect from `/messages` (paged backwards, bounded) since the point.
  2. Nothing new: when asked, send one short fixed line in his style; never call the model.
  3. One `think()` call (kind `digest_voice`) with typing on in the private room (reset in `finally`), then
     `trim_digest`. When the guard trips (`outs_a_persona`), regenerate once, then drop with a log line.
  4. Voice, unless:
     - `HERMES_VOICE_ID` is empty;
     - `month_chars + len(text)` would pass `HERMES_TTS_MONTHLY_CHARS`;
     - TTS, encode or upload fails.
     The voice path is `synth`, then `encode_opus`, then the upload (`client.upload`, `audio/ogg`), then a
     `tts_usage_line`, then the `m.audio` from `voice_content`.
  5. Send the text as an `m.text`, headed by a code-made line «Агора, 10:05–14:10 · 23 повідомлення». Element
     Desktop does not show a voice message's body.
  6. Save the state: the point = the newest collected event, `last_ms`, `day`/`count`.
  7. Log `digest: sent (N messages, M chars, voice | text: <reason>)`, never a text.
- **«підсумок»** from the owner → `digest(asked=True)` at any hour, whatever the minimum.
- **Any other owner message** in the private room is a question:
  - collect the `ask_window`;
  - one `think()` (kind `ask`) with typing on;
  - the guard (regenerate once, else silence and a log line);
  - send the answer as an `m.text`.
- **The settings schema** (`agents/settings.py`): service `matrix-chat`, scope `cap:digest`, group `hermes`
  («Гермес» in `SERVICE_GROUPS`).

  | Name | Kind | Default | Bounds |
  |---|---|---|---|
