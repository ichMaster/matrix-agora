### AGORA-138: The block named
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 8faf6b7 · **GitHub:** #138
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **`blocked_reason(resp)`** (pure) names the stage:
  - the prompt stage: any `prompt_feedback.block_reason`;
  - the answer stage: `SAFETY`, `PROHIBITED_CONTENT`, `BLOCKLIST` or `SPII`.

  It also gives the reason, and the category of the blocked or most probable safety rating. `MAX_TOKENS`,
  `RECITATION`, `OTHER` and no reason are not a block.
- **The log:** `complete`, `reply` and `think` log a block with no text and still return None.
- **Usage line v5:** `blocked` is on every builder, so the line shape stays one. Only a block passes `blocked=` to
  the sink. The agent's, Hermes's and the facts seed's sinks take it.
- **Reconciled:** «the caller can tell a block» was first left to v5.14. AGORA-142 brought it in as `LAST_BLOCK`.

### AGORA-141: A refusal in text is a block
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 955c680 · **GitHub:** #141 (added 2026-10-28 after the owner's live test)
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests · [ ] compose (n/a)
- **Why it was added:** the owner tested a weapons request, then a CSAM request. Gemini 3.1 Pro answered both with a
  text refusal, and the API flagged no block. The second answer was «Я не можу виконати цей запит. Мої правила
  безпеки суворо забороняють…». It went out in voice and text, out of her character.
- **`refusal_marker`** (pure) uses `REFUSAL_MARKERS` in Ukrainian, Russian and English. It applies to every answer
  through `GeminiClient.reply`, on both paths. A background `complete` is never checked.
- **A refusal** is never sent, and it is logged and counted as the stage `refusal`.
