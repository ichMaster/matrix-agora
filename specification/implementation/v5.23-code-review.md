## Findings

### 1. 🔴 Recall cannot bring back the lines just outside the window — `agents/agent.py:1234`

**Failure scenario:**
- `_gemini_private_prompt` tells recall to skip `set(self.private_history) | set(self.gemini_history())`.
  - `private_history` is the backup's view: the last `HISTORY_N` (300) lines of every mark.
  - Before v5.23 that matched Gemini's window. Since AGORA-188 the window is the last 24 hours (about 150–180 lines).
- So a line between the window's start and 300 lines back is neither in her prompt word for word nor reachable by
  recall. `around()` drops such a line as a neighbour too.
- **On an average day** (about 178 lines), 300 lines cover about 40 hours, so the lines from 24 to 40 hours ago
  cannot be recalled.
- **In a quiet week**, 300 lines span more than 7 days. A line older than `TALK_DAY_DAYS` then has no summary, is
  outside the window and is skipped by recall, so nothing of it reaches her prompt.
- ROADMAP §v5.23 says recall works as now for every line outside the window.
- **Confirmed** on made-up lines:
  - he named a line from 40 hours ago;
  - with her exclude set, the recall section was `None`;
  - with the window alone, it returned the line and its neighbour.

**Fix:** skip only the window, by the archive's raw text (`pick_recall` compares `line.text`, before `history_entry`
and `strip_notes`), together with the closed pairs `_closed`, as before. Without the archive, skip her own history
as before. A test: a line 40 hours old, named by his question, is recalled, and a line in the window is not.

### 2. 🟠 The full stop lands inside a tag or a quantity — `agents/emotion.py:62`

**Failure scenario:**
- `closed_line` takes the last `\w` anywhere in the line, including inside a tag that is not at the very end.
  Then it steps over closing quotes and brackets only.
- **Ran:**
  - `«Ну ти даєш [laughs] 😂»` → `«Ну ти даєш [laughs.] 😂»`: the tag is broken and her words still have no mark.
  - `«так [sighs, softly] 🙂»` stays without a mark, because the comma inside the tag reads as the line's mark.
  - `«Надворі +5°»` → `«+5.°»`, `«Знижка 50%»` → `«50.%»`, `«100₴»` → `«100.₴»`.
- A tag followed by her emoji is a common shape of her lines, and her mood line carries the Lviv weather.

**Fix:**
- Find the last word and the mark after it with the tags blanked out, so nothing inside a tag counts.
- Then step over every non-space sign attached to the word that sits below the arrows block (U+2190): `°`, `%`,
  `₴`, `€`, quotes and brackets. The stop still lands before an emoji.
- Add the cases above to `test_every_line_of_her_speech_ends_with_a_mark_never_inside_a_tag`.

### 3. 🟡 An hour can fall between the two levels — `agents/talk.py:61`

**Failure scenario:**
- An hour is due only while `start >= now − TALK_HOUR_DAYS`, and its day only once the day ends before that cut.
- An hour not written while it was inside the band can therefore wait up to a day, until its whole day passes the
  cut. Its lines are then in neither the window nor the summaries. This happens after a container's downtime, after
  a Pro outage, or on the deploy's own first day.
- When the day is written at last, it is written from the hours it has. A day with gaps loses what those hours
  held.
- The test documents it: «11-02 09:00 … not yet due».

**Fix:** an hour is also due while its day ends after the cut. A test: that hour is due, and its day is not.

### 4. 🟡 The fallback window keeps the notes — `agents/agent.py:1364`

**Failure scenario:**
- Without the archive, or when it fails to read, `gemini_history_keyed` returns her own `private_history` untouched.
- An old `[селфі: …]` note then reaches Gemini. That note was the cause of the night's blocks, and ROADMAP §v5.23
  says the notes are cut out before any prompt.

**Fix:** the fallback goes through `strip_notes`, and a line left empty is dropped. A test without the archive.

## Architecture impact

- **§Memory (#1):** recall in her private prompt skips only the lines of Gemini's window and the closed pairs, never
  the backup's 300 lines.
- **§Memory (#3):** `periods_due` makes an hour due while its day is not yet past the band.
- **§The voice (#2):** `closed_line` steps over the signs attached to the last word, and a tag's words and marks never
  count.
- **§The panel (#5):** the Talk tab's levels.

## What's solid

- **`window_start`:** pure, and it counts real hours, so a DST night is right (with a test). The order is sound:
  the 24 hours, then the floor of 20 lines, then his unanswered lines, then the owner's `PRIVATE_HISTORY_N` cap.
  The backup's 300-line view is untouched and tested.
- **The summary levels:** `period_of("day")` cuts at local midnight, DST-safe. `talk_section` and `periods_due` use
  one age rule (`end > now − day_days`). Only `hour` and `day` rows are shown. A day with text hides its hours, and
  an empty day hides nothing. A day falls back from its hours to its four-hour blocks to its lines.
- **The photo retry:** it clears `_pending_shot` and resets `LAST_BLOCK` on each call. `mark_unanswered` and
  `route_step("block")` run only on the second outcome, so the hour's block count is not doubled.
- **`strip_notes`:** it reaches every prompt path that reads the archive: the window, the recall groups, the talk
  items, the facts, the session summary, the reflection and the backup's history. It leaves a line without a note
  unchanged.
- **`HUMAN_MEMORY`:** it says only what she is (checked against `BANNED_RE`) and goes only to a persona's summaries.
