## Findings

### 1. 🟡 Uncounted silences — `agents/bridge.py` `reply`
**Scenario:**
- R1 chooses her for an unnamed owner line while Lumi is away (health down) or the token is refused. `reply` returns
  at `_lumi_closed()` before any turn, and `_count` never runs.
- The same happens for the closed check after the pre-turn flush, and for a trigger no longer in the room's window.
- So on a day Lumi is down, her card reads «0 silent» while she misses every turn chosen for her. That is exactly what
  the owner's check («about as often as the others») needs to see.
**Fix:** count `silent` at those three exits too. A chosen turn is counted once, whatever came of it.

### 2. 🟡 A test that claims more than it checks — `tests/test_turns.py`
**Scenario:** `test_a_ranked_bridge_who_stays_silent_never_grows_the_wave_and_the_fallback_stands_in` builds a `silent`
list and asserts `len(silent) <= len(sent)`, which is always true. Her pass is never simulated: `simulate` lets her
speak like everyone else.
**Fix:** `simulate(…, silent={"lili"})`. A silent member's turns fire, but she posts nothing: no line in the timeline,
nothing in `sent`. The test asserts the bound with her silent, and that she never appears in `sent`.

### 3. 🟡 The fallback of a fallback — `agents/turns.py` `fallback_replier`
**Scenario:** R1 chose two members, both stayed silent (a PASS each), and the best-ranked unchosen member is Лілі. If
she is away or passes too, no one answers the owner. This needs three silences in a row, and the room's Gemini agents
rarely pass on an owner line.
**Home:** backlog. A second fallback would be a turn-taking change (a contract), and shared turn-taking cannot know
another process's health.

## What's solid

- **The modes.** A bridge is `ranked` or `mention-only`; `ambient` and `none` are refused. Her weight of 1 puts her among
  the owner's repliers about as often as each person. The pins were moved, not loosened, and the share bounds keep
  Claude's lower weight checked.
- **The fallback turn** answers its own owner line (`fallback_for`), never a newer one. The test proves it fails without
  the fix.
- **A pass.** Lumi's `pass` is silence. Agent's `reserved()` holds her place only until `FALLBACK_S`, then the room's
  fallback answers, so the room never waits on her.
- **R3 before the call alone.** A fallback turn that lands after a slow chosen agent's answer is the documented slow-engine
  trade-off (ROADMAP v8.2), not a defect. Dropping it after the call would need a retract for nothing the room did wrong.
- **The `ask` move.** Ranked, she is asked like any member, and a mention-only member stays out (its own test).
- **The counts** hold no text, roll at the room's midnight and survive a restart (state file).
