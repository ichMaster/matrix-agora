# v5.18 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.18 — Кіра's videos in Telegram: a кружечок in her voice and short clips** (version **v5 — Voice:
Hermes, the agents hear and speak**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.18 and the
contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase
(AGORA-162 → **AGORA-163…166**).

Кіра sends the owner two kinds of video in her Telegram chat, on her own and in her answers:
- **a кружечок**: she says her answer in her own voice, her face from the reference photos, her lips in time;
- **a short clip** of what she sees, with the sound of the place and no speech.

A last line of Gemini's answer names it (`КРУЖЕЧОК:` or `ВІДЕО:`), as v5.17's `ФОТО:` / `СЕЛФІ:` does. Each video's
first frame is her photo, made by v5.17's image model. The кружечок is made by Kling AI Avatar v2 on fal.ai from that
frame and her ElevenLabs voice; the clip by Gemini Omni Flash from its frame. Both take minutes, so they go after her
answer, in the background.

The phase builds on:
- v5.7: her Telegram door;
- v5.9: her voice with tags, the voice note before the text;
- v5.10: the Lviv weather of her day;
- v5.13/v5.14: Gemini's answers vs the backup's;
- v5.17: the marker line, the image model, her faces, the photo's archive note.

Videos anywhere else are not in this phase.

**Owner decisions** (ROADMAP §v5.18, 2026-11-01):
- both kinds, before v6;
- VISION's «images» non-goal narrowed again, to her videos in Telegram.

**Confirmed at generation** (the owner's «/ship-phase v5.18» on the proposal and the defaults named to him on
2026-11-01):

- `VIDEO_DAY_MAX` 4 (both kinds together);

  `[фото: …]` / `[селфі: …]` (`NOTE_RE`, v5.17 review #2), and returns the last shot. `_compose` runs it only on her
  private answers: Gemini's (`shots` given) and the backup's, whose shot is dropped (v5.17 review #1).
  - `_gemini_text` sets `_pending_shot`, and `private_reply` reads it only when `by == "gemini"`.

  - the input image item is `{"type": "image", "data": <base64>, "mime_type": …}`.
- **fal (Kling AI Avatar v2):**
  - the queue API (`https://queue.fal.run/<model>`; `Authorization: Key <FAL_KEY>`): submit, poll the status URL,

  - the inputs `image_url` and `audio_url` (data URIs) and `prompt`;
  - the output `video.url` and `duration`;
  - `enable_safety_checker` is on by default.

## v5.18 — Кіра's videos in Telegram: a кружечок in her voice and short clips

### AGORA-163 — The video seam: the markers, the clip request, Omni Flash and Kling Avatar on fal (`agents/video.py`)

**Description:**
The pieces that make a video, without the flow:
- the two new marker kinds in `agents/photo.py`;
- the frame's aspect in `PhotoMaker`;
- a new `agents/video.py` with the clip's request, the two makers and ffmpeg's fixed argvs.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The markers** (`agents/photo.py`):
  - `KINDS` gains `КРУЖЕЧОК` → `note` and `ВІДЕО` → `clip`, in `_MARK` and `NOTE_RE` (the longer words first);
  - `NOTES` gains `кружечок` and `відео`;
  - `split_photo` returns the last media line of any kind: one media line per answer, the last one counts;
  - `photo_note` writes `[кружечок: …]` / `[відео: …]`.
- **The frame:** `PhotoMaker.make(shot, moment, aspect="3:4")`:
  - a кружечок's frame is a `selfie`-style request at `1:1` (a front-camera close-up, her faces);
  - a clip's frame a `photo`-style request at `9:16` (nobody's face).

  A pure `frame_shot(shot)` maps `note` → `selfie` and `clip` → `photo`, so `photo_request` is reused unchanged.
- **The clip's request** (`clip_request(shot, moment)`, pure, English):
  - her description, as in `photo_request`;
  - the moment line (the part of the day, the season, the time, Lviv unless named, the weather);
  - `CLIP_STYLE`: «a handheld phone video, natural motion, the sound of the place only — no dialogue, no voice, no
    music; no text; nobody's face».
- **`ClipMaker`** (Gemini Omni Flash through the SDK; `VIDEO_MODEL` default `gemini-omni-1.1-flash`):
  1. `client.aio.interactions.create(model, input=[image item from the frame, text item], response_format={"type":
     "video", "aspect_ratio": "9:16", "resolution": <setting>}, generation_config={"video_config": {"task":
     "image_to_video"}}, background=True)`;
  2. poll `interactions.get(id)` every `POLL_S` (10 s) until a final status, within `TIMEOUT_S` (600 s);
  3. take the video from `output_video` (inline data, or its URI fetched through the SDK, whichever 2.28 returns).
  - **Errors:** one retry on a server error or a timeout. `failed` with a safety reason, or no video, is `blocked`.
    Any other failure is `failed`, never a crash.
  - **Usage:** the sink gets kind `video` with `Interaction.usage` mapped to the Gemini names
    (`total_input_tokens` → prompt, `total_output_tokens` → output).
- **`NoteMaker`** (Kling AI Avatar v2 on fal; `VIDEO_NOTE_MODEL` default `fal-ai/kling-video/ai-avatar/v2/standard`;
  through a `ClientSession` seam, as `agents/judge.py`):
  1. submit to `https://queue.fal.run/<model>` with `Authorization: Key <key>` and `{"image_url": <data URI of the
     frame>, "audio_url": <data URI of the mp3>, "prompt": NOTE_PROMPT}`;
  2. poll the status URL every `POLL_S` until `COMPLETED`, within `TIMEOUT_S`;
  3. fetch the response, then download `video.url`.
  - `NOTE_PROMPT` is a short English line: «she talks to the front camera, natural small head movements».
  - **Errors:** one retry on a 5xx or a timeout. A 4xx that names a content or safety check is `blocked`; any other is
    `failed`.
  - **Usage:** the sink gets kind `video_note` with the seconds (fal's `duration`).
  - The key, the data URIs, the status and result URLs never go into a log.
- **ffmpeg, fixed argvs, never a word of hers in them:**
  - `pcm_to_mp3(pcm)`: s16le 24 kHz mono → mp3, through stdin/stdout;
  - `square_note(mp4)`: the centre square cut, scaled to 640×640, H.264 + AAC, at most 60 s, `+faststart`, the size the
    caller needs for `length`. It runs through temporary files in a 0700 temporary folder, removed after.

  Each returns `None` on a failure, logged by the exit code.
- **The result:** `Video(data: bytes | None, mime: str, seconds: float | None, why: "ok" | "blocked" | "failed")`.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
Given a shot, her moment, her frame and (for a кружечок) her voice's PCM, `agents/video.py` returns a video or why
there is none. No flow is changed yet.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `split_photo` cuts `КРУЖЕЧОК:` / `ВІДЕО:` lines and their kept notes, in any case where v5.17's markers are cut,
  and the last media line of any kind names the shot.
- [ ] `frame_shot` and `PhotoMaker.make(…, aspect=…)` give a 1:1 selfie frame for a кружечок and a 9:16 faceless frame for
  a clip.
- [ ] `clip_request` holds the description, the moment and `CLIP_STYLE`, and the template is English.
- [ ] `ClipMaker` polls to a final status, retries once on a server error or a timeout, and returns `blocked` /
  `failed` as a reason. Its usage line is kind `video` with the tokens.
- [ ] `NoteMaker` submits the data URIs, polls and downloads, retries once on a 5xx or a timeout, and returns `blocked`
  / `failed` as a reason. Its usage line is kind `video_note` with the seconds.
- [ ] ffmpeg's argvs are fixed, and the temporary files are removed.
- [ ] **Tests** (`tests/test_video.py`; google-genai, aiohttp and ffmpeg mocked, no network; made-up descriptions):
  - the markers and the notes;
  - the frame mapping and the aspect in the config;
  - the request;
  - the clip's polling, retry, block and failure;
  - fal's submit/poll/fetch, retry, block and failure;
  - no key, URL, description or request in a log.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
