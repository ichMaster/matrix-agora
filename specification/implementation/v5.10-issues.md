## v5.10 — Кіра's mood of the day

### AGORA-122 — The Lviv weather: `agents/weather.py` (Open-Meteo, once a day, `state/<name>.weather.json`)

**Description:**
A new module, `agents/weather.py`, fetches the day's Lviv weather from Open-Meteo (no key), turns it into one short
Ukrainian line for the mood reading and keeps it for the day in `state/<name>.weather.json`. Pure but for the HTTP
call and the file I/O, as `agents/sky.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `weather_url(lat, lon, tz)`: Open-Meteo's `/v1/forecast` with `daily=temperature_2m_max,temperature_2m_min,
  precipitation_sum,wind_speed_10m_max,sunrise,sunset,weather_code`, `hourly=cloud_cover`, `timezone=Europe/Kyiv`,
  `forecast_days=2`. Lviv's coordinates are constants (49.84 N, 24.03 E).
- `parse_weather(data, day) -> str | None` (pure): the day's minimum and maximum temperature, the rain, the wind,
  sunrise and sunset, and the night's cloud cover (the mean of 21:00–23:00 that day and 00:00–05:00 the next).
  - Output: one line, e.g. «Львів: від +3 до +9 °C, дощ 4 мм, вітер до 18 км/год, схід 07:31, захід 16:52, уночі
    хмарність 85 %».
  - A missing field is left out of the line. An answer with no temperature for the day gives None.
- `fetch_weather(...)`: one `aiohttp` GET with a 10 s timeout, as `agents/guardian.py`. Any failure (network,
  status, JSON) logs its type and gives None, never an exception.
- `load_weather(path, day)` / `save_weather(path, day, line)`: the same shape and rules as `load_sky`/`save_sky` —
  `{"day", "weather"}`, atomic, 0600, a corrupt or old file reads as None.
- In `agents/agent.py`: `weather_file` and `ensure_weather(now)`, as `ensure_sky`:
  - the file first, then one fetch;
  - a failure means no weather and a retry after 10 minutes, never a blocked mood or reply;
  - a lock so two callers make one fetch.
  - No usage line: it is not a model call.
- The weather never reaches a prompt by itself; only the mood reading takes it (AGORA-123).

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
`ensure_weather` gives the day's Lviv weather line once a day and keeps it across a restart; a failure gives None.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One fetch a day: a second call on the same day, or after a restart, reads the file.
- [ ] A failed fetch gives None and no file, and the next try waits 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_weather.py` — the line parsed from a canned Open-Meteo answer (the night's cloud cover
      across midnight, a missing field, no temperature → None); a failed fetch (an exception, a non-200, bad JSON) →
      None; load/save (another day, corrupt, 0600); `ensure_weather` reuses the file and backs off after a failure,
      with the HTTP call mocked and the clock injected.
- [ ] **Contract:** the state file `<name>.weather.json` (`day`, `weather` — world data, no room text) in
      ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and §Data and state files; pinned by `tests/test_weather.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.

### AGORA-123 — The mood for a person: the capability, her natal chart, the reading with the weather, «Настрій дня» in a person's words

**Description:**
The cat's `mood` becomes Кіра's too. Her reading is built on her natal chart, the day's sky, the biorhythms and the
Lviv weather. Its resolution reaches both her prompts as «Настрій дня», a feeling with no word of horoscope and no
number. Touches `agents/mood.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `agents/kira.toml`, the private canon file
`agents/canon/kira.natal.md` (gitignored, never committed), `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the tests.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The capability.** `mood` may be switched on in a persona's TOML. A persona's type default stays without it, so
  Ада, Бруно, Леон, Мія and Ірма are unchanged. `natal` stays required with `mood`.
- **`kira.toml`:**
  - `natal = "agents/canon/kira.natal.md"`;
  - `[capabilities] mood = true`.
- **Her natal chart** (`agents/canon/kira.natal.md`, private canon):
  - Lumi's format, as `kit.natal.md`: `Народження: 02.12.2002, 20:40, Донецьк.`, then the bodies, ASC and MC with
    their houses, then a short reading.
  - Computed for her birth (2002-12-02 20:40 EET, Donetsk) against a real ephemeris, Placidus houses.
  - It must agree with her canon (a Sagittarius with the Moon in Scorpio and Leo rising).
  - Synced by `server/deploy.sh` like the rest of the canon. Never committed, quoted or `git add -f`'d.
- **The reading for a person** (`agents/mood.py`, pure):
  - `mood_request(..., weather=None, person=False)`. The weather line enters the reading's contents as the day's real
    weather, woven into the mood like the biorhythms.
  - With `person=True` the resolution is asked as a person's feeling: no astrology, no planet or sign, no number, no
    advice.
  - The cat's request is byte-identical to today's (no weather, no person rule).
- **The guard** (`agents/mood.py`, pure): `person_mood(resolution) -> str | None`.
  - It drops every sentence that holds horoscope vocabulary (гороскоп, астролог, транзит, натальн, біоритм,
    ретроград, зодіак, ефемерид, тригон, секстил, квадратур), a `BANNED_RE` word or a digit.
  - Nothing left gives None, and then no «Настрій дня» that day.
- **Her prompts** (`agents/agent.py`):
  - `mood_section` for a persona gives «Настрій дня: <guarded resolution>» only. The cat's «Небо сьогодні …» line
    stays the cat's.
  - It is the same block in her Agora prompt and her private prompt (`build_prompt` already places `mood` after
    «Сьогодні»).
- **The day's call** (`ensure_mood`):
  - for a persona, `ensure_weather` (AGORA-122) runs first, next to `ensure_sky`, and the reading takes both;
  - her own `state/kira.sky.json` and `kira.mood.log` — nothing shared with the cat;
  - the private reply awaits `ensure_mood` as the Agora reply does, so a new day's first private answer has its mood;
  - one reading a day, reused from the log after a restart (unchanged).
  - The full reading goes only to `state/kira.mood.log`.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-122

**Expected result:**
On a new day Кіра's log holds one reading built on her chart, the sky, the biorhythms and the Lviv weather. Both her
prompts carry «Настрій дня» as a feeling, and the cat is unchanged.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `mood` is accepted in a persona's TOML; the persona type default does not include it; `natal` is required with
      it.
- [ ] Her prompt (Agora and private) carries «Настрій дня: …» with no horoscope word, no `BANNED_RE` word, no digit,
      and no «Небо сьогодні».
- [ ] The reading's request for her carries the weather line; a missing weather (None) leaves it out and the reading
      still runs.
- [ ] One reading a day, reused after a restart; a failure means no mood and a retry after 10 minutes (unchanged).
- [ ] The cat's request, his prompt («Настрій дня» + «Небо сьогодні») and his files are unchanged.
- [ ] Her natal file exists on the Mac in Lumi's format, agrees with her canon, and is not in git.
- [ ] **Tests:**
      - `tests/test_mood.py`: `person_mood` drops each banned kind of sentence and gives None when nothing is left;
        the person request has the weather and the person rule; the cat's request is unchanged.
      - `tests/test_roster.py`: `mood` on a persona is allowed and needs `natal`; Кіра's TOML has it; the other
        persons do not.
      - An agent test: her `build_prompt` carries the guarded block in both prompts, and the cat's keeps the sky.
      - One reading a day reused from the log after a restart; the private reply ensures the mood.
      - Gemini and the weather fetch are mocked; the clock is injected.
- [ ] **Contract:** the TOML schema (`mood` allowed for a persona, `natal` required with it) and §Prompt assembly
      (the mood for a person: guarded, no sky) in ARCHITECTURE §Contracts and §Prompt assembly; pinned by
      `tests/test_roster.py` and `tests/test_mood.py`.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint, tests.
