### #1 — telegraphed mutations
**Failure scenario:** the day plan says «…поїхати до Дрогобича… але, мабуть, лінь переможе»; in the
conversation the agent effectively announces which plans will fail, so the plan-vs-reality texture
collapses and the hidden-mutation contract is broken in substance even though the tags stay hidden.
**Anchor:** `agents/plans.py` `plan_request`.
**Fix:** state the rule in the request (sincere intention, no hedging, no hint of failure) and strip
hedging clauses from mutated items in code; pin with a test.
