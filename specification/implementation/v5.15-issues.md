# v5.15 — Issues

Issues for phase **v5.15 — The judge: Gemma 4 31B rates the backup's talk** (version **v5 — Voice**), derived from the
Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v5.15 and the contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This
file covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (AGORA-150 → **AGORA-151…155**).

v5.14 closed the backup's talk to Gemini for good: every line it answered waits (`waiting`) with no one to rate it.
v5.15 adds a third model, Gemma 4 31B on OpenRouter, that rates those lines. A clean line is Gemini's again, in the
history, the recall, the facts and the embeddings. A flagged one is censored for good. The window's end is decided by
the rating, and a block has the blocked prompt's older lines rated too (v5.14 review #2).

## v5.15 — The judge: Gemma 4 31B rates the backup's talk

### AGORA-151 — The judge seam: `agents/judge.py`, its policy, `parse_verdicts`, its usage line and settings

**Description:**
A new module, `agents/judge.py`, rates numbered lines through OpenRouter's chat endpoint. It mirrors
`agents/fallback.py`: pure where it decides, the call over `aiohttp` behind a module-level `ClientSession` seam.
Files: `agents/judge.py` (new), `agents/usage.py`, `agents/settings.py`, `agents/agent.py` (the key and the client at
start), `server/kira.env.example`, `tests/conftest.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The request** (`body(model, lines)`): `POST https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions` with:
  - `model` from `CLASSIFIER_MODEL` (`google/gemma-4-31b-it`);
  - `provider: {"sort": "latency", "data_collection": "deny"}`;
  - `reasoning: {"enabled": false}`, `temperature: 0`;
  - a small `max_tokens`, sized by the number of lines.
- **The system message** is the policy (`POLICY`, in Ukrainian):
  - the categories of Google's filter: sexually explicit content, dangerous content, harassment, hate speech;
  - flirt, compliments, kisses, hugs and romantic wishes with no description of a naked body or of sex are `ні` (the
    owner's test, 2026-10-28: without this sentence a kiss was flagged);
  - a tag in square brackets is a voice direction, ignored;
  - the answer format, one line per numbered input line: `N: так · <категорія>` or `N: ні`.
- **The user message** holds the numbered lines only (`1: …`): no canon, no prompt, no speaker names.
- **`parse_verdicts(text, n)`** (pure) gives a verdict per line: `flagged`, `clean` or `None` (unrated).
  - `так` is flagged and `ні` is clean.
  - A missing or malformed line leaves that line unrated.
  - A refusal (v5.13's `refusal_marker`, or OpenRouter's `content_filter` finish) makes every line of the call
    flagged.
- **`Judge.rate(texts)`** returns the verdicts in order.
  - It sends the lines in chunks of at most 25 (`CHUNK`), the chunks in parallel.
  - An HTTP error, an exception, or the `CLASSIFIER_TIMEOUT_S` timeout (30 s) leaves every line of that chunk
    unrated.
  - A failure is logged by type or status only, never the key or a text.
- **Usage:** `judge_usage_line(ts, agent, model, prompt_tokens, output_tokens, ok)` gives the kind `judge`, engine
  `openrouter` and billing `api`. The tokens come from OpenRouter's `usage`, and the price from `PRICE_MODELS` by the
  model's id (`parse_model_prices` already takes a slash in the id). The shape is the same as every other builder's
  (`blocked: None`).
- **Settings** (group «Резерв», `cap:fallback`):
  - `CLASSIFIER_MODEL`: a choice of `google/gemma-4-31b-it` (the default), `openai/gpt-oss-safeguard-20b` or
    `openai/gpt-oss-120b`;
  - `CLASSIFIER_TIMEOUT_S`: 30.
- **The key:** v5.14 popped `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and threw it away; now the agent keeps it for the judge. The judge
  is on for a person with `fallback` and a backup, a key and a model. With no key, v5.14's route stands unchanged.
- **`server/kira.env.example`** names `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, and `tests/test_compose.py` pins it.
- **`tests/conftest.py`:** the network guard also covers `agents.judge.ClientSession`.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
`Judge.rate` rates any number of lines through Gemma 4 31B and returns one verdict per line. A failure never passes
for a verdict, and no text or key reaches a log.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The request carries the model, `sort: latency`, `data_collection: deny`, the reasoning off and temperature 0,
  and the policy with the flirt sentence. The user message holds only the numbered lines.
- [ ] `parse_verdicts` handles `так` and `ні`, a missing line, a malformed line and a refusal.
- [ ] Many lines go in chunks of 25 in parallel. An error or a timeout leaves that chunk unrated.
- [ ] **Tests:** `tests/test_judge.py` covers the request, the parse, the chunks, the failures (unrated, never
  flagged by a failure alone), the usage line and its price, and no text or key in a log. OpenRouter is mocked
  through the `ClientSession` seam.
- [ ] **Contract:** `kira.env` gains `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` (`tests/test_compose.py`), and the usage line gains the
  engine `openrouter` and the kind `judge` (`tests/test_usage.py`). ARCHITECTURE.md is updated in §Token accounting,
  §Configuration, §Security (what reaches OpenRouter, `data_collection: deny`, the key) and §Contracts.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests · compose (`server/kira.env.example` changes)

### AGORA-152 — The verdicts: the mark `clean`, the facts and her history follow; the waiting backlog rated at the watcher's ticks

**Description:**
A verdict sets the line's mark. A clean line reopens to Gemini: in the archive, in her history's `_closed` set, and
in the facts. A flagged one is censored for good. The idle watcher rates what still waits, in bounded chunks.
Files: `agents/archive.py`, `agents/facts.py`, `agents/route.py`, `agents/agent.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The archive:**
  - `MARKS` gains `clean`, rated clean by the judge and open to Gemini. `GEMINI_CLOSED` stays `waiting` and
    `censored`, so `unembedded` and the readers take a clean line as they take a `gemini` one, and the next embed
    tick embeds it.
  - `Archive.unrated(place, limit)` returns the oldest `waiting` lines (with key, speaker, text) for the judge.
- **The facts:**
  - `FACT_MARKS` gains `clean`.
  - `set_mark(folder, fact_id, mark)` writes a fact's mark in place, and the archive's mirror follows at the next
    `facts_now()`.
- **`apply_verdicts(items, verdicts)`** (agent). An item is an archive line (by key) or a fact (by id), with its
  speaker and text.
  - A clean verdict marks the line or the fact `clean` and removes it from `_closed`, in both its full and its cut
    form.
  - A flagged verdict marks it `censored` and closes it (`close_lines`). This also covers a line that was `gemini`
    before.
  - An unrated item keeps its mark.
  - The log gives the counts only: `judge: N rated (M flagged, K unrated)`.
- **The route's counts:** `Route` gains today's `rated` and `flagged`. `after` records them with the outcome `rated`
  and resets them with the day, like `answers`. The file holds no text.
- **The watcher** (`judge_tick`, spawned off the idle watcher behind a lock, so no answer waits for it):
  - it rates up to `JUDGE_TICK_LINES` (100) of the `waiting` private lines, and the `waiting` facts, each tick;
  - a tick with nothing waiting calls nothing;
  - a failed tick backs off for a minute;
  - the lines left waiting by v5.14 are rated this way after the deploy.
- **`/purge`** keeps its marks: it deletes the `waiting` and `censored` lines and facts. A line the judge rated
  clean is Gemini's again and stays. This follows the CLAUDE.md rule, which covers `waiting` and `censored` only.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-151

**Expected result:**
A clean line of the backup's talk reaches Gemini's prompt, recall and embeddings again, a flagged one never does, and
the waiting backlog drains at the watcher's ticks.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A verdict sets the archive's mark. A line rated clean is embedded at the next tick and is in
  `gemini_history()`, the recall and the facts. A censored line is in none of them.
- [ ] A fact waiting for the judge follows its verdict.
- [ ] An unrated line keeps its mark, so a failure alone never censors.
- [ ] The watcher rates the backlog in chunks, bounded per tick, and backs off after a failure.
- [ ] **Tests:** the marks, the facts, `_closed` (including a cut long line), the route's counts and the backlog
  ticks, with Gemini and the judge mocked and the clock injected.
- [ ] **Contract:** the archive's mark `clean`, the fact header's `mark: clean`, and `state/kira.route.json`'s `rated`
  and `flagged` (`tests/test_route.py`, `tests/test_archive.py`, `tests/test_facts.py`). ARCHITECTURE.md is updated in
  §Memory (the verdicts and the marks) and in the `state/` contract table.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests

### AGORA-153 — The judge in her private answer: after a block, in the window, and the window's end by the rating

**Description:**
The route of v5.14 gains the rating, as in ROADMAP §v5.15's table. Files: `agents/agent.py` (`_private_text`,
`_gemini_private_prompt`, `recall_section`), `agents/route.py`, `specification/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **Gemini's state:** nothing else is called until a block.
  - On a block (pinned `gemini` too), the backup answers that message, as in v5.14.
  - Once its answer is sent, the judge rates, in the background, the owner's message, her answer, and the blocked
    prompt's lines it has not rated yet. Those are the `gemini` history lines (their archive keys), the recalled
    lines and the facts in the prompt. `_gemini_private_prompt` keeps what it used, so this needs no second recall.
  - A flagged older line is censored and leaves Gemini's prompt, which closes v5.14 review #2.
- **The window, and a pin on the backup:** the backup answers. After each answer is sent, the judge rates the owner's
  lines and her answer in the background.
- **The window's end:** the judge and the backup start together.
  - The judge rates the new message, and her last private answer if it is still `waiting`.
  - If either is flagged or unrated, the backup's answer goes out and a new window opens (the route's outcome
    `flagged`, which opens a window but is not a Gemini block and does not count toward the day cap).
  - If both are clean, Gemini starts with the prompt without the closed lines. Gemini's answer wins. If Gemini
    blocks, the backup's ready answer goes out and a new window opens.
- **A line she writes first at the window's end:** her last answer's rating alone decides. Her last answer is rated
  now if it is still waiting.
- **The log** names the decision with no text: `route=window_end — judged: clean|flagged|unrated`.
- **Without the judge** (no key), v5.14's route stands.

**Dependencies:** AGORA-152

**Expected result:**
Every exchange of the backup's talk is rated once its answer is sent, and the window ends only when the talk is clean
again.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] In Gemini's state nothing is rated and nothing else is called.
- [ ] Each exchange of the backup's talk is rated after its answer is sent: after a block, in the window, and under a
  pin. The judge never delays an answer.
- [ ] A block has the blocked prompt's unrated lines rated: the history, the recall and the facts.
- [ ] The window's end with a flagged answer, a flagged new message, an unrated one, and both clean. A line she writes
  first is decided by her last answer, and a Gemini block uses the backup's ready answer.
- [ ] **Tests:** the route and the order of the calls with an injected clock: nothing extra in Gemini's state, the
  judge after the answer in the window, and the judge and the backup together at the window's end. Gemini, NovelAI
  and the judge are mocked.
- [ ] **Contract:** the route's outcome `flagged` (`tests/test_route.py`). ARCHITECTURE.md §The message flow covers
  the window's end by the rating.
- [ ] **Gates:** lint · tests
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** after a window, the judge's ratings are in the log (counts, no text). Lines rated clean
  reach Gemini's prompt again, and the log shows how many closed lines Gemini left out.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** a window whose last answer was flagged opens the next window, and a clean one gives the
  chat back to Gemini.
