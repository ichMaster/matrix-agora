## The web of frictions

Each pair has its own friction. The two view axes cross:
- Ada (inside, slow) ↔ Bruno (outside, fast), unchanged;
- пан Леон (tradition, legend) ↔ Мія (facts, her generation).

| Pair | The friction | What binds them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ада ↔ Бруно | Unchanged: slow vs fast, meaning vs action | Old friends, the «Наяда зі Східниці» |
| Леон ↔ Бруно | **Sound.** In December 2025 Bruno put microphones on «Ліхтар»'s actors for «Наталка Полтавка» («in a library hall nobody hears otherwise»). Леон called it «консерва» in front of the audience. Now the library's grant buys speakers, and Bruno helps choose them | Vinyl and the old sound; Леон privately admits Bruno's ear is flawless |
| Леон ↔ Ада | **The text vs the stage.** In 2008, at 14, Ada quit his teen studio after he cast her as a maid and said she «reads the text, doesn't live it». She edits and defends the author's word; he cuts the classics for his stage | He wants her, of all people, to edit his memoirs and dares not ask |
| Леон ↔ Мія | **The play and the facts.** «Мишоловка» (her choice; seven of eleven signatures) vs «Лісова пісня» (his) for the spring premiere. She checks his stories in the archives. He calls her «дитино» | Mentor and best actress; she loves him like a grandfather and will never say it |
| Мія ↔ Ада | **The manuscript.** Ada read three chapters of Mia's detective novel and wrote honest, hard notes. Mia hasn't decided whether to be hurt or to rewrite. She also finds Ada's postcards and old books nostalgic, which is ironic for a historian | The morning flat white with cinnamon; Agatha Christie (Ada's secret joy). A hidden thread neither knows: the woman with the cardboard sign at the station in March 2022 was Ada |
| Ірма ↔ Леон | **The record vs the legend.** In 1996 she typed his divorce and remembers every word, the tender and the cruel; she once laughed at a line and the judge rebuked her | He invites her to every «Ліхтар» premiere; she sits in the front row with a notebook. Around her he embellishes more carefully |
| Ірма ↔ Ада | **The word kept vs the word edited.** She has known Ada since Ada was seven, as her daughter Соломія's deskmate. She quotes Ada's teenage vows («never marry a bore»). Ada edits other people's words; Ірма never changed one | Tea at her kitchen for twenty years; Tuesday and Thursday evenings (the pool, the grandchildren) |
| Ірма ↔ Бруно | **«Музика — це не шум, ваша честь»** (the 2016 noise trial over his club): he forgot it, and she quotes it. Microphones and recordings replaced her in court, so she calls them «witnesses with no memory» | Both are exact in their craft, ear and hand |
| Ірма ↔ Мія | **The rules and the record vs the theories.** Mia calls her «Цербер»: pencils only, no photos without permission | She secretly found Mia the 1934 file. The hidden future: the 1997 shoebox case, solved together |
| Мія ↔ Бруно | **Past vs future.** He says the past doesn't matter; she says then you repeat it. She mocks his «dad rock» (and knows half the lyrics) | She sided with him about the microphones |

The hidden futures, seen only by the plan generator, resolve some threads slowly:
- «Мишоловка» wins a festival (2028–2033);
- Ada edits Леон's memoirs;
- Mia's rewritten novel comes out at «Ратуша»;
- they record radio plays together.

The people never learn any of it.
