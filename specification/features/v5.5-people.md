## The web of frictions

Each pair has its own friction. The two view axes cross:
- Ada (inside, slow) ↔ Bruno (outside, fast), unchanged;
- пан Орест (tradition, legend) ↔ Соломія (facts, her generation).

| Pair | The friction | What binds them |
|---|---|---|
| Ада ↔ Бруно | Unchanged: slow vs fast, meaning vs action | Old friends, the «Наяда зі Східниці» |
| Орест ↔ Бруно | **Sound.** In December 2025 Bruno put microphones on «Ліхтар»'s actors for «Наталка Полтавка» («in a library hall nobody hears otherwise»). Орест called it «консерва» in front of the audience. Now the library's grant buys speakers, and Bruno helps choose them | Vinyl and the old sound; Орест privately admits Bruno's ear is flawless |
| Орест ↔ Ада | **The text vs the stage.** In 2008, at 14, Ada quit his teen studio after he cast her as a maid and said she «reads the text, doesn't live it». She edits and defends the author's word; he cuts the classics for his stage | He wants her, of all people, to edit his memoirs and dares not ask |
| Орест ↔ Соломія | **The play and the facts.** «Мишоловка» (her choice; seven of eleven signatures) vs «Лісова пісня» (his) for the spring premiere. She checks his stories in the archives. He calls her «дитино» | Mentor and best actress; she loves him like a grandfather and will never say it |
| Соломія ↔ Ада | **The manuscript.** Ada read three chapters of Solomiya's detective novel and wrote honest, hard notes. Solomiya hasn't decided whether to be hurt or to rewrite. She also finds Ada's postcards and old books nostalgic, which is ironic for a historian | The morning flat white with cinnamon; Agatha Christie (Ada's secret joy). A hidden thread neither knows: the woman with the cardboard sign at the station in March 2022 was Ada |
| Соломія ↔ Бруно | **Past vs future.** He says the past doesn't matter; she says then you repeat it. She mocks his «dad rock» (and knows half the lyrics) | She sided with him about the microphones |

The hidden futures, seen only by the plan generator, resolve some threads slowly:
- «Мишоловка» wins a festival (2028–2033);
- Ada edits Орест's memoirs;
- Solomiya's rewritten novel comes out at «Ратуша»;
- they record radio plays together.

The people never learn any of it.
